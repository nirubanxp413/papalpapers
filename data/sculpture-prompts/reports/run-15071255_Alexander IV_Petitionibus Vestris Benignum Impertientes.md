# Sculptural form: The Seal of Scholastic Authority

## Concept

The sculpture represents the transition from ephemeral speech to institutional permanence. It takes the form of a heavy, monolithic bronze signet ring, partially embedded into a jagged, unhewn block of white Carrara marble. The bronze is oxidized with a rich, dark patina, featuring a complex, geometric relief pattern that suggests the authority of a university seal. The contrast between the raw, organic marble and the rigid, manufactured metal symbolizes the papal sanctioning of the university’s identity. The work embodies the "common seal" as a physical anchor for academic legitimacy, suggesting that the university’s intellect is now solidified and protected by apostolic decree.
