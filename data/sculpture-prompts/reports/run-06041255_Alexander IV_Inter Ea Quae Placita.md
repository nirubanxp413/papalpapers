# Sculptural form: The Prism of Salamanca

## Concept

The sculpture represents the "inextinguishable light of wisdom" as a singular, monolithic prism carved from translucent, honey-colored alabaster. The form is geometric yet organic, with a jagged, crystalline top that transitions into a smooth, architectural base. Embodying the phrase "multitudo sapientium sanitas est regnorum," the internal structure of the prism contains thousands of minute, fiber-optic-like threads of polished bronze that radiate outward from the center, suggesting that the strength of a kingdom is derived from the convergence of many wise minds. The sculpture stands as a beacon of intellectual fortification, suggesting that wisdom is the structural foundation upon which the stability of a realm rests.
