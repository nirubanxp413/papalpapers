# Render: Quanto in dolore

## Why

The core conflict in *Quanto in dolore* is the silencing and exile of the Society of Jesus, the Church's intellectual and theological defenders, by secular authority. We chose the archetype of the "Imprisoned Scholar/Defender" rather than an abstract symbol (like a broken cross or an empty chair) because the encyclical focuses explicitly on human agents—ministers and teachers—who are persecuted and silenced. The figure of an aged, chain-bound bishop kneeling in prayer, identifiable by his *cappa magna*, immediately captures the human cost of the suppression of the defenders of the faith and the loss of religious authority to lay power, a central grievance of Clement XIII.
