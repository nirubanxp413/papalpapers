# Render: Lux Veritatis

## Why

I chose a single, perfectly interlocking golden monstrance to represent *Lux Veritatis*. The core of the encyclical is the defense of the hypostatic union—the indivisible union of Christ’s divine and human natures—which is the "light of truth" radiating from Ephesus. The monstrance is the preeminent sacred vessel for displaying the *Body* of Christ, visually grounding the abstract theology of the Incarnation in a concrete, recognizable devotional item. It rejects abstraction by presenting a familiar object that is unified and centered, and its radiant form embodies the title's meaning.
