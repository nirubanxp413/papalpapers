# Render: Optatissima Pax

## Why

I chose a highly realistic **lit liturgical candle** on a plinth as the single recognizable subject. *Optatissima Pax* calls for prayer, charity, and a return to Christ in the dark aftermath of war. A candle is instantly legible as an icon of prayer, spiritual light, and the enduring presence of Christ, directly embodying the encyclical’s spiritual remedy without resorting to abstract geometry or complex scenarios. This single object captures the quiet power of interior prayer and the hope for lasting peace.
