# Render: Gravissimo animi

## Why

*Gravissimo animi* addresses the unauthorized entry of clergy and officials into the cloistered convents of nuns, bypassing the bishop's authority. To symbolize the sacred boundary of the clausura that must be respected and the regulatory control (the required licence) that governs access, the sculpture is a monumental, ornate, locked door. This concrete, recognizable subject powerfully represents both the protected enclosure of the religious life and the authority (the bishop's key) that regulates entrance, making abstract concepts of enclosure and jurisdiction tangible and visually immediate.
