# Render: Christi Ecclesiae

## Why

I chose a storm-battered but functional ship's rudder, cast in heavy, seafaring bronze, because Pius VI explicitly describes his pontificate as struggling against "extraordinary and violent waves" and "storms" threatening the Church. The encyclical *Christi Ecclesiae* isn't a doctrinal definition but a pragmatic, procedural change—adjusting the rules for a conclave to ensure survival and stability amidst chaos. A rudder is the quintessential instrument of navigation and control in a tempest, perfectly symbolizing the Pope’s practical modifications to steer the Church through danger towards safe harbor. It avoids abstraction by being a concrete, functional object that embodies the idea of guidance and purposeful direction under extreme duress.
