# Render: Ubi Primum

## Why

For *Ubi Primum*, a papal call for bishops to fulfill their vocational duties, the most recognizable and relevant singular object is the bishop’s crosier, which symbolizes spiritual authority and pastoral care. While a shepherd figure would also be appropriate, a beautifully crafted crosier stands on its own as a potent religious object, representing the duty to guide the flock. It is instantly identifiable in a single view and clearly embodies the encyclical's central theme of vigorous episcopal leadership. This choice rejects abstraction in favor of a concrete, historically rich object that directly addresses the document's message.
