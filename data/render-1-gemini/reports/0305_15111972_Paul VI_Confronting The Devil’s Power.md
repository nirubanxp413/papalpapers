# Render: Confronting The Devil’s Power

## Why

A shield was chosen as the singular, recognizable subject because it directly embodies Pope Paul VI’s central call for defense against the Devil as an active, personal, and dangerous force. The sculpture’s iconography integrates Christ’s specific identity as the "Prince of Peace," while its heavy, battle-worn construction emphasizes the document’s insistence that this spiritual struggle is real, demanding constant defense, rather than being an abstract or psychological metaphor. An abstract form was rejected because the encyclical demands a practical, legible, and personal response to a personal adversary, which this protective sacred object represents clearly and powerfully.
