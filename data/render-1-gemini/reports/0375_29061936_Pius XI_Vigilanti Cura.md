# Render: Vigilanti Cura

## Why

A lighthouse is the ideal singular object to embody *Vigilanti Cura*, representing both proactive moral vigilance and guidance. Its elevated, powerful light symbolizes the "holy crusade" of reviewing and purifying cinema, acting as a beacon of decency that warns of moral danger and guides the faithful toward positive, wholesome expression within the vast "multitude" of media influence. A lighthouse is an instantly recognizable structure that requires no abstract interpretation to convey its protective function. I rejected abstract concepts like "light" or symbolic film strips in favor of this coherent, utilitarian, and widely understood architectural form, ensuring a single, powerful subject on a transparent background.
