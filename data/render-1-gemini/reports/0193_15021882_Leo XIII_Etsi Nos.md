# Render: Etsi Nos

## Why

I chose a lion’s head sculpture, cast in dark, patinated bronze and positioned as if roaring, to embody Leo XIII's *Etsi Nos*. This single, powerful figure represents both the Pope himself (Leo) and the 'Lion of Judah,' capturing the courageous, urgent spirit the encyclical demands from Italy's clergy and faithful when facing the "pernicious sect." While the text includes specific imagery of chains or siege, a dynamic lion—specifically a non-abstract, classical zoomorphic figure—is the most recognizable archetypal subject that translates the encyclical’s entire call for bold spiritual action and resistance into a cohesive, non-composite form.
