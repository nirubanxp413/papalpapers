# Render: Immortale Dei

## Why

I chose a single, magnificent, highly ornamental golden crown supporting a cross to symbolize Leo XIII’s argument for divine authority. The crown, representing legitimate civil rule and the structure of human government (a concept the Pope permits), is fundamentally subordinate to the cross, which signifies the ultimate authority of God and the Church's jurisdiction over salvation and public religion. This tangible subject directly translates the encyclical’s core tenet—that all power derives from God and that Christian ordering must prioritize the sacred—into one immediately recognizable form without resorting to abstract geometry.
