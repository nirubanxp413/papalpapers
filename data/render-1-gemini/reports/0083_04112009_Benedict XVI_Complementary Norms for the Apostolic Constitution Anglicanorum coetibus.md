# Render: Complementary Norms for the Apostolic Constitution Anglicanorum coetibus

## Why

The Complementary Norms provide the stable, governing structure—the "canonical home"—for former Anglicans entering Catholic unity. The sculpture must embody this organized, stable community without being a complex diorama. I chose a large, ornate, architectural tabernacle as the single subject because it represents the secure "dwelling place" (tabernaculum) of Christ, the center of both the Roman Rite and the spiritual heritage of Anglicanism (expressed through Eucharistic devotion). Its strong geometry signifies the *norms* of structure, governance, and defined boundaries, while its sacred purpose speaks to spiritual unity. Abstraction was rejected because it cannot communicate the specific, defined institutionality and welcoming, sacred enclosure defined by these detailed rules.
