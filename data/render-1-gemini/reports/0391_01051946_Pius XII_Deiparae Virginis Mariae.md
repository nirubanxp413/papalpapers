# Render: Deiparae Virginis Mariae

## Why

I chose a monstrance shaped like an ascending figure as the single recognizable subject for *Deiparae Virginis Mariae*. This liturgical object is instantly identifiable in a Catholic context, serving as a vessel for a sacred truth, paralleling how the encyclical treats the dogma of the Assumption. I selected this specific form because it embodies the central theological point—the physical, bodily Assumption of Mary—by materializing her silhouette in a gesture of upward movement, without resorting to vague abstraction. It rejected other options like a crown or a lily, which represent status or purity, but lack the direct action of "assumption" and the sense of a formal, contained doctrine.
