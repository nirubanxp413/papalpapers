# Render: Affari Vos

## Why

I chose a shepherd protecting a lamb with a cloak over abstract concepts like "educational rights" because Leo XIII uses this exact archetype—pastoral care and protection—to frame the Church's responsibility in the Manitoba school crisis. A shepherd is a universally recognized figure representing vigilant guidance, while the protective cloak physically shields the vulnerable lamb from an external threat, embodying the specific defense of Catholic schools against unjust laws. This concrete, single subject is immediately legible as a sacred protector, capturing the encyclical's call for unified, defensive action to preserve the faith of children, and was selected to avoid the vagueness of symbols like keys (too political) or books (too abstract).
