# Render: Inter Praecipuas

## Why

For *Inter Praecipuas*, which warns against non-ecclesiastical Bible distribution and independent interpretation, I chose a large, leather-bound Bible that is heavily reinforced by ornate iron straps and a prominent, functional padlock. This form was selected because it instantly communicates the Pope's core concern: restricting and controlling access to Scripture. The padlock over the book's closure directly represents the Church's effort to "lock down" unauthorized vernacular translations and prevent interpretation outside of clerical authority. I rejected abstraction (like a symbolic 'barrier') because a bound book is universally recognizable; the padlock specifically concretizes the act of restriction, making the theological control immediately legible. It embodies the defensive posture against the "Christian League" and other Bible societies by visibly safeguarding the text within Church structures.
