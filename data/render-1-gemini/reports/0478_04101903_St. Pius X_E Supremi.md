# Render: E Supremi

## Why

The core argument of *E Supremi* is humanity’s prideful apostasy from God, leading to spiritual ignorance and social chaos. St. Pius X responds with the definitive program: "Instaurare omnia in Christo" (Restore all things in Christ). To symbolize this restoration and focus, a single, dominant figure of Christ is required, but depicted not merely in agony, but in authoritative action. I have chosen the figure of Christ the Good Shepherd, emphasizing the Church's role in gathering, healing, and ordering society under the supremacy of God, directly countering man's self-enthronement. The image focuses on the physical embodiment of authority, healing, and guidance found in this sacred figure, rejecting the vagueness of abstract geometric symbols.
