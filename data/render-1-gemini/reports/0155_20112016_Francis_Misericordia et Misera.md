# Render: Misericordia et Misera

## Why

I chose an oversized, deeply etched, dark bronze Holy Door, not as a boundary to be crossed, but as the enduring site where the encounter with mercy is now eternally etched. The letter emphasizes that while the Extraordinary Jubilee has concluded, the grace of mercy must become a permanent, "institutionalized" reality in the Church’s life. The door remains a potent, recognizable symbol of passage and welcome, now visually transformed by the "etching" of thousands of small, recognizable depictions of acts of mercy (feeding the hungry, comforting the sorrowful), illustrating how the *Misericordia* (mercy) has been integrated into the *Misera* (the wretchedness of the world). An abstract form would fail to capture the tangible, repeatable nature of the corporal and spiritual works of mercy or the institutional weight required.
