# Render: Iucunda Sane

## Why

I chose a papal staff, or crozier, as the single recognizable subject for *Iucunda Sane*. It directly represents Pope St. Gregory I, the central historical figure Pius X uses to bridge the early Church and the challenges of the 20th century. The crozier symbolizes the episcopal authority and active pastoral care needed to preach the full Gospel and restore Christian civilization. It embodies the encyclical's call to mimic Gregory's proactive, holy, and unwavering defense against "false science" and error. This concrete object allows for the integration of traditional iconography—the papal ferula and a protective element against the "barbarians" (modernism)—into one cohesive, instantly identifiable religious symbol, rather than resorting to abstract geometry.
