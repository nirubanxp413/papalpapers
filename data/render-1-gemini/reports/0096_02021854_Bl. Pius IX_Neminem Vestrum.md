# Render: Neminem Vestrum

## Why

A single, ornate shepherd's crook, the crozier, is chosen as the sculpture for *Neminem Vestrum*. This object instantly communicates pastoral authority, unity, and governance, which are the central themes of Pius IX's encyclical to the Armenian Catholic nation. The complex interlocking Armenian knotwork patterns and dual-serpent imagery (unity and wisdom) woven into the gilded silver crook materialize the integration and reconciliation Pius IX seeks for the Armenian church, moving beyond abstract concepts of unity to a concrete symbol of guidance. A fragmented or abstract sculpture would fail to convey the required themes of authority, historical continuity, and legible leadership needed to address the ecclesiastical crisis. The crozier stands alone as a potent emblem of the unified guidance necessary to overcome division and silence polemical writings.
