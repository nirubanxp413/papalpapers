# Render: Rerum condicio

## Why

The Apostolic Constitution *Rerum condicio* update the Rule of the Third Order Regular of St. Francis, emphasizing the *form* of religious life, specifically the three vows of poverty, chastity, and obedience. I chose a large, knotted Franciscan cord as the single recognizable subject because it is the preeminent, immediate devotional symbol of the Franciscan order, worn as a belt by Tertiaries and Regulars alike. The three specific knots traditionally represent the three vows that structure this rule, concretely embodying the document's legal and spiritual content far more clearly than a abstract concept like "charity" or a generic symbol of Francis (e.g., a bird). It is a simple, ubiquitous, everyday object from the Franciscan context that translates complex theological and canonical requirements into a unified, recognizable form without requiring environmental explanation or abstract geometry.
