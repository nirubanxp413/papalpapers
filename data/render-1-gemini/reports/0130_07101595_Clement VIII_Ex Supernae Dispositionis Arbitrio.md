# Render: Ex Supernae Dispositionis Arbitrio

## Why

The bull *Ex Supernae Dispositionis Arbitrio* formally ratifies the University of Mexico and, crucially, establishes its absolute equality ("pariformiter et aeque principaliter") with the ancient and prestigious universities of Salamanca, Alcalá, and Lima. This central act of elevation and parity is best captured not through abstract symbols of knowledge or geometry, but by the physical object used in academic rituals to represent authority and incorporation: a ceremonial mace. The sculptural form is a single, ornate mace, standing vertically as an isolated sacred artifact, symbolizing that the new institution now holds the same weight, honor, and formal recognition as its European counterparts, having been granted the authority to confer degrees equal to them. Abstraction was rejected because the document is a concrete legal and institutional act, best symbolized by a recognizable tool of academic power.
