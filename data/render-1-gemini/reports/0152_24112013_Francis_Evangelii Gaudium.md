# Render: Evangelii Gaudium

## Why

*Evangelii Gaudium* calls for a Church that goes forth to the peripheries, marked by joy and inclusion of the poor, moving away from being a self-referential center. I chose a large, worn terracotta oil lamp, as it is a single, recognizable subject that powerfully embodies the metaphor of carrying the light of the Gospel (the oil and flame) and actively 'going out' into the dark (implied by the wear). Abstract geometry cannot convey the humble, active, and essential nature of this mission like an everyday vessel of light. This choice resists abstract symbolism by centering on a universally understood tool of mission and illumination, rendered as a monumental, sacred object.
