# Render: Sacramentum Caritatis

## Why

The chosen subject is a single, large, intricately decorated Eucharistic Monstrance. A Monstrance was selected because *Sacramentum Caritatis* explicitly defines the Eucharist as the "integrating center of Christian faith and practice," spanning belief, celebration, and lived action. This sacred vessel, specifically designed to display the Blessed Sacrament for adoration, provides the most powerful, concrete, and immediately recognizable theological fit. Abstraction was rejected because it cannot communicate the specific, objective reality of the Eucharist that Pope Benedict XVI emphasizes, nor can it capture the tangible devotion and "worthiness" called for in the *ars celebrandi*.
