# Render: Gravissimo Officii Munere

## Why

Pius X in *Gravissimo Officii Munere* is directly responding to the French law that created *associations cultuelles*, which would have seized control of Church property, ignoring the rightful authority of the Pope and Bishops. This isn't about general spirituality but about institutional structure and authority. We chose **A Sovereign Mitre Locked in a Heavy Iron Cage** because it perfectly visualizes this specific conflict: the sacred, episcopal power (the mitre, authority) is still present and valid, but it is physically bound and imprisoned by an external, secular power (the rigid, heavy cage, representing the oppressive *associations cultuelles* law). We rejected abstract concepts or generalized symbols (like chains or keys) because this sculpture must clearly and concretely represent the specific, institutional, and canonical authority that Pius X is defending.
