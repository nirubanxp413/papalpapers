# Render: Magno cum animi

## Why

A miniature, portable altar best translates this document. The encyclical exhaustively defines when and where portable altars are permitted for private Masses, responding to questions from Polish bishops about exempt clergy exploiting their usage to circumvent normal episcopal authority. This specific object *is* the point of the text; it represents the limited concession of private liturgical space while physically containing and standardizing the holy sacrifice of the Mass. A recognizable portable altar—the very item whose usage the Pope is regulating—immediately identifies the text's subject matter far more clearly than any abstract concept or broader religious symbol.
