# Render: Pergrata

## Why

To translate Pope Leo XIII’s *Pergrata*, which addresses the state of the Church in Portugal following a diplomatic agreement, the single recognizable subject is a **Mitre**, specifically one embellished with a stylized, symbolic "Portuguese Cross of Christ." The Mitre, the definitive symbol of an individual bishop and the entire episcopate, directly represents Leo’s central instructions regarding episcopal unity, the restoration of learned clergy in seminaries, and Catholic engagement. It functions as a singular, legible sacred object embodying authority and renewal, rejecting abstraction to ensure the viewer immediately identifies the subject as a concrete emblem of ecclesiastical reform and national Catholic identity.
