# Render: Graves Ac Diuturnae

## Why

The core conflict in *Graves Ac Diuturnae* is the division between the authentic Church, led by the legitimate Pope, and the "Old Catholics," a schismatic group whose false hierarchy, though supported by civil authority, is spiritually empty. The Papal Tiara (Triregnum) and the crossed Keys of St. Peter are the definitive, instantly recognizable symbols of the universal Petrine office, unity, and apostolic authority. By selecting this single, iconic subject, the sculpture immediately embodies the true, unified authority that Pius IX reconfirms against the schismatics, rejecting abstract geometry in favor of concrete, legible sacred symbolism.
