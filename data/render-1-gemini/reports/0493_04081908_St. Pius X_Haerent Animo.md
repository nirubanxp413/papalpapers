# Render: Haerent Animo

## Why

I chose a single, burning sanctuary lamp as the sculptural subject. *Haerent Animo* demands personal priestly holiness as the precondition for all effective ministry; the lamp embodies this perfectly. It is a familiar liturgical object that is immediately legible and non-abstract. The lamp, when lit, represents the interior life of the priest, which must burn constantly and purely with spiritual fervor (the light) rather than focusing solely on external actions (the active virtues alone). To emphasize the "sacred golden jubilee" context and the luxury finish required, the sculpture is rendered in hammered gold with high-contrast, dramatic lighting that highlights the physical flame as the source of illumination.
