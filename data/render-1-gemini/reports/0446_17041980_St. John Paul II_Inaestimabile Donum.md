# Render: Inaestimabile Donum

## Why

For *Inaestimabile Donum*, a papal document addressing abuses and reinforcing the established norms for the celebration of the Eucharist, I have chosen a **Chalices** as the single recognizable subject. This sacred liturgical vessel is the central object of the Eucharistic sacrifice and the precise locus of the liturgical discipline the document seeks to restore. A chalice, isolated and rendered in its ideal form, embodies the "priceless gift" and the call for reverence, liturgical order, and the correct handling of sacred mysteries. Abstraction would fail to capture the specific, tangible liturgical focus of the document, which deals with concrete actions, materials, and roles centered on the Eucharistic elements.
