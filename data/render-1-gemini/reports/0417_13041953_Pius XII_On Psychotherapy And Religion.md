# Render: On Psychotherapy And Religion

## Why

I chose a single, traditional chalice, rendered in a highly complex and articulated manner, to represent Pope Pius XII's address, "On Psychotherapy And Religion." The address emphasizes that while psychology has value, it cannot provide the ultimate healing and moral resolution found in the sacraments, specifically the Eucharist. A highly structured and complex chalice embodies the hierarchy and defined nature of both Church doctrine and the human person—described by Pius XII as a structured unit—contrasted against potentially fragmented or purely humanistic approaches. The chalice is an instantly recognizable sacred vessel of Catholic life, directly pointing to the theological truth and sacramental grace the encyclical insists upon as superior to psychological treatment for fundamental human suffering and guilt. Its single, unified form rejects abstraction while maintaining profound theological weight.
