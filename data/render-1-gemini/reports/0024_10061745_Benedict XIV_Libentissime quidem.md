# Render: Libentissime quidem

## Why

*Libentissime quidem* focuses on Pope Benedict XIV's efforts to correct the widespread and excessive loosening of Lenten fasting rules, particularly the ease with which local bishops granted communal meat-eating dispensations. The core theological and moral tension is between strict self-denial and laxity, symbolized by the mixing of Lenten fish (fasting) with forbidden meat. I selected a common **balance scale** as the single recognizable object to embody this discipline; it is an ancient, universally understood symbol of justice, judgment, order, and precise measurement, perfectly visualizing the pope's desire for balanced spiritual rigor and lawful discipline. Unlike abstract forms or vague concepts of self-denial, the scale is an everyday object that instantly conveys the idea of weighing spiritual obligations against worldly indulgences, making the papal document's message concrete and immediately legible.
