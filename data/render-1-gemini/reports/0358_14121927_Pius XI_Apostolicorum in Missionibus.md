# Render: Apostolicorum in Missionibus

## Why

A single, deeply detailed shower of descending roses, rendered as a cohesive sculptural unit, best translates this document because it visualizes the miraculous grace Pope Pius XI specifically cites in the text—St. Thérèse's promised "shower of roses from heaven"—which the faithful believe she sends even to "distant pagan lands" on behalf of the missions. This concrete, recognizable symbol embodies her unique patronal role, which is the entire subject of the Apostolic Letter. Abstraction was rejected as it would fail to communicate the specificity of the Pope's reasoning, which rests entirely on this particular, well-known devotional manifestation as evidence of her intercessory power and suitability as patroness.
