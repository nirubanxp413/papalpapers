# Render: Reputantiubus

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Reputantiubus* because it is the supreme, universally recognized Catholic symbol of unity and spiritual kinship centered on Christ, directly counteracting the national and linguistic fracture Pope Leo XIII condemned. It perfectly visualizes the single "spiritual kinship that baptism creates" which must transcend all earthly rivalries between the faithful. Abstraction was rejected because a vague symbol of "unity" (like a circle or interwoven pattern) would fail to convey the specifically Catholic mandate to rally around the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist, which is the only force strong enough to collapse national divisions into a single sacred body.
