# Render: Ubi Arcano Dei Consilio

## Why

I chose a highly detailed and stylized sacred lantern to represent *Ubi Arcano Dei Consilio*. The encyclical addresses a world in the immediate aftermath of the Great War, which was physically and spiritually fractured by secularization and a rejection of divine authority, leaving humanity in the "darkness" of materialistic conflict. The lantern perfectly embodies the central argument: that "The Peace of Christ" (which the Pope calls the only remedy) is a spiritual light necessary to illuminate and transform the darkness of modern hearts, families, and states, and that this light resides uniquely within the Church. I rejected abstract concepts like fragmented shapes (chaos) or stylized doves (peace) in favor of this single, concrete, and deeply evocative religious and domestic tool, which communicates the active guidance and hope the document proclaims, especially isolated on a pure background.
