# Render: Novo Millennio Ineunte

## Why

I chose a monstrance as the single sculptural subject to translate *Novo Millennio Ineunte*. This encyclical’s defining message is that all post-Jubilee pastoral planning must flow directly from the contemplative gaze upon Christ’s face, a reality made present in the Eucharist. A monstrance is the sacred liturgical object specifically designed for this purpose: the adoration and visual contemplation of the Blessed Sacrament. It rejects abstraction by presenting a concrete, instantly recognizable object of Catholic devotion that perfectly embodies the document's central tension: the "contemplation of Christ's face" (the sacred center) which inspires and guides all "action" and "pastoral planning" (the radiant, outward-pointing elements of the design).
