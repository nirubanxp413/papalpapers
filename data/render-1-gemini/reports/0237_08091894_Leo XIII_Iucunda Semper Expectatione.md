# Render: Iucunda Semper Expectatione

## Why

I chose a highly ornate Rosary as the single sculptural subject because it is the central practical focus of *Iucunda Semper Expectatione*, which renews the annual appeal for Rosary devotion. The sculpture materializes the document's dual theological focus on vocal prayer (the beads) and meditative contemplation of the sacred mysteries in which Mary actively participated. By rendering the rosary itself in premium materials, I reject abstract forms and instead elevate this simple, recognizable devotional tool into a monumental sacred object that is immediately legible to any observer without explanation.
