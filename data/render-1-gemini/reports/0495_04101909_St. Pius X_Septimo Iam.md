# Render: Septimo Iam

## Why

To embody Pope Pius X's definition in *Septimo Iam*—that the three distinct branches (Friars Minor, Conventuals, Capuchins) are legally equal, authentically Franciscan, and united under the single Rule of St. Francis—I selected the Franciscans’ signature attribute: a single coarse cord with three knots. This everyday object, integral to the habit, immediately identifies the Franciscan identity requested by the text. The three knots, structurally united on one rope, perfectly visualize the encyclical's central decree of three autonomous families bound by one spiritual origin and one Rule, avoiding the need for an abstract geometry or a complex multi-object assemblage.
