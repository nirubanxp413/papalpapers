# Render: Dilectissima Nobis

## Why

I chose a highly detailed *crosier*, or pastoral staff, to represent *Dilectissima Nobis*. As the primary symbol of a bishop’s (and the Pope's) authority and spiritual care, the crosier directly embodies the Church’s established structure that Pope Pius XI was vehemently defending against state suppression and property seizure. Choosing a concrete liturgical object like the crosier provides instant recognition, avoiding the ambiguity of abstract symbols, and captures the core theme of the spiritual authority of the Church under siege in Spain while calling the faithful to unified action.
