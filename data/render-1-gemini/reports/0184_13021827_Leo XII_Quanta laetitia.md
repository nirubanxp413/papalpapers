# Render: Quanta laetitia

## Why

The most recognizable single symbol for the growth and structural reorganization of the Catholic faith in a nation is the Crozier, the bishop's pastoral staff, specifically featuring three distinct cross motifs in its structure rather than the usual single cross. I chose the Triple-Cross Crozier because it translates the complex restructuring of Scotland into Eastern, Western, and Northern districts—and the appointment of three new bishops—into one immediately legible, unified religious object. I rejected abstraction because a conceptual sculpture would fail to communicate the specificity of Leo XII's administrative action and the concrete reality of new episcopal oversight, whereas the Crozier embodies the pastoral care, authority, and missionary growth central to *Quanta laetitia*.
