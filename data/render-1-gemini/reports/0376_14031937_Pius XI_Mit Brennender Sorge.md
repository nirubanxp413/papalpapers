# Render: Mit Brennender Sorge

## Why

I chose a single, steadfast figure of a Catholic bishop because it embodies the institutional and theological "steadfast confessional loyalty" that *Mit Brennender Sorge* demands in the face of Nazi persecution. This solitary figure, dressed for spiritual warfare in a traditional mitre and chasuble, physically represents the enduring resistance of the German Church to "ideological counterfeits," rejecting an abstract concept for a recognizable symbol of clerical hierarchy and commitment. He holds a large, unyielding cross, grounding the message in Christian doctrine rather than vague concepts like blood and race, fulfilling the single-subject discipline with an immediately legible and monumental form.
