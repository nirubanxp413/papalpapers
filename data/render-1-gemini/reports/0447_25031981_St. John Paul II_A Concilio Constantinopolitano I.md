# Render: A Concilio Constantinopolitano I

## Why

I chose a monstrance shaped like an ornate dove for *A Concilio Constantinopolitano I* because this Apostolic Letter calls for a special Pentecost gathering focused intensely on the distinct work of the Holy Spirit and the Incarnation. A traditional monstrance is the established liturgical vessel for the physical manifestation of the Incarnate Christ (the Host), but shaping the monstrance *itself* as a majestic dove visually merges the letter's two central pillars: the Holy Spirit and the Eucharist ("Et incarnatus est de Spiritu Sancto ex Maria Virgine"). It rejects abstraction by utilizing existing, recognizable Catholic devotional forms—the monstrance and the symbol of the Holy Spirit—combining them into a single, cohesive, highly legible object that embodies the letter's spiritual energy and Trinitarian orthodoxy.
