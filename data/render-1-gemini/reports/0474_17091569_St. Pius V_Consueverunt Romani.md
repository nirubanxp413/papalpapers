# Render: Consueverunt Romani

## Why

I chose a pair of clasped hands holding an intertwined rosary to embody *Consueverunt Romani*. This image perfectly encapsulates Pius V's core message: the universal call to prayer (the hands accessible to all) and the definitive solution, the Dominican Rosary, held up as a spiritual weapon and shield. While a simple rosary might suffice, the *act* of prayer through the clasped hands makes the subject dynamic and immediately relatable to the text's goal; it avoids abstraction by rooting the theology in a concrete, recognizable human and devotional posture.
