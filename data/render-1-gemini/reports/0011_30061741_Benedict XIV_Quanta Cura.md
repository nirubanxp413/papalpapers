# Render: Quanta Cura

## Why

A single, fractured set of weighing scales, its beams strained and balances unbalanced, most clearly represents *Quanta Cura's* core argument regarding justice and "base profit." Scales are an instantly recognizable universal symbol of transactional fairness and just measure, perfectly capturing the theme of monetary exchange for spiritual services. The sculpture is concrete rather than abstract because it depicts a known physical tool in a state of catastrophic failure, symbolizing the inherent injustice when Mass stipends are manipulated for greed, violating the "expressed or silent desire" of the donor. I rejected abstraction or multi-object scenes to focus solely on the failure of justice as a broken mechanism, making the spiritual consequence (vice, avarice, automatic excommunication/suspension) tangible in a single, isolated, corrupted object. The shattered state emphasizes the "theft" and spiritual danger, not merely the administration.
