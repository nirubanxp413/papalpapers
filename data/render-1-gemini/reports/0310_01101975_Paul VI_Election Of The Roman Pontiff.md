# Render: Election Of The Roman Pontiff

## Why

A single, ornate liturgical key is selected to visualize *Election Of The Roman Pontiff*. This object is universally recognized as the symbol of papal authority and the power of the keys. It perfectly embodies the exclusive authority of the College of Cardinals during a vacancy, the strict secrecy of the conclave, and the procedural mechanics for selecting the new Roman Pontiff described in the document. A single key is more concrete and recognizable than abstract representations of processes, secrecy, or collegiate authority, fulfilling the requirement for a single, immediately legible sculptural form.
