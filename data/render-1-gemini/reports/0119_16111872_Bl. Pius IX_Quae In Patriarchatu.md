# Render: Quae In Patriarchatu

## Why

To embody Pope Pius IX's corrective account of Patriarch Joseph Audo's jurisdictional overreach and subsequent insincere submission in *Quae In Patriarchatu*, I chose the single recognizable subject of a shepherd's crozier. This traditional symbol represents both legitimate patriarchal authority and pastoral care, but also obedience and fidelity to the supreme shepherd, the Pope. I rejected abstraction because the encyclical focuses on concrete actions, hierarchical order, and the defense of documented truth; a physical, stylized crozier clearly represents the *office* and *authority* that were the central points of the historical conflict.
