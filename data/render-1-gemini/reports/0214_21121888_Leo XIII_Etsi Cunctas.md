# Render: Etsi Cunctas

## Why

We chose a **pontifical glove** because it represents Pope Leo XIII’s direct material gift to the Irish bishops, embodying the "sacred furnishings" and clerical attire mentioned in *Etsi Cunctas*. This single, immediately recognizable liturgical object concretely translates the text's core message of "exceptional sympathy" and concrete support into a tangible form, rather than an abstract concept of relief. By rendering a left-hand glove, we avoid the abstract, symbolic nature of generic hands and instead present a specific, recognizable, and highly decorative devotional artifact that carries the full theological meaning of priestly authority and apostolic protection.
