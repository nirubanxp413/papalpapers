# Render: The Moral Limits Of Medical Research And Treatment

## Why

A shield is the single most appropriate recognizable object to represent the document's central theme of limiting medical and state power to protect the human person. It functions as a moral boundary, defining the point beyond which scientific inquiry and state authority cannot pass, preserving the inherent rights of the individual. This concrete, protective form is chosen over abstraction to immediately communicate the pope's clear moral limits, rather than a vague theological concept.
