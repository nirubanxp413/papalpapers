# Render: Counsel To Teaching Sisters

## Why

I chose a single, open, aged leather book with an oil lamp resting upon it. This object instantly represents the synthesis of genuine academic excellence (the book) and sacred formation (the lamp), which Pius XII identified as the dual, inseparable mission of the Teaching Sisters. The lamp’s constant, humble light symbolizes their consecrated lives (vows and the visible habit) as the necessary source of effective teaching, not an obstacle to it, rejecting abstract forms in favor of two universally recognized, harmonious tools of enlightenment and spiritual guidance.
