# Render: Quemadmodum

## Why

I chose an idealized, sacred sculpture of a single mother cradling an infant as the primary subject, embodying *Quemadmodum*’s central concern for the millions of suffering children and its call for protective charity. This figure immediately conveys the universal, archetypal need for nurturing love and safety, satisfying the theological parallel (children as the Divine Child) and the practical imperative (care for the vulnerable). An abstract form would dilute the encyclical's visceral appeal for aid and fail to capture the profound human distress of "innocent children" that Pius XII emphasizes.
