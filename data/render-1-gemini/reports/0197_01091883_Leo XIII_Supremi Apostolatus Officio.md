# Render: Supremi Apostolatus Officio

## Why

I chose a highly detailed, fortified **Rosary** as the single recognizable subject. It is the central weapon Leo XIII promotes as the "surer and more efficacious means" of celestial help, historically proven against the Albigensians and at Lepanto. An ornate Rosary immediately signifies the document's spiritual focus and practical directive without needing abstract geometric interpretation. This specific Rosary, featuring shield-like Pater beads and a sword-integrated Crucifix, visually emphasizes the Marian devotion as a powerful, protective spiritual arsenal.
