# Render: Amantissimus

## Why

The most appropriate sculptural form for *Amantissimus* is a highly ornate **Pontifical Tiara** resting upon an equally elaborate **Crosier (pastoral staff)**, which is topped not with a simple scroll, but a distinctive **Eastern-style (Byzantine/Greek) cross**. This single unified subject clearly encapsulates the encyclical's central tension: the absolute authority of the Roman Pontiff (represented by the Tiara and the Crosier of authority) over the entire Church, including the Eastern Rites, while simultaneously acknowledging and preserving their distinct liturgical traditions (represented by the specific shape of the Eastern cross). The sculpture rejects abstraction in favor of these instantly recognizable and historically loaded liturgical items, creating a concrete visualization of Petrine primacy and Rome's universal jurisdiction.
