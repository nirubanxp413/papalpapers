# Render: Datis Nuperrime

## Why

*Datis Nuperrime* is Pius XII’s direct condemnation of the brutal Soviet suppression of the 1956 Hungarian Revolution. He decries the violent destruction of newly restored institutions and frames the immense bloodshed, referencing Abel's cry to God, as a divine moral charge that will not go unpunished. A single, broken liturgical chalice is the most effective subject for this encylical, capturing the simultaneous violence against religious life and the shattering of Hungarian national sovereignty. The chalice is instantly recognizable and sacred, making its obvious violent destruction feel like a profound moral and theological injury, rather than an abstract concept.
