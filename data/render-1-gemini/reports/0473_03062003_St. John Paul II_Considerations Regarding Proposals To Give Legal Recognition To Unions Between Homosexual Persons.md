# Render: Considerations Regarding Proposals To Give Legal Recognition To Unions Between Homosexual Persons

## Why

This document emphasizes marriage as uniquely established by God between a man and a woman as the fundamental cell of society. To represent this indispensable, irreducible foundation and the Church's role in defending it, I chose the simple, ancient, and universally recognizable symbol of a **foundation stone**. This single, concrete object represents both the Church (Peter, the rock) and the natural institution of marriage that the document defends as the only legitimate context for human union. Any abstract or multi-object concept would fail the legibility test and risk depicting "unions" or "comparisons" that the document itself explicitly rejects.
