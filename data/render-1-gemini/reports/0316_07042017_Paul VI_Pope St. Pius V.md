# Render: Pope St. Pius V

## Why

Given the document’s nature as a mere bibliography of texts issued under Pope St. Pius V, the single object must serve as a repository or container of this intellectual and spiritual tradition. We reject abstraction as unable to represent a specific pope or historical record. The most potent and recognizable subject is a papal *tiara*—specifically, the three-tiered crown—which signifies the three universal powers traditionally ascribed to the papacy and uniquely links the figure of St. Pius V, known for his definitive liturgical and theological reforms (the Tridentine Mass, the revised Missal), to the authority represented by the list. A sacred, sculptural tiara embodies the centralized authority and tradition contained within the documents listed.
