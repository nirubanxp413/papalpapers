# Render: Summi Maeroris

## Why

To translate the encyclical *Summi Maeroris*, which calls for an end to social hatred and political disorder through the application of truth, justice, and charity, the sculpture is an ornate Olive Branch. An olive branch is instantly recognizable as an ancient and universal symbol of peace and reconciliation, perfectly capturing the core objective of the document. The single branch, laden with fruit and leaves, embodies the possibility of moral renewal and lasting concord when rooted in Christian principles, avoiding abstract shapes by presenting a specific, legible, sacred object.
