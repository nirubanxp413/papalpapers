# Render: Caritatis Studium

## Why

I chose a monstrance as the single recognizable subject for this sculpture. It best captures Leo XIII's argument that the Eucharist is the essential perpetuation of the Cross, rather than an addition to it, which represents the theological core of his call for reunion. A monstrance is immediately identifiable as a sacred vessel, avoiding abstraction while powerfully symbolizing the central mystery and act of worship that the encyclical seeks to restore to Scotland.
