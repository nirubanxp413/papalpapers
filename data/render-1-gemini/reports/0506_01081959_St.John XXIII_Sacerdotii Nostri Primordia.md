# Render: Sacerdotii Nostri Primordia

## Why

I chose the subject of a priest in the act of elevation during Mass because it is the quintessential expression of the priesthood's purpose, central to St. John XXIII's *Sacerdotii Nostri Primordia*. While symbols like a chalice or a confessional are legible, this single figure unites the encyclical’s focus on St. John Vianney's intense Eucharistic piety, his sacrificial ministry, and the "twin lights" of the spiritual life that all priests are called to emulate. An abstract representation was rejected because it would fail to capture the human fidelity, personal holiness, and concrete pastoral action that the encyclical presents as the standard for diocesan priests.
