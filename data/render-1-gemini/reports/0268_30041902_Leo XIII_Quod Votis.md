# Render: Quod Votis

## Why

The establishment of a Catholic University is fundamentally an intellectual endeavor rooted in sacred truth and authoritative endorsement. The standard archetype for an academic institution, particularly one authorized by a Pope, is the open book, symbolizing knowledge, study, and revelation. Rather than using abstract geometry to represent concepts like wisdom or institution, the sculpture of an open volume provides an instantly recognizable subject. The single book, depicted in a state of reading and transmission, captures the central purpose of Leo XIII's letter—the culmination of many years of intellectual preparation for the university—and isolates this meaning as a sacred, unified object without environmental context.
