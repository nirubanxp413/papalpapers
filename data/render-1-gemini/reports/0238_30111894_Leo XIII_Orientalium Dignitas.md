# Render: Orientalium Dignitas

## Why

I chose a highly ornate Byzantine-style *Epigonation* (the diamond-shaped liturgical vestment worn by Eastern clergy, often featuring an icon) as the single recognizable subject. This sacred object instantly identifies the unique, ancient heritage of the Eastern Rites that *Orientalium Dignitas* seeks to protect, specifically linking liturgical distinctiveness with patriarchal authority (as the vestment hangs from the belt). I rejected abstract concepts like "unity in diversity" or "legal defense," opting instead for this specific, concrete, and deeply symbolic item of Eastern vesture to visualize Leo XIII’s central act of guarding Eastern identity and jurisdiction.
