# Render: The Sacrament Of Anointing Of The Sick

## Why

I chose a singular, consecrated oil lamp as the sculptural subject because *Sacramental Unction of the Sick* focuses on the material and spiritual reality of anointing. The document clarifies the specific sacramental action using holy oil for healing, strength, and grace, transforming the understanding from only 'last rites' to a sacrament for the dangerously ill. A single lamp symbolizes both the natural medium (vegetable oil) and the spiritual effect (the light of grace and comfort), offering a concrete, instantly recognizable object of care and faith over any abstract theological concept.
