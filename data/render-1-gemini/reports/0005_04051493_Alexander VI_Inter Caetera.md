# Render: Inter Caetera

## Why

I chose a monumental astrolabe to represent *Inter Caetera* because it is a single, recognizable object that concretely embodies the document's fusion of advanced navigation (Columbus’s voyage) with papal authority (Alexander VI’s territorial grant). While the document deals with abstract concepts like political legitimacy and spiritual mandates, the astrolabe is a physical, graspable tool used to chart the stars and divide the world, translating the abstract line of demarcation into a specific technical instrument. It captures the essence of exploration and ownership without resorting to an abstract sculpture or a complex diorama, satisfying the single-subject discipline while reflecting the Papacy's role in licensing global expansion.
