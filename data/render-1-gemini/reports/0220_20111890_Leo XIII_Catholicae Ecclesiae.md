# Render: Catholicae Ecclesiae

## Why

A broken, shattered shackle, rendered as a sacred sculpture, instantly signifies the encyclical’s focus on the liberation of human beings from slavery. The fracture in the metal represents the active suppression of the slave trade and the end of bondage, as called for by Leo XIII. While a missionary figure or cross would emphasize evangelization, the specific focus on opposing the *African slave trade* is most immediately and universally conveyed by this single, potent, and concrete symbol of broken captivity. Abstraction was rejected because it could not provide the same instantaneous and visceral recognition.
