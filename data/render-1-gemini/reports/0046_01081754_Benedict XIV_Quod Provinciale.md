# Render: Quod Provinciale

## Why

I chose a **baptismal font** because it is the primary physical site of Christian initiation and the context in which a name is traditionally bestowed and recorded within the Church. It directly represents the sacrament and the "Christian profession" that *Quod Provinciale* claims is being "cowardly concealed" by adopting non-Christian names. A font, as a unified, sacred, and universally recognized liturgical vessel, is much more concrete and legible than abstract concepts of identity or concealment, perfectly encapsulating the conflict between the required public admission of faith (the water of baptism) and the practical avoidance of persecution (a name *not* given at the font).
