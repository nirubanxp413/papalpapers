# Render: Mirae Caritatis

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Mirae Caritatis* because the encyclical presents the Eucharist as the absolute center and "very soul of the Church," toward which all Christian life gravitates. A monstrance is the quintessential liturgical object for publicly displaying and focusing veneration on the Eucharist (Host), embodying Leo XIII's call to revive frequent reception and devotion as the remedy for societal ills. A more abstract form was rejected because it would lack the immediate, tangible recognition and the specific religious focus required to translate the Pope's central argument about Eucharistic centrality.
