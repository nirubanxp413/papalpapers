# Render: Orientale Lumen

## Why

I chose an idealized oil lamp for *Orientale Lumen* because the entire apostolic letter uses "The Light of the East" (*Orientale Lumen*) as its core metaphor for Eastern Christian theology, liturgy, and monasticism. This single, ancient, recognizable object perfectly captures the document's central theme of Eastern spirituality as a gift that illuminates the universal Church. I rejected abstract concepts like "divinization" or "ecumenism" in favor of the concrete source of light, a form immediately associated with sacred ritual and historical continuity in the East.
