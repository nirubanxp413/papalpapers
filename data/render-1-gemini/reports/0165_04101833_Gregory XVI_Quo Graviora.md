# Render: Quo Graviora

## Why

I chose a single, monumental papal tiara resting on a solid, unbreakable rock base for *Quo Graviora*. This subject was selected because Gregory XVI's encyclical is a direct defense of papal authority, hierarchical order, and the fixed doctrines of the Church against a coordinated "reforming" movement seeking to undermine these very things. The triple crown of the tiara is the most recognized symbol of the Supreme Pontiff's sovereign authority, directly contrasting the "false doctrine" of the priests who sought to weaken it, while the unyielding rock represents the established Church as an immutable fortress against their attacks, fulfilling the single recognizable subject rule without resorting to abstract geometry. The sculpture clearly communicates the reassertion of centralized, hierarchical power against a specific threat.
