# Render: Ubi Nos

## Why

I chose a solitary, highly ornamented fortress gate with a heavy, unyielding lock to symbolize *Ubi Nos*. The gate represents the Holy See’s inherent and necessary temporal sovereignty, which Pope Pius IX argued was essential for the spiritual independence and safety of the Papacy. The impenetrable nature of the gate, reinforced by sacred symbols and a prominent lock, directly visually translates the refusal to accept the Italian government’s “Law of Guarantees” and the reassertion of the Pope’s right to independent civil rule, rejecting secular control. This concrete structure avoids abstract representation while capturing the themes of fortified sovereignty and resistance.
