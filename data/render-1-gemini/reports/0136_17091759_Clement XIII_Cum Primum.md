# Render: Cum Primum

## Why

*Cum Primum* addresses the specific problem of clerics prioritizing commerce, money-changing, and worldly profit over their spiritual duties. A single, overturned wooden counting table, with coins falling from it and collecting below, vividly captures the action of prioritizing spiritual values over financial accumulation, directly referencing the biblical cleansing of the temple. This concrete, recognizable everyday object immediately communicates the encyclical's focus on material versus spiritual values, avoiding abstract concepts and providing a clear, impactful visual representation.
