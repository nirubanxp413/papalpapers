# Render: Mense Maio

## Why

A single, monumental set of rosary beads, presented as a complete and unbroken loop, embodies Pope Paul VI's twofold appeal in *Mense Maio*: the devotion traditionally linked to the month of May and the primary spiritual weapon against the escalating geopolitical conflicts. The sculpture rejects abstraction for the concrete practice of the Rosary, which the document explicitly urges as the answer to war and internal Church challenges. By isolating the rosary on a transparent background, its circular, unified form symbolizes the binding strength of prayer, the interconnection of the faithful, and the spiritual protection of the "Queen of Peace" without requiring the visual clutter of a diorama, the Virgin Mary figure itself, or complex abstract geometry.
