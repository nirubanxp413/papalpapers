# Render: Acerbo Nimis

## Why

I chose a highly ornate, illuminated book resting on a specialized lectern as the single sculptural subject. *Acerbo Nimis* identifies "ignorance of things divine" as the root of spiritual decay and prescribes systematic catechetical instruction as the essential remedy. A prominent, open book symbolizes the Catechism and the divine knowledge it contains, which Pope Pius X argues must be transmitted clearly to all Christians. This concrete object embodies the intellectual enlightenment that guides the will and prevents moral ruin, avoiding abstraction by presenting the primary tool of the encyclical's proposed solution.
