# Render: Pastorale Munus

## Why

I chose a single, ornately decorated mitre for this sculpture because it is the most recognizable, singular symbol representing the office and function of a residential bishop. *Pastorale Munus* explicitly focuses on granting expanded faculties and autonomy to these specific bishops, elevating their local apostolic authority and simplifying their direct governance of their dioceses. Choosing the mitre, rendered in sacred materials, visually embodies the dignity, authority, and pastoral responsibility the Pope is bestowing and affirming through this specific, non-abstract, concrete liturgical object.
