# Render: Urbanitatis Veteris

## Why

I chose an open antique book displaying the Greek alphabet (specifically, the Alpha and Omega visible in a stylized form) resting upon a base featuring relief carvings of classical Doric columns and Byzantine architectural elements. This single recognizable object, a sacred text or scholarly book, captures the essence of *Urbanitatis Veteris*, which appeals to Greece’s dual legacy of classical philosophy and Eastern Christianity. The book symbolizes the intellectual and spiritual "treasures" Pope Leo XIII wishes to reclaim and reunite with Rome, making the call for union a restoration rather than an imposition. I rejected abstraction because the encyclical relies entirely on concrete historical figures, specific philosophical systems (Aristotle), and defined ecclesiastical history (Greek Fathers), all of which demand tangible, legible iconography rather than ambiguous geometry.
