# Render: Deus Caritas Est

## Why

A unified, sculpted heart best embodies *Deus Caritas Est*’s definition of love: not abstract emotion, but a concrete reality where human affection (eros) and divine charity (agape) merge. This universally understood symbol grounds the encyclical's profound theology in an instantly legible form, fulfilling the mandate for immediate recognition and a single subject. The choice rejects abstraction by offering a tangible representation of Pope Benedict XVI's primary theme: the dynamic, unified nature of love that originates in God and must manifest as concrete action through the Church.
