# Render: Auspicato Concessum

## Why

For *Auspicato Concessum*, which promotes the Third Order of St. Francis as a practical means for laypeople to restore Christian morality amidst modern social and spiritual decay, the single recognizable subject is the Franciscan habit’s cord and the cross. The Franciscan cord is the primary devotional object identifying members of the Third Order, symbolizing their rule of life and commitment to poverty, chastity (implied simple living), and obedience within secular society. Its single loop and the attached cross visually and concretely represent Leo XIII’s call to imitation of Francis, anchoring the spiritual call in a tangible, recognizable object of Catholic practice, rather than an abstract concept.
