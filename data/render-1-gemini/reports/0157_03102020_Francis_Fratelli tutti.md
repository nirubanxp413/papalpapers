# Render: Fratelli tutti

## Why

To translate *Fratelli Tutti*’s call for universal fraternity and an outward-facing love that crosses all borders, I have chosen a **sculptural bridge**. The bridge is an instantly identifiable, concrete, and familiar archetypal structure of connection, physically spanning a divide. It embodies the encyclical’s core argument for dialogue over "a culture of walls" much more directly than an abstract shape or a specific figure like the Good Samaritan, maintaining immediate legibility on a transparent background. A bridge is fundamentally about relationship and accessibility, rejecting the fragmentation and exclusion described in the text by its very function.
