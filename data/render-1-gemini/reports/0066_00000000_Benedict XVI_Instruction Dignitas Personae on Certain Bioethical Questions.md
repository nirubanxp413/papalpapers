# Render: Instruction Dignitas Personae on Certain Bioethical Questions

## Why

*Dignitas Personae* centers on the fundamental principle that every human being, from the moment of conception, possesses full anthropological and ethical status and must be treated as a person. The chosen sculptural subject is a human embryo, not depicted abstractly, but rendered as a highly naturalistic, identifiable form—the recognizable "sacred figure" of the earliest human life. By presenting the embryo as a single, monumental sculpture isolated from any clinical context, the artwork embodies the document's insistence on recognizing the dignity inherent in "the first moment of existence." A symbolic object or abstraction was rejected because the encyclical's core "yes" is not to an idea, but to the *person* at every stage, demanding a tangible, biological visualization.
