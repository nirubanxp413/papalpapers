# Render: Exiit qui seminat

## Why

I chose a single, recognizable subject—a monstrance—because it is a sacred liturgical vessel that perfectly embodies the core conflict of *Exiit qui seminat*. The monstrance is defined by holding and displaying the Blessed Sacrament, yet its entire existence is for a spiritual, rather than material, purpose. For the Franciscan Rule, it captures the paradox of necessary *usus facti* (simple use of fact) without any accompanying ownership (*dominion*). Like the monstrance, the friars use material necessities to support spiritual life, while legal possession of everything is held elsewhere by the Papacy. The object is a single, clear, and instantly recognizable devotional form, rejecting abstraction in favor of a concrete object that is defined by its role as a vessel of transcendence, not self-contained ownership.
