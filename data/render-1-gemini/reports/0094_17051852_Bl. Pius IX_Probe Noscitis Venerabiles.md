# Render: Probe Noscitis Venerabiles

## Why

*Probe Noscitis Venerabiles* is a pastoral directive urging Spanish bishops to implement and defend a formal Agreement (Concordat) while concurrently purifying and unifying the Church through internal reform and sound teaching. A mitre with a crozier (the symbols of episcopal authority and pastoral care) has been selected as the single subject because it precisely encapsulates the encyclical's primary target audience and its dual mandate: external legal defense (crozier) and internal spiritual discipline (mitre). These recognizable symbols are chosen over abstract forms because they are immediately legible to the faithful and historically specific to the episcopal authority *Pius IX* is addressing and empowering in Spain.
