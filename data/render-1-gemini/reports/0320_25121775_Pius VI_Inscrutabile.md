# Render: Inscrutabile

## Why

I chose a single, traditional mitre to represent *Inscrutabile* because it embodies the Pope's dual message of pastoral governance and a defensive stance against ideological external threats. The mitre, a concrete and instantly recognizable sacred vestment, signifies both internal episcopal unity, which Pius VI urgently calls for, and the ecclesiastical authority that is explicitly being challenged. I rejected abstraction because it could not clearly or immediately communicate this specific, hierarchical, and institutional defense of the Church's ancient traditions against the "philosophy full of deceit."
