# Render: NOTA DOTTRINALE SU ALCUNI ASPETTI DELL’EVANGELIZZAZIONE

## Why

We chose a lighthouse as the sculptural form because it is a single, recognizable, and universally understood object that perfectly symbolizes the doctrinal note's argument. Just as a lighthouse provides a necessary, life-saving light to guide ships safely to port, the document asserts that proclaiming the Gospel is an essential obligation to offer humanity the light of Christ. A lighthouse embodies the core theme of evangelization—which the text calls "the first service"—as a clear, non-negotiable beacon that respects human dignity while addressing humanity's deepest spiritual needs. We rejected abstract forms or a cluster of symbols because the docment demands a powerful and unambiguous message about missionary activity; an abstract sculpture would be too vague, and multiple objects would dilute the clarity of the lighthouse as the singular, unwavering source of guidance and conversion.
