# Render: Editae Saepe

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a highly decorated gold **monstrance** holding the Eucharist. This single object captures the encyclical’s core call for a 'true reformation' focused on internal spiritual renewal through the Sacraments, specifically the Eucharist as a defense against Modernism and doctrinal novelty. It embodies St. Charles Borromeo’s example of adhering to Roman authority and sacred tradition, standing in stark contrast to political noise or the iconoclasm associated with the Protestant reformers St. Pius X critiques. As a defined liturgical vessel, it is instantly recognizable and profoundly sacred, avoiding abstraction while symbolizing the source of Catholic unity and spiritual strength central to the document’s remedies.
