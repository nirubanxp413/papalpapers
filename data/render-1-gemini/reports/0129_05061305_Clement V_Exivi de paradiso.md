# Render: Exivi de paradiso

## Why

I chose a single, worn monastic sandal to embody *Exivi de paradiso* because the document attempts to define the specific boundaries and requirements of Franciscan poverty, especially regarding the 'strict use' of essential items. The sandal is a simple, recognizable symbol of the mendicant life and the literal vow of walking in Christ's footsteps according to the Rule. This single object concretizes the abstract theological debate, materializing the essential minimum—a covering for the foot—over a more complex abstraction or an elaborate scene of friars in consultation.
