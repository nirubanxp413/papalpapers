# Render: Singulari Quidem

## Why

We chose a detailed bronze **compass rose** as the single recognizable subject for *Singulari Quidem*. The encyclical directly attacks "indifferentism," the claim that any spiritual path leads to salvation; the compass, as an object that decisively indicates a single correct direction (North/Truth), concretely refutes this relativistic view. Its function is to provide unwavering direction in a sea of error, perfectly embodying Pius IX’s reaffirmation of the Catholic Church and the See of Peter as the exclusive vessel for salvation. We rejected abstract geometry or a metaphorical "anchor" because a compass is more intellectually precise: it is about orientation toward truth (North) and the corrective discipline required to maintain it against the surrounding deviations of rationalism and indifference. A single, refined navigational instrument provides immediate, universal legibility.
