# Render: Sacramentum Ordinis

## Why

*Sacramentum Ordinis* defines the imposition of hands as the single essential physical act (matter) for a valid ordination, explicitly downgrading the *traditio instrumentorum* (handing over the instruments of office). The most direct and universally recognizable translation of this concept into a single object is a sculptural representation of a bishop's hands performing this specific act. It isolates the singular 'matter' of the sacrament—the imposition of hands—and transforms the gesture itself into the sacred sculptural object. This choice emphasizes the essential human-divine contact over any secondary material instruments, using a clear figurative subject rather than an abstract form to convey the document’s focus.
