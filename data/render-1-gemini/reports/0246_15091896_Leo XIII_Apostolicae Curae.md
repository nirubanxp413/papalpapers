# Render: Apostolicae Curae

## Why

I selected a single, meticulously sculpted Catholic crozier for *Apostolicae Curae* because the encyclical definitively adjudicates the invalidity of Anglican Holy Orders, a matter intrinsic to the pastoral office symbolized by the bishop's staff. An abstract sculpture would fail to communicate this specific ruling on ecclesiastical jurisdiction and authority. A crozier is an immediately recognizable sacred object that carries the weight of spiritual and temporal authority, made physically distinct as a Roman Catholic symbol to embody the Pope’s binding judgment on a question of valid apostolic succession.
