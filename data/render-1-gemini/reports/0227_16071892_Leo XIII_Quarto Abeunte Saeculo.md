# Render: Quarto Abeunte Saeculo

## Why

An antique maritime astrolabe was chosen because it represents Columbus's journey as a feat of advanced navigation—the "celebrated explorer"—while its surface is unified by the engraving of the Most Holy Trinity and a central, radiant cross. This transformation from a purely functional tool into a sacred object perfectly embodies the encyclical's central argument: that while Columbus’s voyage was a major historical exploration, its driving purpose was evangelization. It rejects abstract geometry or a collage of separate symbols (like a cross and a boat) by unifying the specific historical artifact with its religious purpose into a single, cohesive, recognizable form.
