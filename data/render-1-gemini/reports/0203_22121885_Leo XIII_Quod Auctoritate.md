# Render: Quod Auctoritate

## Why

The chosen subject is a massive, oxidized bronze bell, its surface covered in ornate relief. The bell is a globally recognized object and sacred symbol whose singular function—ringing out a call—perfectly mirrors Pope Leo XIII's proclamation of a universal Jubilee indulgence. It represents the urgent, collective summons to all faithful to observe this sacred year through prayer, penance, and the Rosary, contrasting directly with abstract concepts or silent geometric forms. The physical weight of the bell conveys the magnitude and gravity of the Jubilee announcement for the entire Christian world in response to moral decay.
