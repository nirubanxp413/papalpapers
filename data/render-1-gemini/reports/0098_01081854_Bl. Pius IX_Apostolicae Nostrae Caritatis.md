# Render: Apostolicae Nostrae Caritatis

## Why

Apostolicae Nostrae Caritatis calls for intensive spiritual weaponry — prayer, penance, and almsgiving through a jubilee — to combat forces subverting the Church and human society, while pointing towards the imminent definition of the Immaculate Conception. The censer, a vessel central to both liturgical worship and penitential processions, perfectly embodies this response. It translates the abstract need for purified prayer and communal atonement into the single, concrete action of sacrificial incense rising to heaven, representing the faithful’s plea for protection and divine intervention against spiritual calamities.
