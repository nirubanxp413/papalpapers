# Render: Aeterni Patris

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this sculpture because *Aeterni Patris* focuses on re-establishing solid, traditional theology (specifically Thomism) as the source of Truth. While a book or a lamp might symbolize knowledge or reason, a monstrance specifically enshrines the Eucharist, the source and summit of the Catholic faith, which Thomas Aquinas powerfully defended through reason. This form represents the harmonious relationship Leo XIII sought to restore: sound philosophy (the ornate, rational structure of the monstrance) holding and presenting Sacred Mystery (the Eucharist) clearly to the world. It is a concrete liturgical object, instantly recognizable and visually coherent, avoiding all abstraction while perfectly capturing the document's call for intellectual order grounded in revelation.
