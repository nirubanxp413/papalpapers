# Render: Quam Singulari

## Why

*Quam Singulari* is best embodied by the sculpture of a child's single First Holy Communion shoe. The document addresses the profound injustice of delaying access to the Eucharist, ensuring even the youngest "use of reason" (age seven) could approach the altar. This small, familiar object of devotional attire instantly communicates the identity of the forgotten child while representing the journey of faith—the "walking" to the sacrament—made accessible again by Pope Pius X's decree. Choosing a specific, recognizable item of clothing is more powerful and legible than an abstract representation of "grace" or "age," focusing the meaning entirely on the newly admitted recipient.
