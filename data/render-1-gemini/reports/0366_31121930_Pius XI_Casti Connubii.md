# Render: Casti Connubii

## Why

I chose a **Ciborium**, a specific vessel used to hold the Eucharist, because *Casti Connubii* elevates marriage to a sacramental status on par with other holy mysteries. The ciborium's recognizable form and function within Catholic liturgy perfectly symbolize the encyclical’s defense of marriage as a divinely instituted, sacred, and fruitful bond, with the three distinct parts (base, stem, bowl/lid) embodying the three "blessings" (sacrament, faith, offspring) into a single, cohesive, and revered devotional object. This concrete and legibly sacred item anchors the abstract theology of the sacrament in a tangible form, rejecting abstraction in favor of liturgical clarity.
