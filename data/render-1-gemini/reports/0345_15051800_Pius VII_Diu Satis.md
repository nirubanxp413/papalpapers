# Render: Diu Satis

## Why

I chose a single, reinforced shepherd’s staff (crozier) as the sculptural form because it perfectly embodies Pius VII's rallying cry in *Diu Satis*. The crozier is the primary, instantly recognizable symbol of episcopal authority and pastoral care, directly addressing the bishops to whom the document is written. By making it from forged metal and embedding it firmly in a rocky plinth, the sculpture translates the encyclical’s themes of resilience, discipline, and the Church's survival through severe trials into a single, concrete, and unyielding object, rejecting all abstract symbolism.
