# Render: Benedictus Deus

## Why

To translate Benedictus Deus into a single recognizable form, I chose the **Great Jubilee Door of St. Peter's Basilica**. While the document extends the indulgence globally, the door remains the preeminent and universally understood visual symbol of the Jubilee, representing the passage from sin to grace and the welcoming of pilgrims. This choice was selected over an abstract form (such as a geometric representation of 'extension') because the door provides a tangible, sacred focal point for the complex theological ideas of mercy, pilgrimage, and universal reception of the Church's treasury. It rejects abstraction by grounding the meaning in a specific, historical devotional object.
