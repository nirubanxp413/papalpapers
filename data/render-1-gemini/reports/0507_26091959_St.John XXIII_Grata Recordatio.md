# Render: Grata Recordatio

## Why

The rosary is chosen as the single recognizable subject because Pope John XXIII explicitly defines it as the unifying instrument and central remedy for all the diverse challenges addressed in *Grata Recordatio*, from the continuity of the Papacy to global peace. A simple rosary is the quintessential devotional item that immediately legibly captures the encyclical's core call to action. To avoid abstract form and emphasize the monumental spiritual power of this humble object, the sculpture is rendered as a singular, robust loop of oversized beads carved from white marble, presented with the weight and sacred presence of a timeless monument.
