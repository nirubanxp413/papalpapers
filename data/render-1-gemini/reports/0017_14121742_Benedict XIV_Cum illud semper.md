# Render: Cum illud semper

## Why

I chose a set of balancing scales, featuring a mitre in one pan and a heavy, standardized rule in the other, as the single recognizable subject. This form encapsulates *Cum illud semper*'s core demand for justice and order in the selection of rectors, directly visualizing the competitive examination (the rule) as the objective counterweight to worthiness for office (the mitre). The subject is immediately legible and concrete, unlike an abstract representation of fairness; it avoids the complexity of a tableau or diorama by presenting a unified, ancient symbol of standardized judgment and pastoral authority.
