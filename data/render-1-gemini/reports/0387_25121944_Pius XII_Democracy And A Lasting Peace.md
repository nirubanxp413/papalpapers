# Render: Democracy And A Lasting Peace

## Why

A single, monumental bell is chosen to represent Pius XII’s 1944 Christmas message, "Democracy And A Lasting Peace." As a recognizable, everyday sacred object, it symbolizes the "awakening" of the peoples (its booming voice), the moral character required for authentic democracy, and the proclamation of human dignity (the Church’s role). The bell, having "cracked" the old order of dictatorship to ring in a new, just peace, represents the "crusade of charity" and the international community of nations, offering a powerful, resonant symbol of hope and unity during wartime. Abstract forms were rejected because they lack the necessary legibility and universal resonance to clearly communicate the encyclical's message to a broad audience.
