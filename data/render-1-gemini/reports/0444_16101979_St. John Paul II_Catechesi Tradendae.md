# Render: Catechesi Tradendae

## Why

A burning candle on a simple stand was chosen because it perfectly encapsulates the encyclical's core message: Sunday as a sacred day that radiates divine light throughout the rest of the week. This single, recognizable subject symbolizes the light of the Risen Christ (*Dies Christi*) and the creation of light (*Dies Domini*), providing spiritual warmth and illumination to the faithful. A abstract form would struggle to convey this sense of living, vital, and accessible spiritual light that Pope John Paul II emphasizes as a necessary human and spiritual need.
