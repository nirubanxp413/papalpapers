# Render: Amoris laetitia

## Why

A mother pelican vulning itself to feed its young best captures Pope Francis’s *Amoris laetitia* because it is an ancient, recognizable symbol for sacrificial love within a family context. This specific sacred figure was chosen over abstract forms because it vividly embodies Christ’s self-giving love (Eucharist) and the everyday parental sacrifice required for family life, rejecting the vagueness of an abstract design. The choice of a *mother* pelican (pelican-in-her-piety) directly translates the document’s focus on the concrete realities, joys, and struggles of families and child-rearing. The sculpture avoids being a "multi-object scene" because it is a single unified figure depicting one maternal act, where the offspring and the sacrificial action are essential, built-in parts of this established sacred iconography.
