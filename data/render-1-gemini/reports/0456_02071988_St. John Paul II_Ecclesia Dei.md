# Render: Ecclesia Dei

## Why

The Apostolic Letter *Ecclesia Dei* addresses the Ukranian Greek Catholic Church, a Church that has endured severe persecution but remained steadfast in its fidelity to Rome while also seeking unity with Orthodoxy. A monstrance (custodia) containing the Eucharistic Host is the ideal symbol because it is the quintessential expression of the Catholic Church’s visible presence, central worship, and enduring faith, as well as the point of communion that *is* the body of Christ, unifying all believers. A monstrance, specifically one crafted with a distinctive Byzantine or Eastern-style radiant cross (referencing the Ukrainian Greek Catholic tradition), immediately communicates the reality of the visible Church, the enduring Eucharist, and the theological goal of unity (the one Body of Christ) without resorting to complex, abstract geometry. The subject is concretely recognizable, religiously profound, and perfectly encapsulates the encyclical's focus on the suffering and fidelity of this specific Church within the universal Catholic communion.
