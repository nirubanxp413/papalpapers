# Render: Clara claris praeclara

## Why

I chose a single, perfectly spherical, polished optical-grade glass orb as the sculptural subject to embody *Clara claris praeclara*. This choice directly translates Pope Alexander IV’s central metaphorical argument, which plays exhaustively on Clare's name meaning "clear," without resorting to abstract geometry. The pure, flawless clarity and radiance of the glass perfectly visualize her spiritual purity and sanctity. I rejected an abstract form or a traditional figure because only a simple, highly defined physical object like this flawless orb could simultaneously capture her profound simplicity and her brilliant, illuminating presence.
