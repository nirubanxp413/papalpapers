# Render: Communium Interpretes Doloraum

## Why

I chose a single, stylized **Olive Branch** for *Communium Interpretes Doloraum*. The document is a cry for peace at the end of WWII, specifically through divine intervention (May devotions to Mary) and a just negotiation that doesn't sow seeds of future conflict. While complex, the entire argument points toward one desperate outcome: peace as a fragile, divine gift, symbolized universally by the olive branch. An abstract form would dilute the emotional urgency of 1945, whereas a single, highly refined branch captures both the hope for peace and the 'leaves' (nations) suffering the 'anguish.' I rejected multi-object scenes or abstract representations to ensure the sculpture remains focused, recognizable, and deeply connected to the document's central plea for an end to the "grievous distress" through justice and charity.
