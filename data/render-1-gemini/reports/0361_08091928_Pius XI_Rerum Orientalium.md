# Render: Rerum Orientalium

## Why

Rerum Orientalium emphasizes that the key to unity between East and West is rigorous scholarship, study, and a correct understanding of Eastern traditions. I chose a large, leather-bound volume of scholarly study on a ceremonial stand because it is the quintessential, immediately recognizable symbol of academic discipline, intellectual formation, and the textual transmission of knowledge the encyclical demands. An abstract form could not capture this emphasis on rigorous, institutional study as the remedy for mutual ignorance and the path to full communion.
