# Render: Triumphantis Hierusalem

## Why

The bull *Triumphantis Hierusalem* celebrates the elevation of Saint Bonaventure to the status of Doctor of the Church, emphasizing his profound theological wisdom, his leadership as Minister General of the Franciscan Order, and the integration of his intellectual work with spiritual devotion. A single, stylized mitre was chosen because it immediately and concretely represents his ecclesiastical authority, his scholarly preeminence as a bishop, and the high spiritual honor bestowed upon him. This specific, recognizable object was selected over an abstract representation of "wisdom" or "sanctity" (which would violate the guidelines) or a figure of the saint (which might become a tableau if include attributes) because the single object, enhanced with sacred materials and a key symbol, perfectly captures the bull's core theological elevation and recognition in a unified, legible form.
