# Render: Omnibus Compertum

## Why

A patriarchal staff was chosen as the singular sculptural subject because it perfectly encapsulates the encyclical’s focus on established hierarchy, patriarchal authority, and the delicate balance of jurisdictional rights. It is an immediately recognizable religious symbol of unity, pastoral care, and governing power within the Melchite structure. An abstract form would fail to convey the specific, concrete restoration of order and confirmation of rights between the Patriarch and the bishops that Leo XIII prescribes to prevent future disagreements. The staff provides a powerful, unified silhouette embodying leadership and church harmony without resorting to a multi-object scene or dependency on text for meaning.
