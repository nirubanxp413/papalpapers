# Render: Inimica Vis

## Why

We chose a detailed bronze and marble key. This choice rejects abstraction by selecting a single, concrete, recognizable object rooted in Church authority and spiritual discipline. The key represents Pope Leo XIII’s central act in *Inimica Vis*: identifying and locking down the "hostile force" of Freemasonry within Italy. The sculpture symbolizes the Pope exercising his teaching authority to counter deceit, strengthen the wavering, and guard the spiritual 'gates' of the Church against infiltration and civil encroachment, embodying the disciplined pastoral action and unity he demands.
