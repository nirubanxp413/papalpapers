# Render: Inter Multiplices

## Why

A Crosier, the bishop's pastoral staff, best symbolizes the encyclical *Inter Multiplices*. It directly represents the authority and pastoral office of the bishops Pope Pius IX addresses, charging them to reform seminaries and protect the faithful. While a shepherd figure would also work, the solitary Crosier offers a singular, iconic religious object that powerfully represents the pastoral duty, unity, and vigilance the Pope demands.

I have rendered a crosier, the bishop's pastoral staff, to represent the Pope's address to the French episcopate regarding their duty of vigilance and unity. The high-end 3D render features an ornate bronze and gold design with embedded gems and a central figure of a lamb, set on a dark plinth. The composition is dead-center with dramatic lighting, and the background is a generated transparency grid.
