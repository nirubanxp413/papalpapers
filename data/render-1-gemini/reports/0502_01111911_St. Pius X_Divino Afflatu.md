# Render: Divino Afflatu

## Why

To translate the encyclical *Divino Afflatu*, which is fundamentally about the comprehensive, orderly, and complete weekly recitation of the entire Psalter, the chosen sculptural subject is a single, unified, and perfectly balanced bundle of sheaves of golden wheat. The Psalter is often compared to a "field" or "granary" of prayer, where every Psalm is a necessary seed or grain for spiritual nourishment; therefore, the full bundle, held together by a single band, symbolizes the entire Psalter restored to its complete use. A bundle of sheaves is a universally recognizable, concrete object that immediately conveys a sense of fulfillment, harvest, and unified collection, rejecting abstraction while embodying the idea of all 150 Psalms (represented by the multitude of individual stalks and heads of grain) brought back into proper liturgical arrangement. A sacred symbol like a harp was rejected as it represents only one aspect of the Psalms' authorship, not the systemic, exhaustive, and complete collection of prayers that Pius X sought to ensure the Church prayed weekly.
