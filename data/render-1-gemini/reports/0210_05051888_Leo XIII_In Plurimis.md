# Render: In Plurimis

## Why

I chose a shepherd actively rescuing a stray sheep to embody *In Plurimis* because it is an instantly recognizable sacred figure representing the Church's long-standing effort to protect and liberate humanity from the bondage of sin and slavery. This single, unified sculpture captures the transition from peril (slavery) to salvation (freedom), perfectly reflecting the document's narrative of emancipation while avoiding a generic or abstract symbol like a broken chain. The dynamic action of lifting the sheep encapsulates both the Pope's past efforts regarding Brazilian slaves and his final appeal for the suffering populations in Africa, fulfilling the requirement for a specific, identifiable subject.
