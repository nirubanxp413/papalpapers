# Render: Responses to some questions regarding certain aspects of the Doctrine of the Church

## Why

I chose a **liturgical chalice** because it is a single, universally recognizable sacred object that perfectly encapsulates the CDF document's key argument: that the true and full expression of "Church" requires the valid celebration of the Eucharist. A chalice concretely visualizes the primary theological criterion for ecclesial reality—the Eucharistic Mystery—which Benedict XVI argues only the Catholic and Eastern Orthodox Churches possess. By using this single, unambiguous object, I directly embody the text’s sacramental focus without resorting to complex multi-object dioramas, abstract symbols, or scenes that would violate the prompt's structural and conceptual constraints.
