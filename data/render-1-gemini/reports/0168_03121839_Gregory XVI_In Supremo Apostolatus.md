# Render: In Supremo Apostolatus

## Why

I chose a single broken manacle as the sculptural form because it is the most visceral and instantly recognizable symbol of liberation from enslavement, the core act Pope Gregory XVI demands in *In Supremo Apostolatus*. A whole chain would suggest captivity, and a complex scene with figures would violate the single-subject discipline, so this concrete object, clearly snapped, isolates the encyclical’s definition of justice as the decisive act of breaking bonds and restoring human dignity. To use an abstract form would fail to communicate the specific, urgent historical demand for the immediate cessation of the slave trade.
