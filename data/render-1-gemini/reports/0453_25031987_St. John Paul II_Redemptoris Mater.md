# Render: Redemptoris Mater

## Why

For *Redemptoris Mater*, which centers on Mary as the first disciple who "advanced in her pilgrimage of faith" while simultaneously being the Mother of the Church, the optimal sculptural form is a single sacred figure: Mary herself, depicted as the "Woman of Faith" on a pilgrimage. This specific figure was chosen over abstract geometry because it instantly visualizes a person making a journey of belief, anchoring the abstract theological concept of "maternal mediation" in a relatable human form. A literal, recognizable human figure walking dynamicizes the concept of faith from a static state to an active path, and this single subject is more legible and emotionally resonant than any object or symbolic assembly, fulfilling the prompt's mandate for a concrete, non-abstract, immediately recognizable subject.
