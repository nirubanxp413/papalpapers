# Render: Le Pelerinage De Lourdes

## Why

I chose a sculpture of a single, unadorned iron scourge to materialize the encyclical's central and urgent call for "Penance, penance, penance!" over any more decorative or comfortable symbol of the Lourdes centenary. A scourge is an instantly recognizable, albeit harsh, tool that represents profound spiritual discipline, self-denial, and conversion—the indispensable remedy Pope Pius XII prescribes for societal materialism. Abstraction was rejected because the demand for "Penance" is concrete and demanding, requiring a form that is equally unambiguous and raw in its meaning when isolated.
