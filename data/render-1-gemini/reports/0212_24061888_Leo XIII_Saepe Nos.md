# Render: Saepe Nos

## Why

I chose a single, monolithic balance scale to embody Leo XIII's *Saepe Nos*. This classic symbol of justice directly translates the Pope’s absolute moral standard: that no cause, regardless of its perceived justice or the suffering involved, permits recourse to violence or injustice (like the Boycotting and Plan of Campaign). A scale is universally recognizable, providing immediate theological legibility and concrete form rather than abstract geometry. The sculpture depicts one unified object—the scale itself—with the two weighing pans in static, perfect alignment, emphasizing the clear, unyielding line of moral law the Pope insists the bishops uphold without ambiguity.
