# Render: Satis vobis compertum

## Why

To embody Pope Benedict XIV’s *Satis vobis compertum*, which regulates secret "marriages of conscience," I have chosen a **sealed, chained metal strongbox or lockbox**. This specific subject is instantly recognizable as an everyday object and a clear symbol of secrecy, containing contents that must be protected and preserved. The sealed nature represents the secret register that must be maintained in the diocesan chancellery, safely storing the records of clandestine marriages to prevent the "serious evils" and bigamy that Benedict XIV condemns, rather than using an abstract form which would fail to convey the concrete necessity of regulation and record-keeping. It physically manifests the Pope’s solution of secure, canonical documentation and authorized secrecy.
