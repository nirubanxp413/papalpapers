# Render: Dives In Misericordia Deus

## Why

To represent "Dives In Misericordia Deus," I chose a single recognizable subject: the **Sacred Heart**, but rendered not as a soft painting, but as a monumental sculpture of a human heart (representing the Incarnation) physically breaking through a rigid block of cold stone. The encyclical argues that Divine Mercy is not a sentiment but the essential foundation of reality, which justice alone cannot fulfill. The Heart emerging from the rock vividly symbolizes mercy (love, the Incarnation) piercing through the hardening effects of sin and abstract justice. I rejected abstract forms because only the visceral, identifiable heart and the impenetrable rock can convey the human reality and the 'hardened' global crises the Pope addresses, fulfilling the prompt's mandate for an immediate, legible subject.
