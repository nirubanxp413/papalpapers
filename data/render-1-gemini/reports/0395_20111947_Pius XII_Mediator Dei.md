# Render: Mediator Dei

## Why

A single, ornate monstrance, containing a perfectly rendered host, is chosen to represent *Mediator Dei*. It is the supreme symbol of the Mass, which Pius XII defines as the summit of the liturgy and the continuation of Christ's priestly mission. This recognizable object visually reconciles the encyclical’s core tension: the host represents the spiritual reality, and the precious monstrance signifies the necessary external, hierarchical, and regulated structure of Catholic worship, rejecting both abstract theology and purely internal devotion.
