# Render: Magnae Nobis

## Why

I chose an ornamental papal key to represent *Magnae Nobis*. The key is an instantly recognizable symbol of Catholic authority and teaching, derived from the keys given to Saint Peter. This single object visually grounds Pope Benedict XIV's exercise of magisterial power and judgment in refuting a widespread theological error. He uses his authority, like a master key, to clarify Church law, lock out false opinions regarding matrimonial dispensations for heretics, and reinforce the established, sacred boundaries of the faith. A key is inherently concrete and binary (locked/unlocked, true/false), which rejects the ambiguity of abstraction and the chaotic pluralism that the Pope was actively condemning.
