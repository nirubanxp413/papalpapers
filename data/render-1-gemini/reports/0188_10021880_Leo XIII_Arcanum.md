# Render: Arcanum

## Why

The encyclical *Arcanum* emphasizes that Christian marriage was instituted by God and elevated to a sacrament by Christ, defending its sacred nature and Church jurisdiction against secular appropriation and divorce. A sculpted representation must signify this indissoluble bond and divine origin using a single, recognizable object. We choose a **monumental, stylized wedding ring, forged of gold and stone, surmounted by a crown and a small cross**, symbolizing that true marriage is a sacred contract and sacrament ruled by Christ and His Church. The single unified object rejects abstraction and visually anchors the encyclical's complex arguments in the concrete, familiar symbol of the marital bond.
