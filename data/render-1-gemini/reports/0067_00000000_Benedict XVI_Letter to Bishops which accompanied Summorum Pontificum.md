# Render: Letter to Bishops which accompanied Summorum Pontificum

## Why

To represent Pope Benedict XVI’s message of liturgical continuity and internal reconciliation in *Summorum Pontificum*, I have selected the **Mitre**. This single, recognizable liturgical object embodies the unified pastoral authority that bridges the "ordinary" and "extraordinary" expressions of the one Roman Rite. The Mitre was chosen over abstract forms because it immediately and concretely symbolizes the Bishop of Rome’s unifying role in safeguarding the Church's liturgical tradition and preventing schism, without needing complex symbolic explanation.
