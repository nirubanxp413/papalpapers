# Render: Redemptor Hominis

## Why

The core argument of *Redemptor Hominis* is that Christ is the center of the universe and history, and only through Christ does humanity understand its own irreducible dignity. The chosen subject is the **Head of Christ**, rendered as an elevated, sacred sculpture, focusing the viewer entirely on the figure of the Redeemer. This choice avoids abstraction by anchoring the theological concept in the central figure of Christian faith. The sculpture must emphasize both divine transcendence and profound human suffering, uniting the Incarnation and Redemption in one recognizable form. A multi-object scene or abstraction would dilute this singular Christological focus and the direct claim that Christ is "the way for the Church."
