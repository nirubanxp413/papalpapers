# Render: Prodiit jamdudum

## Why

I chose a single, antique Lenten candle as the sculptural subject. This recognizable devotional item perfectly embodies the central conflict and ultimate resolution of *Prodiit jamdudum*: the tension between Carnival excess and obligatory fasting (represented by the dark, austere wax) and the stabilizing authority of the Papacy in providing a canonical "light" or path forward. The sculpture is concrete and legible; an abstract form would struggle to communicate this specific, practical tension between temporal indulgence and religious duty, whereas the candle, isolated on a simple plinth, acts as a unified symbol of established canonical law providing necessary guidance.
