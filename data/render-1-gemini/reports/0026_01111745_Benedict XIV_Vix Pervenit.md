# Render: Vix Pervenit

## Why

A set of balances (scales) was chosen because it is the universal symbol of justice and equilibrium, directly embodying *Vix Pervenit’s* core demand for strict equality in loan contracts. The encyclical condemns usury because the interest demanded creates an unjust surplus, disrupting the required "strict equality" between what is given and returned. A scale resting in perfect equilibrium is the most potent and recognizable subject to visualize this principle of justice and intrinsic fairness without resorting to abstraction or a collection of complex symbols.
