# Render: Quod Nunquam

## Why

I chose a shepherd’s staff (a crozier) to represent *Quod Nunquam* because it is the quintessential symbol of the pastoral office and spiritual authority which Pope Pius IX is defending. The encyclical explicitly denounces the secular laws that attempt to remove and imprison bishops, thus striking at the authority symbolized by this object. By depicting the crozier as standing firm despite suffering (implied by the crown of thorns and heavy metal), I reject abstraction in favor of a universally recognized religious symbol that directly embodies the concept of legitimate ecclesiastical authority under siege.
