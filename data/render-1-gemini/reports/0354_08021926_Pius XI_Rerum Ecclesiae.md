# Render: Rerum Ecclesiae

## Why

To represent Pope Pius XI's *Rerum Ecclesiae*, which prioritizes forming indigenous clergy and sustainable local churches over foreign dependency, I chose a single, recognizable sapling taking root in native soil. A young tree embodies the organic, living nature of a nascent church that must establish itself from within its own environment to become robust and self-sustaining. An abstract concept or a mere collection of symbols would fail to capture the encyclical's visceral emphasis on internal growth, the overcoming of environmental barriers, and the shift from fragile mission to enduring, rooted ecclesial life, which the image of a sapling naturally and powerful expresses.
