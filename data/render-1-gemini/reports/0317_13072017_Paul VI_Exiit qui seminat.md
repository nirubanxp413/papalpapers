# Render: Exiit qui seminat

## Why

*Exiit qui seminat* clarifies the Franciscan commitment to "evangelical poverty," defined specifically as the total renunciation of property and ownership, allowing the friars only the simple, necessary "use of fact" (*usus facti*) of items. The rule was identified as following Christ's example. To embody this, I rejected abstract symbols and selected a pair of **sandals**, which represent the barest necessity for apostolic movement and pilgrimage, fitting for those who own nothing and must "use" the simple road to follow Christ. The chosen sculpture is a weathered pair of sandals made from dark, distressed bronze, showing significant wear, cast upon a simple, rugged stone base, illustrating the cost and authenticity of this *usus facti* and Christ-like life.
