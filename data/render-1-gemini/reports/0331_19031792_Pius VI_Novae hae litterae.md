# Render: Novae hae litterae

## Why

To capture Pius VI’s two-part message in *Novae hae litterae*—joy for the steadfast and a final, ninety-day warning for the schismatic clergy before canonical penalties—I have chosen a large, liturgical **Censer (Thurible)** as the single recognizable object. A censer is instantly identifiable as a sacred object associated with worship, and it powerfully embodies the encyclical's dual theme: the pleasant, fragrant incense symbolizes the faith and prayers of the steadfast *legitimate* clergy praised by the Pope, while the red-hot embers inside represent the impending temporal and spiritual judgment facing the *intruded* clergy who reject the warning. A censer was selected over a pastoral staff or miter because it dramatically synthesizes the paradoxical message of grace and impending, fiery canonical judgment without requiring abstract symbols.
