# Render: Mirabile Illud

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this sculpture because it concretely symbolizes the core prescription of *Mirabile Illud*: organized communal prayer for peace. The encyclical calls for a worldwide supplication on the Feast of the Immaculate Conception, explicitly stating that true peace requires divine intervention through prayer, surpassing human capacity. The monstrance is the quintessential vessel for Eucharistic adoration and communal supplication in Catholic devotion, perfectly embodying this spiritual appeal. I rejected abstraction because it cannot communicate the specific, devotional nature of the pope's instruction or capture the immediate legibility required by the rubric; the monstrance is universally recognizable in a religious context.
