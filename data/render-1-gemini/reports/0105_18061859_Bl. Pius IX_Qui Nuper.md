# Render: Qui Nuper

## Why

I chose a papal crown (the tiara) resting upon a foundation stone as the single recognizable subject. *Qui Nuper* concerns the revolt in the Papal States and Pius IX's assertion that temporal power—the "foundation stone" of his civil authority—is indispensable for the free exercise of his spiritual authority (the tiara). The image of the tiara needing its physical support embodies the core argument better than abstract shapes or figures of intercession, immediately symbolizing the union of spiritual and temporal rule that the encyclical defends.

I have generated a high-end 3D render of a papal tiara anchored upon a rough stone foundation to symbolize the core themes of *Qui Nuper*. The tiara represents the spiritual office, while the heavy stone base serves as a metaphor for the temporal power that the Pope defends in this encyclical. The composition is dead-center with dramatic studio lighting and a transparent background.
