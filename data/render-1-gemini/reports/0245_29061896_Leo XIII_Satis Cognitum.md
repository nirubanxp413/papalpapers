# Render: Satis Cognitum

## Why

*Satis Cognitum* argues that Christ founded a single, visible, hierarchical Church, a unified body built upon the unique authority of Peter as the necessary center of unity. I chose the **petrine key** (specifically, a large, ornate liturgical key) because it is an instantly recognizable, concrete symbol of the unique authority entrusted to Peter for binding and loosing, which Leo XIII asserts passes unbroken to the Roman Pontiffs. A single key avoids abstract concepts by grounding the theological argument in a physical object that signifies access, governance, and the foundational structure of the Church as a unified entity rather than a loose collection.
