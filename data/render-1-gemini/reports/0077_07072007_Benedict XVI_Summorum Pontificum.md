# Render: Summorum Pontificum

## Why

For *Summorum Pontificum*, which affirms the equal legitimacy of the 1962 Missal alongside the modern form, I chose the **double-faced Roman Janus herm** as the sculptural subject. A traditional herm, typically a stone pillar with a carved head, is adapted here to feature two distinct faces looking in opposite directions on a single neck. This classical, recognizable structure perfectly embodies Benedict XVI's central argument: that the ancient and newer expressions of the Roman Rite do not represent competing rites but are, instead, *two lawful expressions* of the *single* Roman Rite. The single column unified below two faces, facing both the liturgical past and present, makes this profound theological duality concrete and instantly legible, avoiding the need for abstract geometry or a multi-object scene.
