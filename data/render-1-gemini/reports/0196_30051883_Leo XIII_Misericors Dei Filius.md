# Render: Misericors Dei Filius

## Why

I chose a highly simplified but explicitly marked Rule book, represented by a recognizable liturgical text resting on a stylized plinth, as the single recognizable subject. This form directly translates the encyclical’s function: a legislative document issuing a *revised Rule* (the book) that acts as a practical spiritual tool (the sacred, readable object). The book is recognizable, grounded, and immediately communicates the concept of a rule of life or law. A single cross on the cover ensures liturgical legibility without text or abstract symbols. The book itself, rendered with physical texture, is chosen over abstract geometry because the encyclical focuses on a tangible set of rules for a visible, active order in the world, not abstract spiritual concepts. It represents a concrete tool for spiritual life made accessible to many.
