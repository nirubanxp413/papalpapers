# Render: Rerum Novarum

## Why

A worker's hammer was chosen as the singular, recognizable subject to embody *Rerum Novarum*. It is universally identified with manual labor, directly addressing the "hardheartedness of employers" and the difficult conditions of the industrial proletariat the document champions. This concrete object allows us to depict the text's primary concern with the dignity of labor, fair wages, and the protection of workers' associations (traditionally organized by trade) through an everyday, recognizable form rather than resorting to abstract symbolism. Abstraction was rejected because it would fail to immediately and clearly communicate the encyclical's visceral and specific focus on the material realities and human rights of the working class.
