# Render: Cum Multa

## Why

The core conflict of *Cum Multa* is the damaging division caused by political parties tearing apart Catholic unity in Spain. Pope Leo XIII demands that Catholics unite in faith under their bishops, regardless of their political disagreements. The episcopal mitre represents this unifying spiritual authority, which must transcend temporal political factions. A single mitre, rendered in white (representing purity and the faith) and gold (symbolizing spiritual authority and unity), stands as the solitary object under which political differences must be reconciled and transcended. This specific choice rejects abstract representations of unity in favor of a recognizable, sacred object of authority and ecclesiastical order.
