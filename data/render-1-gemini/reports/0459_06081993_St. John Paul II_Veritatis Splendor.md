# Render: Veritatis Splendor

## Why

I chose an ancient, stylized lighthouse to represent *Veritatis Splendor*. This subject translates the encyclical’s theme of "the splendor of truth" as a beacon that guides, rather than constrains, human freedom. The lighthouse embodies the inseparable connection between genuine freedom and moral law, acting as the fixed source of objective truth that enables safe passage. An abstract form was rejected because the document deals with practical moral choices, requiring a concrete, recognizable structure like a lighthouse to symbolize the tangible guidance provided by Catholic moral teaching amidst confusion.
