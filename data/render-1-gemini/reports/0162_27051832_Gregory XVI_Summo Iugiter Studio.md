# Render: Summo Iugiter Studio

## Why

We chose a heavy iron padlock as the sculptural subject to represent *Summo Iugiter Studio*, as it symbolizes the Pope’s binding reaffirmation of Catholic unity and the spiritual boundaries governing marriage. The padlock’s keyhole is fused shut with red wax and a clear cross seal, embodying the rigid prohibition against illicit mixed marriages and the "no salvation outside the Church" doctrine. This single, recognizable object directly visualizes Gregory XVI's binding theological restriction, offering a powerful, tangible interpretation over an unreadable abstract form.
