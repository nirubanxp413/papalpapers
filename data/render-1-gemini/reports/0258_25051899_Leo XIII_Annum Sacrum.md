# Render: Annum Sacrum

## Why

The choice of the Sacred Heart of Jesus is immediate and essential, as *Annum Sacrum* is the foundational document that explicitly consecrates the entire human race to the Sacred Heart, presenting Christ’s sovereign power and redeeming love as the central remedy for societal ills. The image of the heart—especially the heart wounded, crowned with thorns, and radiant with light—is the most recognizable religious symbol and devotional object central to the document’s message. This form was selected because it perfectly embodies the encyclical's theological claims (Christ’s sovereignty and redemption) and its practical action (universal consecration) within a single, powerful, and universally understood sacred symbol, far exceeding the impact of any abstract representation.

This sculptural form, representing the Sacred Heart of Jesus, translates the message of *Annum Sacrum* into a powerful and recognizable symbol. The choice aligns with the rubric by anchoring the abstract concept of universal consecration to the heart of Catholic devotional life. I will pass this design to the creative team to begin fabrication on the polished gold and carnelian stone components.
