# Render: Providentissimus Deus

## Why

A shield was selected as the singular recognizable subject to represent *Providentissimus Deus*, embodying its message of robust theological defense. The encyclical calls upon Biblical scholars and theologians to use disciplined academic methods as armor against the aggressive critique of Rationalism, preserving the doctrine of plenary inspiration. A stylized helmet on the shield specifically references the "helmet of salvation" from Ephesians 6:17, underscoring the spiritual and protective nature of this intellectual effort. Abstract geometry was rejected because it fails to capture the dynamic tension between Rationalism's attack and the Church’s defensive engagement defined in the text.
