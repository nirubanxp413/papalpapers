# Render: Caritas in veritate

## Why

A compass has been chosen as the single recognizable subject for *Caritas in veritate*. Benedict XVI’s encyclical argues that global development has lost its ethical direction, emphasizing that 'charity in truth' is the necessary orientation for authentic human progress. The compass, as a fundamental navigational tool, perfectly embodies this need for moral and spiritual guidance amid complex global systems. A purely abstract form would fail to communicate this specific, urgent need for redirection and would become dependent on explanation, violating the primary requirement of immediate legibility and concrete representation.
