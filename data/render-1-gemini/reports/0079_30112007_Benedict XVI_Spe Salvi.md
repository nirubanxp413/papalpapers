# Render: Spe Salvi

## Why

A single, monumental anchor is the ideal sculptural form for *Spe Salvi*. While other symbols might touch on parts of Benedict XVI's text, the anchor is the definitive Christian emblem of hope, representing spiritual stability and certainty in the face of the world's turbulence, directly counteracting vague optimism or individualistic desire. Its historical rooting in the early Church aligns perfectly with the encyclical's theological grounding, ensuring immediate legibility and complete rejection of the abstract while embodying a rugged, performative faith.
