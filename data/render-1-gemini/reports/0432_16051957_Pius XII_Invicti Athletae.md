# Render: Invicti Athletae

## Why

We chose a highly stylized *palisade shield* as the single recognizable subject to embody Pius XII’s encyclical *Invicti Athletae*. This choice immediately captures the two primary themes: the strength, endurance, and defensive posture required of "unconquered athletes" (Invicti Athletae) of the faith, and the specific image of Poland as "the outer bastion of Christianity" defending against a perceived secularizing or hostile environment. The form is a recognizable defensive tool—not abstract geometry—standing alone to symbolize resilience and the boundary of belief, avoiding the complexity of a battlefield or multi-figure tableau.
