# Render: Il trionfo

## Why

A broken chain with a papal cross attached was chosen because it perfectly captures the encyclical’s core message of Pius VII's liberation after five years of captivity. The chain visually represents his "violent" imprisonment, while the cross signifies the triumph of divine mercy over "human arrogance" that held him, rather than an abstract concept. This single, recognizable object embodies the return of legitimate authority and pastoral care that the Pope addresses to his "beloved subjects," celebrating his freedom without resorting to complex, abstract geometry or multi-object scenes.
