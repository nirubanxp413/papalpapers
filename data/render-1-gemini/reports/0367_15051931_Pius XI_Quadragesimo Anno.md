# Render: Quadragesimo Anno

## Why

A single, grand, interconnected archway was selected as the most suitable sculptural form for *Quadragesimo Anno*. This form physically and visibly constructs a unified whole by joining separate, specialized structural elements (piers and archstones) at a central point (the keystone), perfectly embodying the encyclical’s core proposal for vocational/corporatist orders where different social and economic functions cooperate under a common goal. This archway represents "subsidiarity" by showing lower structures (piers) that support and integrate, and "social charity" by the keystone that binds the entire edifice together, directly contrasting the fragmentation of both individualism and class struggle. An abstract sculpture was rejected because the encyclical requires a solution based on clear, functional, and tangible relationships, demanding an easily recognizable symbol of structural integration and cooperative strength that only a single coherent architectural object can provide.
