# Render: Gravissimum supremi

## Why

The most fitting concrete subject for *Gravissimum supremi* is the single figure of a Saint, specifically represented as a wandering, missionary friar. This image rejects abstract ideas like "missionary zeal" by embodying the specific call for actual personnel—like the Jesuits, Dominicans, and the bishops themselves—to travel to spiritually isolated regions. By capturing the figure in motion with a staff and simple habit, the sculpture directly materializes the encyclical's order for personal pastoral action and organized preaching in distant rural and mountainous communities.
