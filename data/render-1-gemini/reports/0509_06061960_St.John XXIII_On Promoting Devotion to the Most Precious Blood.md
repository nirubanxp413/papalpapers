# Render: On Promoting Devotion to the Most Precious Blood

## Why

I chose the chalice as the sculptural subject because it is the preeminent Catholic symbol and container of the Precious Blood, instantly legible to both faithful and casual observers as connected to the Eucharist. While the crucifixion itself embodies the shedding of Blood, the chalice specifically represents the ongoing *devotion* and liturgical celebration that Pope John XXIII calls for. Its single, defined form adheres strictly to the constraint for a concrete, non-abstract object, avoiding multi-part scenes or vague symbology while remaining deeply theological.
