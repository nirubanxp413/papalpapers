# Render: Missale Romanum

## Why

The core of *Missale Romanum* is the reform and reorganization of the Roman Missal, the central text for celebrating the Eucharist. While the document discusses complex historical developments and theological shifts (three-year cycles, new Eucharistic prayers), the ultimate *outcome* is a codified, standardized physical book containing the words and rites of the Mass. Therefore, a single, monumental Missal, combining elements of sacred book and liturgical vessel, is the most direct and recognizable translation, physically anchoring the abstract liturgical changes in the actual object used at the altar.
