# Render: Sapientia Christiana

## Why

I chose a single, recognizable subject: an ancient, forged **iron bell**, specifically one with a cross mounted on top and rough, scarred texture. This form captures the encylical’s theme of Lithuania’s enduring faith through centuries of persecution ("Baptism" of 1387, then modern trials); a bell is a universal call to faith, a marker of identity, and its sound resonates through time, symbolizing the continuous custody of faith across generations. I rejected abstract forms because they would require explanation and would fail to convey the concrete, resilient character of the Lithuanian church’s history and identity as powerfully as this singular, recognizable object that survives adversity.
