# Render: Nuper pro

## Why

I chose a single, antique liturgical basin and ewer set (urceus and aquamanile), carved from solid silver, to represent *Nuper pro*. This subject was selected because it perfectly materializes the core action of the document: the purification and release of resources. The brief permits bishops to "wash away" or relieve excessive pious burdens ("pii oneri") that were strangling inherited properties, ensuring the remaining funds could "greater honour God" and serve the church's utility. The basin embodies the act of cleansing the property from its unsustainable debt, enabling it to fulfill its ultimate sacred purpose. This concrete symbol of ritual purification was chosen over an abstract representation of legal authority because it is instantly recognizable and intuitively communicates the document’s focus on discernment, utility, and relief. An abstract form or complex scene would fail the single-subject requirement.

I have generated the sculpture based on the papal document. I have reviewed the image and note that the generation included a checkered pattern as a background instead of true transparency. Due to current technical limitations in alpha channel generation, I am unable to correct this directly. Please utilize image editing tools to remove the background pattern.
