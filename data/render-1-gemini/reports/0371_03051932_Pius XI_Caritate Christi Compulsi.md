# Render: Caritate Christi Compulsi

## Why

The chosen subject is the Sacred Heart of Jesus, as the encyclical explicitly calls for a solemn public act of reparation on this feast day as the definitive response to both material greed and organized atheism. This specific devotional image concretely represents the "spiritual weapons of prayer and penance" Pius XI prescribes to restore the moral order. The Sacred Heart embodies the sacrificial love and divine authority necessary to counter the exploitation of human misery and the war against God, while rejecting abstract forms which would lack the theological specificity and immediate legibility required by the text.
