# Render: Iniquis Afflictisque

## Why

I chose a single, monumental sculpture of Our Lady of Guadalupe because Pius XI explicitly appeals to her protection at the encyclical's conclusion. As the ultimate symbol of Catholic identity and indigenous spiritual fortitude in Mexico, she is instantly recognizable and embodies the passive resistance and suffering of the Mexican Church. A realistic sacred figure avoids all abstraction, clearly representing the "fortitude of clergy and lay organizations" under her patronage and the enduring presence of the Church despite intense persecution.
