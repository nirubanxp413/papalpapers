# Render: Accedamus cum fiducia

## Why

We chose a heavy, ancient iron key as the single recognizable subject for *Accedamus cum fiducia* because it directly embodies the theme of "full indulgence and remission of sins." A key is universally understood as the object that unlocks freedom or provides access, visually capturing the Pope’s formal conferral of a jubilee-level indulgence that 'unlocks' forgiveness for those who fulfill the spiritual conditions. While the document mentions various acts like prayer, fasting, and alms, the *result* of the indulgence is the spiritual opening or unlocking of mercy, making the key a more potent and legible single subject than an abstract form, which could not clearly convey this definitive action. Abstract geometry was rejected because it cannot communicate the specific, decisive act of spiritual liberation (unlocking) that the papal brief formally grants; a recognizable key makes the theological concept of indulgence immediate and tangible.
