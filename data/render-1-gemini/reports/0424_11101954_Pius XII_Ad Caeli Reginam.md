# Render: Ad Caeli Reginam

## Why

I chose a monumental, jewel-encrusted crown as the single sculptural subject. This object directly and instantly embodies the central action and theological argument of *Ad Caeli Reginam*: Pope Pius XII's institution of the feast of Mary's Queenship and his assertion of her title as Queen. A crown is universally recognized as the symbol of sovereignty, and it concretely captures the encyclical's "coronation" of the Marian Year without relying on abstract concepts of majesty or grace.
