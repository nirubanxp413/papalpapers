# Render: Summa Dei

## Why

A single, grand ceremonial key best encapsulates *Summa Dei*, which commemorates Pope Pius VI's opening and closing of the Holy Doors of the Jubilee and his subsequent worldwide extension of spiritual indulgences. The key is an instantly recognizable symbol of apostolic authority and the spiritual power to grant remission (opening access to grace), making it vastly superior to abstract geometric forms or complex environmental depictions of crowds. This concrete, single subject embodies the central theme of the bull—the exercise of the keys of the kingdom for the global universal benefit of all Christians—with immediate legibility.
