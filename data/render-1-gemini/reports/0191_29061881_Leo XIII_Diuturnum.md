# Render: Diuturnum

## Why

*Diuturnum* argues that all political authority descends directly from God, rather than originating in the people, establishing obedience as a sacred duty. The sculpture must embody this principle of divine delegation to temporal rulers, rejecting abstract forms like pyramids or geometric hierarchies that would fail to capture the active nature of the argument. A scepter, universally recognized as the essential symbol of ruling power, was chosen as the subject, specifically depicted as the temporal "hand of God" reaching down to grasp a sphere representing earthly authority. The verticality and recognizable form of the scepter clearly visualize the top-down nature of authority derived from God, while the hand, a potent symbol of agency and blessing, reinforces the concept that all legitimate rule is an active commission from a higher power.
