# Render: Custodi Di Quella Fede

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a **Fortress-Church on a Rock**. This single, recognizable architectural structure, which fuses the stability of a fortress with the sacred identity of a Church, powerfully captures the document's central theme: defending Italian society and the Catholic faith against a total war by an external enemy. The Fortress-Church embodies the encyclical's call for unity around the papacy, the steadfastness required to resist Masonic incursions, and the creation of a 'bulwark' of distinct Catholic institutions. This concrete subject is immediately legible and was selected over abstraction or complex scenes to clearly communicate the specific, defensive action the Pope commands.
