# Render: Inscrutabili Divinae Sapientiae

## Why

We chose a monumental, stylized set of Papal Keys because *Inscrutabili Divinae Sapientiae* is, at its core, a juridical and sacramental act asserting the Pope’s unique authority to open divine treasures—specifically, the granting of a universal plenary indulgence. A single, recognizable liturgical or devotional object, like a monstrance or chalice, represents a specific sacrament but fails to capture the comprehensive jurisdictional power exercised in the encyclical to "open" grace to the entire global faithful through various conditions. We rejected abstraction because a recognizable symbol ensures the viewer immediately connects the sculpture to tangible Church authority and grace, fulfilling the "legibility first" rule where geometry would only mystify. The massive, golden Keys, representing the binding and loosing authority, best embody this specific theological and administrative action of Pope Clement XIV.

This set of Papal Keys represents *Inscrutabili Divinae Sapientiae* as a concrete exercise of the Pope's binding and loosing power—specifically the granting of a universal plenary indulgence to open divine grace. The monumental, golden form on its base symbolizes the authority asserted in this juridical-sacramental decree. I have rendered it with a high-end, Cinema 4D finish and dramatic, high-contrast lighting on a transparent background to emphasize its solemn, isolated character.
