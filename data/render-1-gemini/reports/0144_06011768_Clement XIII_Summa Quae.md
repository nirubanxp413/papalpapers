# Render: Summa Quae

## Why

A single, monumental liturgical mitre was chosen to symbolize *Summa Quae*. This specific item of episcopal vesture perfectly represents the intended recipients—the archbishops and bishops of Poland—and their sacred identity, which Clement XIII insists they must uphold despite civil pressures. The mitre captures the central conflict: it is a badge of authority that must remain pure, distinct from the civil roles the Pope accuses the bishops of mixing with their sacred office. Choosing an abstract sculpture was rejected because it would fail to provide the immediate legibility required by the rubric; the mitre anchors the theological warning in the recognizable reality of the episcopal vocation.
