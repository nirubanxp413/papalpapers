# Render: Orientalis Ecclesiae

## Why

The chosen sculptural form is an antique, ornate Greek Orthodox-style bishop’s mitre made of aged silver and gold, unified with a Roman Catholic episcopal crozier, symbolizing Pope Pius XII's central theme: the reunion of Eastern Churches with Rome through the example of St. Cyril. This single, recognizable object embodies the synthesis of distinct traditions—Eastern (the mitre) and Latin (the crozier)—brought together in common theological fidelity and obedience to the Apostolic See, which Cyril championed. It avoids abstraction by presenting concrete symbols of episcopal authority and unity, emphasizing that true integration respects historical distinctiveness while sharing the same root.
