# Render: Super soliditate

## Why

To represent Pope Pius VI's definitive assertion of Papal primacy and supreme jurisdiction over and against claims of episcopal equality in *Super soliditate*, a single, monumental, and authoritative object is required: the Papal Tiara, specifically a tri-regnum. This recognizable liturgical object serves as the ultimate symbol of the unique, divinely established authority that is exclusive to the Successor of Peter, not shared equally by all bishops as Eybel argued. By choosing this concrete symbol of universal pastoral authority rather than abstract geometric representations of hierarchy or a complex multi-figure tableau, the sculpture clearly, immediately, and legsibly visually translates the encyclical’s central theological argument into a singular, sacred object isolated on a transparent background.
