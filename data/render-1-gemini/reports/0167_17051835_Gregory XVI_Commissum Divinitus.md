# Render: Commissum Divinitus

## Why

I chose a single, traditional episcopal crosier (or crozier) because it is the quintessential symbol of the Church's pastoral and governing authority, distinct from all secular power. A bishop's crosier directly embodies the concept of "divine institution" and the pastoral mission given by Christ to the apostles—the very authority Gregory XVI defends against secular interference. A crosier is immediately recognizable as a single subject, avoiding abstract geometry, and its singular form clearly conveys the entire concept of ecclesiastical jurisdiction and spiritual governance.
