# Render: Sollicitudo Rei Socialis

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Sollicitudo Rei Socialis* because it perfectly unites Pope John Paul II's core message of spiritual transcendence and human solidarity. The Eucharist, displayed within, represents Christ as the source of authentic development—a development that is only complete when it lifts the whole person as an image of God. The strong rays of the *sol* (sun) form symbolize the outreach and connection required for true solidarity, moving beyond mere economic growth to a unified moral vision. A monstrance is a universally recognized sacred object, avoiding any abstract or multi-object ambiguity, and visually carries the "formula *Opus solidaritatis pax*" through its luminous, centered radiance.
