# Render: Doctrinal Note on Some Aspects of Evangelization

## Why

The 2007 *Note* defends the legitimate, ongoing, and mandatory nature of evangelization against contemporary religious relativism, asserting that sharing the truth of Christ respects human dignity and is necessary for genuine love of neighbor. I chose a monumental, stylized cross made of polished obsidian to represent this central, immutable truth which the Church must continually and clearly uphold. The cross is the irreducible core of the Gospel message that must be proclaimed to all nations; its stark, defined form avoids abstraction, symbolizing the concrete reality of Christ's sacrifice and salvation, a 'rock' of truth that remains relevant and accessible despite pluralism.
