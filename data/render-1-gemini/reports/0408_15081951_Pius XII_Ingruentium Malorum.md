# Render: Ingruentium Malorum

## Why

To capture Pius XII's prescription for the "calamitous conditions" described in *Ingruentium Malorum*—specifically the fractures, conflicts, and persecutions—the Marian Rosary is the singular spiritual "weapon" that can be universally recognized. A detailed, premium sculpture of a complete Rosary, elevated as a sacred object and isolated on a transparent background, fulfills the need for instant legibility while directly embodying the encyclical's central theological solution. Abstract forms, chains, or complex assemblages were rejected in favor of this single, concrete devotional item that visually links prayer, the family, and spiritual victory.
