# Render: Africae Munus

## Why

*Africae Munus* centers on reconciliation, justice, and peace as the theological foundation for healing Africa and making it a "spiritual lung" for the world. I selected a single, stylized olive branch, which is the universal archetypal symbol for peace and reconciliation, specifically mentioned in the text. This recognizable natural form avoids the ambiguity of abstract geometric symbols while embodying the document's central imperative, and its singular focus satisfies the single-subject requirement for a powerful, high-end 3D render.
