# Render: Mysterium Fidei

## Why

I chose a monstrance because it is the quintessential Catholic object associated with Eucharistic adoration and the assertion of the Real Presence outside of Mass. While *Mysterium Fidei* addresses transubstantiation (making the host a viable choice), the monstrance visualizes the *reservation* and public *worship* of the Blessed Sacrament, which Paul VI emphasized as necessary piety following Vatican II confusion. It transforms the text's theological argument into a single, concrete, instantly recognizable object of devotion. I rejected abstract symbols (like conceptual geometry representing 'presence') or multi-object scenes (like a priest celebrating Mass) in favor of this specific, sacred vessel which encapsulates the entire encyclical's focus on the substantial reality of Christ in the Eucharist.
