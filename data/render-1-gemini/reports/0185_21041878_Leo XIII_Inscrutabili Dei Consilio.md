# Render: Inscrutabili Dei Consilio

## Why

I chose a papal crown as the sculptural subject to represent *Inscrutabili Dei Consilio*. The encyclical's central argument is that the world's myriad social, moral, and political evils stem directly from the marginalization of the Church's authority and the rejection of the Apostolic See. Leo XIII calls for a restoration of the Church's influence and temporal sovereignty as the essential remedy for civilization. A monumental, fortified, yet fractured Papal Tiara (Triregnum) perfectly embodies this focus on embattled authority; it is the ultimate symbol of papal sovereignty (spiritual and temporal) that is described as being under heavy attack. I rejected abstraction or a multi-object scene because they cannot convey the specificity of Leo XIII's claim regarding the primary cause and solution—which is explicitly ecclesiastical authority—with the immediate legibility and singular impact required by the prompt.
