# Render: Beneficia Dei

## Why

A ceremonial **papal tiara with two oversized, interlocking keys of St. Peter** is chosen to represent *Beneficia Dei*. This single recognizable liturgical and hierarchical object synthesizes Pope Pius IX's long pontificate: the tiara symbolizes his 25-year "Peter-like" reign, the triple crown structure, and the expansion of the hierarchy (definition of the Immaculate Conception, Vatican Council). The two interlocking keys symbolize the specific claim to Peter's spiritual and temporal authority, a central point of contention as the papacy faced the loss of Rome to a secular power, and the assertion of continued Apostolic power (granting plenary indulgence) despite the physical confinement and suffering the document describes. A recognizable sacred object is chosen over abstract geometry to grounded, legible authority and crisis in concrete Catholic symbolism, directly reflecting the Church's defined structures rather than vague spiritual concepts. The interlocking keys represent the tension of a pope claiming universal authority while experiencing temporal restraint, a central theme of the encyclical.
