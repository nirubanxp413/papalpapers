# Render: Ubi Primum

## Why

For *Ubi Primum*, which calls for public prayers and Episcopal judgment concerning the definition of the Immaculate Conception, I selected a monumental devotional object: a set of oversized, handcrafted silver and gold rosary beads. This single, recognizable subject perfectly embodies the encyclical’s core message by making tangible the *request* for universal prayer and the bishops' collective consensus on Marian devotion. I rejected an abstract representation of sin or purity because a rosary is an immediate, concrete symbol of intercessory prayer and the faithful’s deep-seated devotion, allowing the viewer to grasp the encyclical's focus on structured prayer and Catholic tradition without ambiguity.
