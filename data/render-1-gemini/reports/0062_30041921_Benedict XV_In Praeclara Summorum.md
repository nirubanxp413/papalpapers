# Render: In Praeclara Summorum

## Why

I chose a highly ornate, medieval-style liturgical cross as the single sculptural subject. This object embodies the encyclical's central argument that Dante’s *Divina Commedia* (symbolized here by the highest form of ecclesiastical art and devotion) is inherently and inseparable from Catholic theology (represented by the cross itself and its specific ornamentation, referencing St. Thomas Aquinas). The cross visually asserts the Church's special claim to Dante's legacy over contemporary secular interpretations, which Benedict XV critiques. An abstract form was rejected because the document explicitly links Dante's greatness to a specific, historical, and concrete faith tradition; a recognizable sacred object best materializes this direct theological connection and authority.
