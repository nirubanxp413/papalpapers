# Render: Gaudete In Domino

## Why

We chose a **stylized, single, ascending dove** to represent *Gaudete in Domino*. This choice was made because the dove is universally recognized in Christian tradition as the primary symbol for the Holy Spirit, which Pope Paul VI identifies as the direct and only source of true, authentic Christian joy. The dove's ascending posture symbolizes the joy that lifts the soul from spiritual desolation and boredom toward Communion with God. An abstract form was rejected because the encyclical focuses on a specific, personal, and tangible experience—the joy rooted in the person of Jesus Christ—which requires a concrete, recognizable figure rather than an ambiguous concept.
