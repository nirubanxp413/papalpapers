# Render: Regnans in Excelsis

## Why

I chose a papal tiara for the sculptural form because the primary argument of *Regnans in Excelsis* is the assertion of absolute, divine, and juridical authority over all peoples and kingdoms by the papacy. This triple-crowned headdress is the ultimate single, recognizable symbol of that specific, hierarchical power and is intrinsically linked to the "power of the keys" Pius V claims to wield in deposing Queen Elizabeth I. I rejected abstract forms because they would fail to communicate this specific, concrete assertion of authority and deposition, whereas the papal tiara immediately encapsulates the very "weapons of justice" and juridical sentence the Pope declares.
