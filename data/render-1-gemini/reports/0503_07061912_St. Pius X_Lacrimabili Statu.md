# Render: Lacrimabili Statu

## Why

I chose a shepherd actively removing a branding iron from the hand of a master to express *Lacrimabili Statu* because it captures the active, forceful papal intervention on behalf of the indigenous people. The encyclical is not a passive lament; it is a strong condemnation and a directive to the bishops to protect their flock and stop the specific, physical atrocities described, such as branding and enslavement. The single figure of the Good Shepherd, a recognizable sacred archetype, embodies both the religious authority and the active protection, physically halting the brutal act of branding, symbolizing the condemnation of slavery and trafficking without resorting to abstraction or a cluttered multi-object narrative scene.
