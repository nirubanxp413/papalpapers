# Render: Testem Benevolentiae Nostrae

## Why

I chose an ornate, jewel-encrusted Papal Tiara, resting firmly and singularly, as the subject to represent *Testem Benevolentiae Nostrae*. This concrete, recognizable symbol of universal papal authority and the Chair of Peter perfectly embodies the encyclical's central argument against "Americanism." It stands as a powerful rejection of the idea that Church doctrine or authority could be adapted to local cultures or individual inspiration ("passive virtues"), emphasizing instead the immutable divine deposit and centralized unity of doctrine and government. A complex theological abstract sculpture would fail to convey this definitive reassertion of hierarchical primacy as clearly or immediately.
