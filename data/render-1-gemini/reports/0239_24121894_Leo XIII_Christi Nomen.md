# Render: Christi Nomen

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject for *Christi Nomen* is an ornate, multi-layered metal alms box, or offering chest, specifically modeled in the 19th-century Neo-Gothic style relevant to Leo XIII's era. This recognizable, practical object is selected because the encyclical focuses concretely on the *financial* and organizational resources needed to achieve the Pope’s spiritual goal of Christian unity, particularly with the Eastern Churches. The sculpture embodies the direct link Leo XIII draws between material generosity and the success of the mission, transforming an everyday object of giving into a sacred vessel for supporting the work of the Society for the Propagation of the Faith. An abstract form would fail to capture the pragmatic, transactional nature of the Pope’s urgent appeal for funding.
