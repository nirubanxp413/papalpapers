# Render: Proclamation of St John of Avila and St Hildegarde of Bingen as "Doctors of the Church"

## Why

I chose an ornate, open lantern containing a single, potent flame to represent this homily on the New Evangelization. The lantern is a recognizable, concrete object that immediately signifies bringing light into darkness, perfectly embodying the "urgent pastoral priority" of re-evangelizing baptized Catholics who have "drifted from the faith." It rejects abstraction by utilizing the archetypal symbol of a lamp for faith, personalized here by the intricate, detailed filigree that can subtly allude to the combined contributions of St. John of Avila (priestly formation, scripture) and St. Hildegard of Bingen (creation, prophecy, feminine authority) within a single, unified sacred form.
