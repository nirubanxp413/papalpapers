# Render: Quam Aerumnosa

## Why

A mother and child sculpture is chosen because it universally symbolizes the *materna pietas* or "motherly pity" that Leo XIII expresses for the Italian immigrants. It concretely embodies both their physical vulnerability (the child) and the Church's response of protection and nurture (the mother). Rejecting abstract forms, which might represent only one dimension of their suffering or the proposed structural solutions, this single human figure, steeped in Catholic iconographic tradition, makes the profound human and spiritual dimension of the pastoral crisis immediately legible.
