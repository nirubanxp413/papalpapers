# Render: Regnans in Excelsis

## Why

I chose a papal tiara as the single recognizable subject for *Regnans in Excelsis* because it is the quintessential symbol of the supreme, plenary, and juridical authority of the papacy over both temporal and spiritual domains, which Pope Pius V asserts directly in this bull by excommunicating and deposing Queen Elizabeth I. This concrete, recognizable liturgical object perfectly embodies the claim of universal jurisdiction—possessing the power "to plant and destroy"—without resorting to abstract shapes or multi-object allegories about power dynamics or theological conflict.
