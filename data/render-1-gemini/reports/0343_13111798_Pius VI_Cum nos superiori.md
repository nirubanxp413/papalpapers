# Render: Cum nos superiori

## Why

The choice of a stylized, fortified Papal Tiara (Triregnum) best captures *Cum nos superiori* because the encyclical is not a theological reflection but a pragmatic legal adaptation for an existential crisis. The three crowns symbolize the Pope's universal authority, here depicted as heavily fortified with structural reinforcements and a durable, patinated bronze, emphasizing that while the physical Pope is exiled and Rome plundered, the *office* and the necessary legal mechanisms for succession must remain secure and structurally sound. This concrete object—immediately recognizable as a symbol of the Papacy but modified to look resilient, not ornamental—directly embodies the core argument: the necessary preservation of the Church's continuity and legal authority despite catastrophic external disruption, fulfilling the criteria for a recognizable single subject. A stylized, fortified form is chosen over abstraction to ensure instant readability while conveying the specific theological urgency of the moment.
