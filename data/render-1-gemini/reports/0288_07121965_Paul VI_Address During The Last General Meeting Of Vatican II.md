# Render: Address During The Last General Meeting Of Vatican II

## Why

I chose an oversized, monumental chalice, specifically rendered in a mix of humble, tarnished bronze and polished gold interior, as the single recognizable object. The chalice best embodies Pope Paul VI's message that Vatican II's intense focus on humanity is not a departure from its theocentric mission, but its fulfillment; the chalice, a vessel for the sacred mysteries and fraternal communion (the source of divine love), is simultaneously a physical container rooted in the human reality of eating, drinking, and community service. I rejected abstraction because a clear, physical sacred object like a chalice provides an immediate, universal symbol of connection between God and man that vague geometry cannot achieve. A single figure or complex scene would fail to capture this specific blending of divine grace and human action.
