# Render: Indulgentiarum Doctrina

## Why

To represent *Indulgentiarum Doctrina*, which clarifies that indulgences dispense the Church’s treasury of merits to remit temporal punishment, I chose a single, recognizable **Thurible (Censer)**. It is a powerful liturgical object that is immediately identifiable and represents the faithful's prayers and offerings rising to heaven. It concretely symbolizes the communal sharing of charity and merits within the Mystical Body, which is the theology the document seeks to protect. This choice avoids any abstract geometry or complex scenarios, using a single, legible object to embody the spiritual concepts.
