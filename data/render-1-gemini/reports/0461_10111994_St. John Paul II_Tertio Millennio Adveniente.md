# Render: Tertio Millennio Adveniente

## Why

For *Tertio Millennio Adveniente*, which calls the Church to examine its conscience and prepare for the Great Jubilee of the Year 2000 through a three-year Trinitarian programme, I chose a monumental, polished bronze sculpture of a single, massive **Open Door**. This subject is instantly recognizable, universally understood, and profoundly biblically resonant for a Jubilee: it signifies Christ ("I am the door"), the entrance into the new millennium, the welcome of the Jubilee indulgence, and the threshold for reconciliation. An abstract form would fail to capture this concrete invitation, threshold, and passage, while other symbols like a compass or a book only represent specific aspects (guidance or doctrine) rather than the overarching event of the Jubilee itself, which is a definitive historical moment of opening to grace.
