# Render: Adeo nota

## Why

Adeo Nota addresses a rebellion where both spiritual and temporal authorities were violently overthrown by the very subjects who should have obeyed them. I chose the Broken Scepter because it perfectly captures the fractured nature of both the papal spiritual authority over the clergy and the princely temporal authority over the territory of Avignon. The splintered wood and damaged metal clearly show the active rejection and violence against the established order that Pius VI condemns, while the remaining form asserts the inherent legitimacy and memory of that broken authority. This single, recognizable object embodies the central conflict far more clearly than any abstract representation of power or illegality could.
