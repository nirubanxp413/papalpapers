# Render: Petitionibus Vestris Benignum Impertientes

## Why

The core meaning of *Petitionibus Vestris Benignum Impertientes* is the explicit granting of institutional authority and a unique identity to the University of Salamanca through the legal mechanism of a common seal ("commune sigillum"). A single, recognizable sculptural object that perfectly embodies this act of sealing is a monumental, ceremonial matrix or stamp. While a finished wax impression might be intuitive, the matrix itself represents the *source* of that authority and the tool of authorization granted by the Pope. Choosing an abstract representation of unity or authority would fail the clarity and legibility requirements, whereas this concrete object is immediately recognizable as an ancient implement of legal power.
