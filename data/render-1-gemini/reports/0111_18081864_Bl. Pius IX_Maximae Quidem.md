# Render: Maximae Quidem

## Why

A Bishop’s Mitre was selected because it is the preeminent, universally recognizable symbol of an ordained bishop, the primary audience of *Maximae Quidem*. The encyclical commends and charges the Bavarian bishops to maintain unified episcopal action, sound clerical formation, and defend Church authority, making this single sacred object a perfect encapsulation of their collective responsibility and the document's central command. This concrete liturgical item is chosen over abstract symbols like interconnected circles or geometric shields, ensuring immediate legibility as a symbol of pastoral leadership and teaching authority. The object’s material richness allows for the required luxury render finish while maintaining spiritual gravitas.
