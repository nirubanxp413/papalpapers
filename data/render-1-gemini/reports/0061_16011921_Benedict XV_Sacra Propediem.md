# Render: Sacra Propediem

## Why

The chosen subject is a single recognizable Dominican rosary, made of olive wood beads and featuring a San Damiano crucifix. The rosary is the quintessential devotional item for Franciscan Tertiaries, embodying their call to personal piety, penance, and reflection on the life of Christ through the Franciscan spirit. The use of olive wood, a material central to the Holy Land where St. Francis began his mission, connects the object directly to the order’s charism of poverty and simple living, while the specific San Damiano crucifix clearly identifies the Franciscan dedication to the cross and peace, making it instantly recognizable and symbolically potent without resorting to abstraction.
