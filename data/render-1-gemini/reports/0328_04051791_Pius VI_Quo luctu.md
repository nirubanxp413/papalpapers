# Render: Quo luctu

## Why

I chose an improvised liturgical lantern as the sculptural form, assembled from an aged, battered copper oil lamp housing placed inside a protective cage of heavy, hand-wrought iron bars. This subject is instantly recognizable as a source of light, translating the encyclical's theme of preserving the essential mysteries of faith (the *light*) even when canonical rules must be 'imprisoned' or adapted within crude, emergency constraints. The rough, functional materials highlight the crisis described by Pius VI, while the structure provides a concrete, single recognizable object rather than an abstract geometric symbol, embodying the resilience and adaptation of spiritual practice in a fractured environment.
