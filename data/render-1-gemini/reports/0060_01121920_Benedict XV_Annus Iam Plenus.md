# Render: Annus Iam Plenus

## Why

I chose a single, worn **collection box** because it directly embodies the encyclical’s central and specific demand: a practical collection of alms on the Feast of the Holy Innocents for starving children. This object is instantly recognizable, avoiding any abstract ambiguity, and represents the humble, repeated call for material aid from wealthier regions to their suffering peers. The specific material choice of dark, aged oak with silver bindings makes the object feel like a sacred, historical vessel dedicated to this one specific, concrete act of charity, reflecting the gravity of Benedict XV’s appeal.
