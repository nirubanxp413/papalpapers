# Render: Motu Proprio

## Why

This Motu Proprio reinforces the traditional requirement of a two-thirds majority in all papal elections, including runoffs, reversing a 1996 change that allowed an absolute majority after many deadlocks. A balance scale made of precious liturgical metals is chosen to symbolize this "qualified majority." One pan of the scale is significantly heavier and lower, visibly requiring more weight—the greater 'measure' of consensus—to achieve perfect balance and complete the decisive action of the election. An abstract sculpture was rejected because it cannot immediately communicate the specific *quantity* and *weight* of the two-thirds requirement.
