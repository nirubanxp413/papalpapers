# Render: Ubi lutetiam

## Why

I chose a single, traditional episcopal crosier as the sculptural subject. This object directly embodies the role and authority of the bishops, who are the central figures and recipients of the document’s specific directives. A cross-section analysis reveals its structure, symbolizing the Pope's dissection and evaluation of episcopal faculties and priestly ordinations. Abstraction was rejected in favor of this highly recognizable, concrete symbol, ensuring clarity and immediate legibility of the subject’s meaning.
