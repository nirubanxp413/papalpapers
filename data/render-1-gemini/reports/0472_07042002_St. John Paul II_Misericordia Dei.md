# Render: Misericordia Dei

## Why

I chose a sculpture of "Open Hands Offering a Seedling" to embody *Misericordia Dei*. This form directly captures the encyclical’s themes of the equal dignity and mutual partnership of man and woman, their dynamic, creative vocation as the "feminine genius," and the active, nurturing work of redemption that restores creation. A generic human figure would not specifically convey the collaborative relationship central to the text, nor would an object like a simple cross capture the generative, ongoing work of mercy and love in the world, making this specific, recognizable gesture the only coherent choice.
