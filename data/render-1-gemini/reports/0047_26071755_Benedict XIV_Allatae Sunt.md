# Render: Allatae Sunt

## Why

I chose an ornate episcopal mitre, sculpted from a fusion of materials representing both Eastern and Western traditions, to symbolize the Pope's defense of Oriental rites. The single, unified vestment perfectly embodies the encyclical’s core message: unity in faith does not require the assimilation or erasure of ancient, approved liturgical traditions. An abstract form was rejected because it could not communicate this specific canonical and cultural tension; the recognizable form of the mitre makes the theme instantly legible as a matter of ecclesiastical authority and ritual identity.
