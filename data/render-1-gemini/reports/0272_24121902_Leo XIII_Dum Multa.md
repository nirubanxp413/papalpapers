# Render: Dum Multa

## Why

The most appropriate subject for *Dum Multa*, Pope Leo XIII's condemnation of civil marriage and divorce, is a sculpture of a wedding ring. This recognizable everyday object instantly symbolizes the concept of marriage. The ring, in its form as an unbroken, continuous band, represents the core theological argument of the encyclical: the indissoluble nature of the bond created by God ("What God has joined, let no man divide"), which cannot be undone by civil laws. This choice avoids abstraction by anchoring the complex theological concept in a concrete, universal symbol of union. To reflect the sacramental and divine nature of Christian marriage as defined by the Church, and to signify its superiority over mere civil contracts, the sculpture should be made of a precious, lasting, and sacred material, like gold with embedded symbolic elements that emphasize its religious character.
