# Render: Saepe Venerabiles Fratres

## Why

I chose a shield, specifically the "Scutum Fidei" (Shield of Faith), as it directly translates Pope Pius IX's militaristic exhortation for the faithful to "advance in full phalanx" against attacks. This concrete, recognizable defensive object embodies both the "vivid demonstration of Catholic unity" that protected the Church during the fall of Rome and the unified prayer required for future victory. I rejected an abstract representation of "unity" or "persistence" because only a tangible, defensive tool can capture the defensive solidarity, spiritual battle, and "crushing" of enemies described in the text.
