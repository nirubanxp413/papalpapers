# Render: A Quo Primum

## Why

I chose a medieval boundary marker, specifically a simple stone pillar carved with a distinct dividing cross, as the single recognizable subject. *A Quo Primum* is explicitly about enforcing spatial, social, and economic segregation, commanding the Polish bishops to "revive dormant synodal statutes" and create clear limits where Benedict XIV claims they have eroded. This physical, ancient object embodies the concept of demarcation and restraint—Jews are to be separated but not physically harmed ("not be persecuted or killed")—which abstract forms would fail to communicate. A stone boundary marker is the definitive, historically accurate symbol of a concrete limit being reinforced, satisfying the "Isolation test" by conveying its purpose even on a transparent background.
