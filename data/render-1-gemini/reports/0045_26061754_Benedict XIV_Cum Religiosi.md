# Render: Cum Religiosi

## Why

The chosen subject is a single, large candle being carefully cupped and protected by two large, surrounding hands. This recognizable image directly addresses the core meaning of *Cum Religiosi*: the need to protect, nurture, and pass on the light of basic faith (the candle) amidst the ignorance and spiritual sluggishness (darkness) of the laity. The candle symbolizes the "necessary means" of salvation, and the protective hands represent the bishops' renewed zeal and pastoral effort (as taught by St. Charles Borromeo) to provide catechesis and overcome the negligence of the people. This concrete subject is immediately legible and chosen over abstract concepts to emphasize the specific, tangible, and necessary spiritual instruction that was being neglected and requires renewed pastoral care.
