# Render: Voi Forse Sapete

## Why

I chose a single, powerful hand, positioned with palm open and upward, holding a simple yet clearly defined baptismal shell. This subject directly embodies the encyclical's central message about belonging to the Church. Voi Forse Sapete asserts that "you are the Church," a belonging initiated through Baptism (represented by the shell), which creates the dignity, good fortune, and duty the Pope speaks of. This open hand is a deeply recognizable archetypal character of receiving, offering, and inclusion, extending to separated Christians and even sinners, who are "received" and remain in the fold unless they commit acts of explicit separation. I rejected abstract forms because they would fail to capture the visceral, personal, and universally understood concepts of incorporation and spiritual receiving that this concrete hand and shell symbolize immediately and clearly.
