# Render: Constanti Hungarorum

## Why

The encyclical *Constanti Hungarorum* calls upon the Hungarian hierarchy and faithful to unify and defend their Catholic faith against anti-religious threats and legislation, appealing to Hungary's long history as a Catholic bastion. To represent this defensive and resilient unity, I chose a single, recognizable helmet from a set of medieval plate armor. While not directly a religious object, the helmet symbolizes the 'spiritual armor' Leo XIII implicitly requests, and explicitly evokes the historical defensive victories over invaders (like Tartars and Muslims) that the Pope cites as proof of the strength that flows from faith. A helmet is instantly recognizable, unified, and carries the necessary weight of both protection and heritage without resorting to abstraction.
