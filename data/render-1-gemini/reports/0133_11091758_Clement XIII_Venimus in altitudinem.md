# Render: Venimus in altitudinem

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a traditional anchor, specifically configured with a stout wooden stock and a complex, heavy iron shank, all appearing as though just retrieved from deep waters. This single, instantly recognizable maritime object embodies the "nautical metaphor" of Clement XIII ("venimus in altitudinem"). By selecting a concrete symbol of stability and hope used by early Christians, the sculpture visualizes the pope’s response to "fierce contrary winds": the turn to prayer and the announcement of a spiritual Jubilee, which anchors the Church in God amidst the storm, avoiding abstract representations of anxiety or systems.
