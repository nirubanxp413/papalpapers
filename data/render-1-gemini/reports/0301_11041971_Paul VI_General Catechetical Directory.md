# Render: General Catechetical Directory

## Why

The *General Catechetical Directory* (1971) aims to renew faith transmission by providing a structured, hierarchical framework for teaching Catholic doctrine in a pluralistic world. I chose a detailed model of a Catholic Church **pulpit** (or ambo) because it is the primary architectural and liturgical object specifically designated for "the ministry of the word" and the proclamation of the Gospel, central to catechesis. The pulpit represents the physical and symbolic source from which the structured truths and the Christian message emanate, visually embodying the Directory's focus on structured, faithful, and coherent teaching over abstract, undefined spirituality.
