# Render: In Dominico Agro

## Why

I chose a solitary, intricately gilded bell, as it is a single recognizable liturgical object. This subject captures the encyclical's central theme of restoring unity and a singular, authoritative standard (the Roman Catechism) amidst fracturing multiplicity. Its clear, singular tone represents the unified doctrine that calls all faithful together, overriding competing and divisive voices. An abstract form would fail to immediately convey the idea of authoritative, communal summons and clear instruction in faith and charity.
