# Render: Sacra Virginitas

## Why

I chose a consecrated lily as the single recognizable subject for *Sacra Virginitas* because it is the pre-eminent iconographic symbol of purity, chastity, and consecrated virginity, often associated with the Virgin Mary and saints who embraced celibacy. The inclusion of a delicate, integrated crown emphasizes the positive aspect of consecration—spiritual marriage to Christ as a high calling, rather than just an absence of marriage—fulfilling the central argument of the text. An abstract form was rejected because it could not instantly communicate the concept of sacred purity and spiritual fidelity with the same immediacy and recognized devotion as this concrete symbol.
