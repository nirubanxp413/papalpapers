# Render: Magnum Matrimonii Sacramentum

## Why

I chose a single, open liturgical book as the sculptural subject because it perfectly balances the institutional reality of establishing a Pontifical Institute for academic study with the theological content of marriage and the family. The book, resting on an ornate plinth, instantly communicates academic degrees, canonical provision, and research, while its open pages symbolize the depth of theological reflection on the sacrament and the source material of the Church. Unlike an abstract representation of "study," this concrete object anchors the institutional act in a tangible, sacred form, rejecting abstraction in favor of immediate legibility and sacramental weight.
