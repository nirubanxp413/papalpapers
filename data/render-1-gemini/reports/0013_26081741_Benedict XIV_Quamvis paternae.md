# Render: Quamvis paternae

## Why

A perfectly symmetrical, gilded balance scale represents the *Quamvis paternae* encyclical's central focus on restoring correct canonical justice and ending judicial abuses in ecclesiastical courts. The object visibly embodies the re-establishment of legal standard and moral integrity, where each judge (the *libra*, or balance) must be properly weighted with competence and dignity according to established canonical norms. This singular, universally recognized symbol of justice was selected over abstraction to convey an immediate, legible message of institutional reform and the required balance of practical and doctrinal expertise in the selection of judges.
