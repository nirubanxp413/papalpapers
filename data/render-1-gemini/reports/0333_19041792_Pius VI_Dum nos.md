# Render: Dum nos

## Why

I chose a monumental papal key as the single recognizable subject for *Dum nos* because it materializes the central conflict: the assertion of legitimate sovereignty and divine authority against a secular takeover. While other symbols might evoke suffering, the specific focus of Pius VI’s letter is the re-validation of five centuries of papal possession in Avignon and the Venaissin, declaring the French National Assembly’s annexation invalid and re-establishing canonical order. The key is the definitive archetypal symbol of this binding and loosing authority, embodying both the keys of the kingdom and the keys to a sovereign territory. I rejected abstraction or figures because the key concretely defines the legal and spiritual control being re-asserted in a single, unmissable object.
