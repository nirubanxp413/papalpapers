# Render: Slavorum Apostoli

## Why

A single, monumental liturgical scroll is chosen to represent *Slavorum Apostoli* because it embodies the central theological concepts of the encyclical: authentic evangelization through inculturation and the preservation of Cyrillo-Methodian heritage. Specifically, the scroll represents the development of the Glagolitic alphabet and the translation of scripture into the Old Church Slavonic language, a singular concrete achievement that brought the Gospel into the heart of Slavic culture. It is not abstract but a recognized object of sacred learning and tradition, serving as the literal vehicle for the unity between the Churches of East and West through shared sacred texts. Abstraction was rejected because a specific, recognizable object like a scroll is necessary to convey the legibility and historical grounding that defined the work of Cyril and Methodius, making the concept immediately understandable without explanation.
