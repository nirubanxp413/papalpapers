# Render: Respicientes

## Why

*Respicientes* is Pope Pius IX's direct protest against the final military occupation of Rome and the Papal States in 1870. A single, heavy iron gate, firmly chained and locked, perfectly embodies this historical moment of the Pope being forcibly confined within the Vatican, becoming the self-declared "Prisoner of the Vatican." This form represents the *consequence* of the invasion described in the document—the loss of temporal power and the forced physical limitation of the Papacy. It was chosen over abstract symbols like fragmented authority or general conflict because it provides an immediate, recognizable image of obstruction, restriction, and the firm rejection of the new secular rule, which mirrors the encyclical's tone.
