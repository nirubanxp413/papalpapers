# Render: Caritas in veritate

## Why

I chose a **balance** (a single beam scale, like the Scales of Justice) as the subject. The encyclical *Caritas in veritate* argues that development must be balanced: it cannot just be economic (technical) but must include ethical and spiritual dimensions. The scales embody the need to integrate market, State, and civil society, and to balance "truth" (veritas) and "charity" (caritas), which must always work together. This form was selected over abstraction to provide an immediately identifiable visual anchor for the concept of necessary equilibrium, rejecting an environment or collection of symbols in favor of one unified object of measurement and justice.
