# Render: Redactional Note on the Instruction Universae Ecclesiae

## Why

A single, ornate monstrance, containing the consecrated Host, is the most accurate sculptural representation of this document. The instruction's core purpose is to preserve and regulate the traditional forms of the mass and sacraments, particularly regarding the holy Eucharist. The monstrance directly embodies the focus on proper reverence, liturgical correctness, and the physical presence of the sacred mystery, making the abstract concept of traditional preservation immediately tangible and recognizable. An abstract sculpture would fail to capture the specific liturgical significance and deep theological focus on the Eucharist and structured worship.
