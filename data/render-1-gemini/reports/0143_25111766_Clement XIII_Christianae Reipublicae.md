# Render: Christianae Reipublicae

## Why

A shield is chosen because it represents the essential defensive action Clement XIII demands from the bishops. The encyclical calls for the vigorous suppression of "pestilential" books to *protect* the faithful and the foundations of religion. A single, solid shield immediately and concretely communicates "defense" and "protection," adhering to the single-subject discipline. A book was rejected because the encyclical focuses on the *danger* and *containment* of books rather than the positive value of holy scripture. Abstraction was rejected in favor of this instantly recognizable, powerful defensive object, which serves as a vessel for the encyclical's central argument.
