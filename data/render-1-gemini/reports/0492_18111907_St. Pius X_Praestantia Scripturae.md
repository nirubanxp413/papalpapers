# Render: Praestantia Scripturae

## Why

*Praestantia Scripturae* powerfully asserts the absolute authority and immutability of Biblical interpretation, mandating the removal of "Modernist" deviations from Catholic education and leadership. The shield was chosen because it embodies protection, defense against doctrinal deviation, and the unwavering strength of established truth. Its shape, specifically the traditional papal *scudo* surmounted by the tiara and keys, serves as a direct symbol of this specific, enforced authority, acting as an impenetrable barrier against heterodoxy; it is concrete, defensive, and immediately identifies the source of that unyielding authority, which an abstract form cannot convey.
