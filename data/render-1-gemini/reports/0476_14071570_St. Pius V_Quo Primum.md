# Render: Quo Primum

## Why

I chose a single, open, ornate liturgical codex—a recognizable sacred book—over abstract forms to visualize Quo Primum because the document directly mandates and authorizes one specific, physical Missal (a book of Mass texts) for universal use. A book instantly communicates the definitive textual source and standardized practice that the encyclical established, grounding the abstract concepts of orthodoxy and uniformity in a tangible devotional object. Any abstraction would fail to capture the direct, practical impact of the papal mandate on the liturgical life of the Church. The open book, while a single object, represents the standardized words of the Mass newly stabilized for all generations.
