# Render: Credo Of The People Of God

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this sculpture because it is the preeminent Catholic vessel used specifically to display the Eucharist for adoration, and Pope Paul VI’s "Credo" strongly and insistently emphasizes the dogma of transubstantiation in response to theological experimentation. The monstrance visibly contains the consecrated Host (modeled as a luminous disc) at its center, providing the exact "light felt by so many faithful souls" that the document aims to provide against doctrinal novelty. It rejected abstraction because the encyclical is not about a vague feeling of presence, but a precise, concrete assertion of a defining Catholic dogma that is instantly legible through this ritual object.
