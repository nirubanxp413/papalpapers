# Render: Quod Multum

## Why

I chose a single, fortified Church-Tower as the single recognizable object. It captures *Quod Multum's* central theme of a Catholic Hungary (rooted in its long heritage) that must be fortified against "modern threats" (rationalism, secularism, socialism, etc.). This object embodies the "Church liberties," "clerical formation," and "Catholic schooling" Pope Leo XIII calls for—practical, concrete institutions protecting faith like a fortress protects a city. An abstract form was rejected because it could not legibly express the defensive strength, historical legacy, and institutional structure that the encyclical demands from the Hungarian bishops. A fortress-like church-tower is instantly recognizable as both a sacred space and a defensive stronghold.
