# Render: Quemadmodum nihil

## Why

To encapsulate Benedict XIV's message that prayer—both mental and vocal—is the essential "ascension" to God and "conversation" with Him, the sculpture must depict the human practice of this prayer. I chose a *kneeling figure with bowed head and joined hands*, embodying the internal and external humility and focus required for both mental meditation and vocal prayer as a singular devotional act. This figure represents the actual practitioner of the indulgences described, rejecting abstraction for the concrete reality of the praying subject, who is "summoned" to this posture by the Church, and now serves as a tangible beacon of this necessary spiritual union.
