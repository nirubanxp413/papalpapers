# Render: Optime Noscitis

## Why

I chose a bishop's mitre, specifically an ornate and authoritative 'mitra pretiosa,' to sculpturally represent *Optime Noscitis*. The encyclical is a direct communication from the Pope to the Austrian hierarchy, delivering precise instructions on how they must govern their dioceses under the new Concordat, which secured Church freedom. The mitre is the singular, globally recognized symbol of episcopal authority, governance, and spiritual responsibility within the Catholic tradition. It perfectly encapsulates the subject matter (bishops), the theological meaning (authorized spiritual stewardship), and the practical effect (structured governance of the Church) in a single, concrete object, rejecting any abstract representation of 'order' or 'agreement.'
