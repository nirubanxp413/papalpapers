# Render: Sicut Dudum

## Why

I chose a single, forged-iron broken manacle as the sculptural subject because it is the most recognizable and powerful symbol of direct emancipation demanded by *Sicut Dudum*. While the document discusses theological justifications and ecclesiastical penalties, the practical command is the immediate physical cessation of slavery and the restoration of freedom. This single, concrete object avoids abstract concepts of justice or liberty, presenting the literal state of being set free—a snapped binding—in a form that is instantly legible and requires no explanation.
