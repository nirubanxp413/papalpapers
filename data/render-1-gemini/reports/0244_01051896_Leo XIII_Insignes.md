# Render: Insignes

## Why

I chose a regal crown with a prominent cross, referencing the historic Crown of St. Stephen, as the single recognizable subject. This object embodies the core message of *Insignes* by synthesizing the Hungarian nation (symbolized by the crown itself, representing sovereignty and the 1000-year history) with its essential Catholic identity and obedience to Rome (manifested in the crown's integral cross). A crown immediately communicates temporal authority and national stability, while the cross signifies the religious foundation that Leo XIII argues is inseparable from Hungary's longevity and political well-being, effectively making the crown a "sacred symbol" of this combined identity. I rejected abstraction because a recognizable historical crown provides the necessary clarity and instant legibility required to represent a specific temporal and religious history; an abstract form would fail to convey the critical historical connection between the Hungarian State and the Catholic Church that is central to the encyclical. This concrete object allows for the representation of both political history and religious fidelity in a unified, recognizable form.
