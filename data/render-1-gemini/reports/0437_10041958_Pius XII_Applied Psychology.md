# Render: Applied Psychology

## Why

A scales object was chosen because the encyclical focuses on weighing the moral legitimacy of modern psychological techniques against the unchanging rights and dignity of the human person. It captures the essential action of Pius XII: balanced judgment and the application of higher moral norms to scientific practices, ensuring techniques like testing or narcosynthesis are not used when they violate essential human integrity. An abstract form would fail to communicate this active, decisional quality of weighing and measuring methods against morality.
