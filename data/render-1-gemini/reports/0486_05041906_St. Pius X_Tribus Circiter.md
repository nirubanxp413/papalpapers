# Render: Tribus Circiter

## Why

The core conflict of *Tribus Circiter* is the breakdown of Church authority caused by the Mariavites’ illicit obedience to a woman over legitimate ecclesial structures. I chose a monumental, polished-wood crozier (pastoral staff) to visually embody St. Pius X's assertion of Petrine authority. By focusing on the *one concrete subject* of the crozier, the sculpture immediately translates the encyclical’s disciplinary force into a single, recognized symbol of episcopal oversight, rather than attempting to represent the concepts of obedience, suppression, or heresy through confusing abstract geometry. The heavy, dark wood and stark lighting emphasize the serious, non-negotiable weight of correct governance over unauthorized devotion.
