# Render: Inter caetera

## Why

The choice of a sculpted *Carnival Mask and Lenten Ashes* in a combined form directly captures the conflict at the heart of *Inter caetera*. While Benedict XIV tolerates Carnival, he sharply defines and defends the boundaries of sacred time (Lent) against profane encroachment (masquerade), illustrating the messy transition and clerical disapproval. Rather than abstracting this tension through symbolic geometry or creating a diorama of a Church service, this sculpture renders the literal object of contention—the mask—while immediately integrating its counter-point, the ashes, as its dominant material texture.
