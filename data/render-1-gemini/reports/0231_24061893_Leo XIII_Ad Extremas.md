# Render: Ad Extremas

## Why

I chose a single, rooted **seedling tree** as the sculptural form because it perfectly embodies the central premise of *Ad Extremas*: that the Church in the Indies can only find permanent stability and life by being "rooted" in native clergy. A seedling is a recognizable, concrete object that symbolizes organic growth, future potential, and essential stability, directly addressing Pope Leo XIII’s argument that native pastors are the only "firm foundation." A fully grown tree would imply maturity already achieved; a seedling emphasizes the founding work of the seminaries and the crucial establishment phase of the native Church. Abstraction was rejected because a geometric symbol for growth would be vague and require explanation, whereas the seedling is universally understood as a new, living plant taking hold.
