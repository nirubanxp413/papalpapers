# Render: IN EMINENTI

## Why

The core argument of *In eminenti* is the condemnation and absolute prohibition of secret societies because their oaths, sworn on Scripture, create a dangerous, hidden bond that threatens both the unity of the Church and the stability of the state. These groups require secrecy and a parallel authority, pulling members away from the public, transparent authority of the Church and civil governance. I chose the subject of a monumental, massive padlock for this reason. A padlock is an instantly recognizable everyday object that concretely symbolizes confinement, secrecy, denial of entry, and binding constraint, perfectly encapsulating the 'secret societies' that the Church is 'locking out.' The sculpture, a premium 3D render, will be made of oxidized iron, visually representing the ancient and heavy restrictions imposed, rejecting any abstract shape for this very tangible metaphor of locking down spiritual safety and public order.
