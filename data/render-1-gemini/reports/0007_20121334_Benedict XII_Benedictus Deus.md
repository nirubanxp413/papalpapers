# Render: Benedictus Deus

## Why

A single, monumental *monstrance* has been chosen to visualize *Benedictus Deus*. This liturgical object is specifically designed to display the Sacred Host for adoration and contemplation, which perfectly embodies the encyclical's central definition of the **beatific vision**: the intuitive, face-to-face, uninterrupted seeing of God's essence by the blessed immediately after death. The circular lunette, glowing at the center, symbolizes the immediate presence of Christ (God) seen intuitively, while the expansive sunburst rays radiating outward represent the divine light, glory, and eternal life experienced in that vision. A monstrance is universally recognizable, sacred, and conveys the idea of divine contemplation and eternal adoration far more directly and legibly than abstract forms could. It avoids the depiction of a specific human figure, focusing instead on the essential experience: seeing God.
