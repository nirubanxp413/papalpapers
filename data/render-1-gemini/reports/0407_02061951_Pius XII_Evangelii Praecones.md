# Render: Evangelii Praecones

## Why

The most appropriate single recognizable object to represent *Evangelii Praecones* is a ship. A ship immediately visualizes the "heralds of the Gospel" physically carrying the faith to new lands, a process that Pius XII reviews with statistical detail regarding the expansion of missions. The vessel, particularly an ancient or early modern one, is the primary archetype of missionary travel, encountering new horizons and establishing connections between the existing Church and the native cultures the document emphasizes. The single vessel also encapsulates the urgency of the final warning, symbolizing the bark of the Church navigating a dividing world, rather than an abstract form, a multi-object scene, or a detailed tableau of missionary work.
