# Render: Ancorché antichissimo

## Why

I chose a solitary, highly fortified key as the sculptural form for *Ancorché antichissimo*. This subject represents Pope Pius VI's unwavering defense of legitimate papal sovereignty over Avignon, a territory held for centuries "by treaty and purchase" and confirmed by multiple kings, symbolised by a massive, ancient key. The key's construction—cast in thick bronze and iron, with a handle shaped like fortified ramparts—specifically visualizes his rejection of the French annexation as null, highlighting the "territorial patrimony" he is sworn never to alienate or negotiate. This single, recognizable object of authority and enclosure directly embodies the legal and sacred claims of the Apostolic Chamber over a specific place, rejecting the abstraction of concepts like 'protest' or 'annexation' for a concrete symbol of ownership and exclusion.
