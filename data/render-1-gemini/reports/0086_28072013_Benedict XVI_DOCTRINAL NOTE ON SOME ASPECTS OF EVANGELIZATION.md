# Render: DOCTRINAL NOTE ON SOME ASPECTS OF EVANGELIZATION

## Why

The Note argues that evangelization is the explicit proclamation of Jesus Christ and is a fulfillment, not an infringement, of human freedom, as persons are made for the Truth (Christ). A sculpture of an illuminated lantern best embodies this concept; the lantern provides necessary light (the Gospel) to a person in darkness, fulfilling their fundamental need to see the way (Truth) without coercing them, just as the Note describes evangelization as bringing the liberating truth of Christ. Abstract forms were rejected because they cannot clearly communicate the specific, purposeful, and illuminating nature of proclamation and the clarity of Truth that is central to the Church's missionary mandate.
