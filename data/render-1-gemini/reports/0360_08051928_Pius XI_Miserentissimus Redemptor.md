# Render: Miserentissimus Redemptor

## Why

The core argument of *Miserentissimus Redemptor* demands active expiation and reparation for sins, rather than simple passive devotion to the Sacred Heart. We chose a singular, consecrated liturgical vessel—a golden monstrance—which visually emphasizes the Eucharist and Christ’s sacrificial presence. Within the lunette (the glass center), we placed a realistic Crown of Thorns encircling the consecrated host. The Crown of Thorns is an instantly recognizable symbol of Christ’s suffering and the sins (spikes) of humanity that require atonement. This concrete, tangible object makes the abstract theological concepts of expiation and mystic priesthood legible through a single, complex devotional form that is immediately recognized as a tool for worship, adoration, and penitence.
