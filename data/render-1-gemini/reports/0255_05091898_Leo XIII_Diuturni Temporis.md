# Render: Diuturni Temporis

## Why

The chosen sculptural form is an intricate, over-the-heart Rosary, serving as the definitive, single devotional item that Leo XIII promoted tirelessly throughout his pontificate and culminating in *Diuturni Temporis*. It captures the core theological fit—the efficacy and papal endorsement of this specific prayer—while providing instant recognizability as a sacred object without relying on abstract concepts. This concrete representation isolates the subject, ensuring it remains legible and communicates its spiritual meaning through material beauty and a strong silhouette. An abstract form would fail to communicate the specificity of the Pope's personal devotion and the very "subject" he championed for fifteen years, which this Rosary captures in its purest essence.
