# Render: Quoties animo

## Why

The chosen subject is a single, large balance scale. This form is immediately recognizable and represents the crucial act of weighing the spiritual good of "pious foundations" against the material emergency of the "public debt" and the king's request. It captures the encyclical's central dilemma: the calculated transaction—balancing the value of alienated Church properties and the potential risk of censure against the guaranteed three-percent annual interest payment (guaranteed by the scale's own structure) and the unique circumstances of the "difficult times." The scale is chosen over abstraction as it clearly embodies the quantifiable compromise and the weighing of disparate values.
