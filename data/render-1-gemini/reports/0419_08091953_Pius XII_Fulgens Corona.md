# Render: Fulgens Corona

## Why

I chose a golden crown adorned with intertwined lilies and roses as the single sculptural subject to represent *Fulgens Corona* ("Radiant Crown"). This form directly embodies the encyclical's title and its central theme: the unique spiritual royalty of Mary as Mother of God, specifically celebrating the hundredth anniversary of the Immaculate Conception definition. The crown signifies her Queenship, while the lilies (purity) and roses (love/spiritual perfection) materialize the text's emphasis on her sinless life and the virtues the faithful are called to imitate. An abstract form was rejected because it would fail to immediately convey the established Marian iconography and the radiant, triumphant nature of the 'corona' that Pius XII proclaims as a remedy for the spiritual crisis.
