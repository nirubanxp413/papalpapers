# Render: Second Vatican Council II Closing Speech

## Why

I chose an open door as the sculptural subject to represent the Second Vatican Council II Closing Speech because it perfectly embodies Pope Paul VI's definition of the moment as both an ending and a beginning. The Council (a door that opened the Church to the modern world) had concluded, yet that very opening created the entrance point for the Church to go forth and engage with humanity, which is symbolized by the open doorway. This concrete, recognizable everyday object is a powerful metaphor for connection, universal greeting, and dynamic progress, rejecting abstract geometry in favor of an easily legible symbol of a 'church of the opening.'
