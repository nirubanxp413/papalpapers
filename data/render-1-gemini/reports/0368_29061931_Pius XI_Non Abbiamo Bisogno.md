# Render: Non Abbiamo Bisogno

## Why

I chose a single, idealized youth figure with head slightly raised in spiritual contemplation. This sculpture embodies the Church's foundational argument for its inherent mandate to form souls from childhood, directly challenging the Fascist total state's claimed monopoly over youth (statolatry). The figure's resolute but gentle posture symbolizes the "Catholic Action" movement’s non-political, religious character that was suppressed, and it was chosen over an abstract form (like an unbroken link or a light beam) because it directly represents the human subject at the heart of the conflict: the child or young adult being spiritualized rather than politicized. An abstract sculpture would fail to capture the pastoral urgency and human reality of the crisis that Pius XI addresses.
