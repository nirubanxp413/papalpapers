# Render: Etsi minime

## Why

To embody Pope Benedict XIV’s demand for the vigorous promotion of catechetical instruction and explicit knowledge of faith over vague belief, I have selected a detailed, open catechism as the singular recognizable object. This subject rejects abstraction entirely, presenting the concrete, fundamental tool specified in the document—the Bellarmine catechism—as the source of enlightenment. The sculpture symbolizes the structure, clarity, and authority required for the faithful's salvation, showing the text itself as a durable, necessary vessel of truths that must be actively "opened" and understood.
