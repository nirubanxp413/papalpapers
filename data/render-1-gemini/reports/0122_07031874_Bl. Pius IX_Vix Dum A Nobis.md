# Render: Vix Dum A Nobis

## Why

I chose a solitary bishop’s mitre—specifically, a *mitra pretiosa* or precious mitre—because *Vix Dum A Nobis* is fundamentally a defense of the Church’s inherent, divinely granted spiritual authority. The mitre is the most potent and recognizable symbol of that authority within the hierarchical structure that Pope Pius IX asserts is being usurped by the Austrian government’s proposed laws. A single, isolated, high-end 3D render of this mitre, appearing fortified and inviolable, rejects the ambiguity of abstract symbols like broken chains or geometry in favor of the one concrete object that immediately signifies the sovereign ecclesiastical jurisdiction the Pope is fighting to protect. This choice directly translates the conflict between divine authority and civil interference into a single, legible sacred object.
