# Render: Doctor Mellifluus

## Why

I chose a single, recognizable subject—a golden honeycomb—because the title *Doctor Mellifluus* literally means "Honey-Sweet Doctor," which was St. Bernard's traditional nickname. The honeycomb perfectly encapsulates the dual core of the encyclical: Bernard's "sweet" contemplative love (represented by the natural structure) and the "warmth" of the charity that Pope Pius XII calls for (represented by the golden liquid and light). An abstract form would fail to capture this specific title, and other traditional symbols like a book or saintly figure would emphasize the doctrinal aspect of Bernard's work rather than the essential quality of contemplative "sweetness" and the warmth of social charity that the encyclical prioritizes for modern times.
