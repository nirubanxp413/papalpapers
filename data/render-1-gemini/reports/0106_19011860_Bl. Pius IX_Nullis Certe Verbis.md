# Render: Nullis Certe Verbis

## Why

The core conflict of *Nullis Certe Verbis* is Pope Pius IX's resolute refusal to cede temporal sovereignty over the Papal States in the face of political pressure and military threat. This is a struggle for preservation, integrity, and non-concession. A broken or open object (like a broken chain, open door, or shared bread) would fail to capture the encyclical's central act of defensive refusal. The most fitting single, recognizable subject is an **armored fortress tower** that is actively **sealed, whole, and impregnable**. This form instantly communicates a resolute stance, a closed defensive posture, and the preservation of a sacred enclosure, making it a powerful concrete embodiment of the papacy's refusal to yield its legitimate temporal and spiritual territory.
