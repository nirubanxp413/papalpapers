# Render: Placuit Deo

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a single, unified figure of Christ, depicted as a physical, embodied man. This choice was made because *Placuit Deo* is a direct counter to the dual threats of radical individualism (Pelagianism) and disembodied spirituality (Gnosticism), both of which minimize the essential role of the Incarnation. A sculpture of the embodied Christ directly asserts that salvation is concrete, physical, and achieved *through* the flesh of Jesus in union with the Father and humanity. Abstract forms were rejected because they are incapable of capturing the central mystery of the Incarnation and would inadvertently reinforce the very Gnostic disembodiment the document condemns.
