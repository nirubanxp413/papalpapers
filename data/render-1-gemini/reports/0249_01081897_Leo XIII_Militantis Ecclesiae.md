# Render: Militantis Ecclesiae

## Why

I chose a highly ornate, illuminated book on a sculpted stand for *Militantis Ecclesiae*. This single, recognizable subject perfectly embodies Pope Leo XIII's core argument that faith and reason are not enemies but mutually reinforcing. The open book symbolizes "learned defenders of the faith" and "catechisms," the primary remedies advocated to combat "revolution and looseness of doctrine." I rejected abstraction because it could not communicate the specific, critical role of education and theological clarity; a purely symbolic void or shape would lack the necessary, concrete connection to "learned knowledge" and "religiously infused teaching."
