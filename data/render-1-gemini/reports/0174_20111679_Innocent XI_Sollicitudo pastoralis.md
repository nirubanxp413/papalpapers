# Render: Sollicitudo pastoralis

## Why

I chose a pair of worn Franciscan sandals as the single, instantly recognizable subject for *Sollicitudo pastoralis*. These everyday objects perfectly embody the specific spiritual obligations emphasized by Innocent XI, specifically the requirement that the friars go "unshod" (relying on simple footwear like sandals) and observe extreme poverty and simplicity in dress. The sandals, showing signs of use, concrete the core meaning of retaining the original Franciscan Rule, including the absolute prohibition on handling money, by symbolizing the physical reality of the friars' vow. An abstract sculpture was rejected because it could not clearly express the specific, practical, and visible practices of life—like footwear and simplified clothing—that the Pope specifically enforced to restore the order's original founding spirit.
