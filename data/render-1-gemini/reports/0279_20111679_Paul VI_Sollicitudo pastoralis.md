# Render: Sollicitudo pastoralis

## Why

The encyclical *Sollicitudo pastoralis* confirms the Franciscan Friars Minor Observants' adherence to their original Rule without dispensations, enumerating specific ascetic practices like going without footwear and absolute poverty. A single, humble Franciscan sandal, cast as a precious, non-abstract relic, perfectly embodies this commitment to mandatory poverty and physical austerity. It is a universally recognizable object of religious life that specifically points to the rejected luxuries and strict observances (going barefoot or poorly shod) detailed in the document, capturing the essence of the order's identity and vow of poverty better than any conceptual or multi-figure scene.
