# Render: Divinum Illud Munus

## Why

I chose the figure of the Dove to represent *Divinum Illud Munus* because it is the preeminent and universally recognized symbol for the Holy Ghost, whose nature, work, and worship are the entire subject of Pope Leo XIII’s encyclical. This concrete, archetypal sacred figure instantly communicates the "Divine Gift" without requiring abstract geometric metaphors for grace or spirit, satisfying the primary legibility requirement. By focusing solely on the descending Dove, the sculpture captures the encyclical's central themes: the Holy Ghost as the animator of the Church, the sanctifier of souls, and the perpetual source of divine grace descending upon the faithful.
