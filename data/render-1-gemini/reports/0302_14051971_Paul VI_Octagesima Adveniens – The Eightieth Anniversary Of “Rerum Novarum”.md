# Render: Octagesima Adveniens – The Eightieth Anniversary Of “Rerum Novarum”

## Why

I chose a highly specialized and reinforced pair of **working sandals** to represent *Octagesima Adveniens*. The sandals symbolize the encyclical’s focus on diverse social "walks of life"—urbanization, youth, women, and migrants—all called to action. This choice rejects abstraction by using a concrete, universal object of labor and movement, embodying Paul VI's call for local communities to discern and "walk" their own paths of justice. The form is instantly recognizable and allows for a rich, sculptural visualization of durability and pilgrimage.
