# Render: Quamquam Pluries

## Why

Given *Quamquam Pluries*’ central theme of extending the October Rosary devotion specifically to include the intercessional power of St. Joseph as Patron of the Universal Church and "Patron of all the faithful," a traditional Dominican Rosary is the single most legible devotional object to encapsulate the Pope’s practical, theological, and liturgical request. The Rosary is an instantly recognizable symbol of Catholic prayer, discipline, and Marian devotion. By rendering it as a monumental, high-end sculpture with intricate material detail, it captures the spiritual weight and urgency Leo XIII places on divine intercession in the face of widespread crisis, specifically tethering the new Josephite devotion to the existing Rosary structure he championed throughout his papacy. This concrete object embodies the encyclical’s spiritual remedy far more clearly than any abstract representation of intercession or spiritual warfare.
