# Render: Instruction on the application of the Apostolic Letter Summorum Pontificum of His Holiness Pope Benedict XVI given Motu Proprio (April 30, 2011)

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this instruction because it is the preeminent Catholic object for displaying the sacred reality (the Eucharist) that is central to both forms of the Roman Rite, visualising the document's argument of 'lex orandi, lex credendi' and continuity. It represents the liturgical worship and doctrinal continuity that *Summorum Pontificum* seeks to safeguard by making the traditional liturgy accessible. A monstrance is immediately recognizable as a liturgical vessel, avoiding abstraction while capturing the elevated, sacred quality required, and its central circular window offers a clear focal point suitable for a single-object composition.
