# Render: Pastor Bonus

## Why

The Pope re-proposes the Rosary, which was previously a set of only three sets of "mysteries" (Joyful, Sorrowful, and Glorious). This entire document is defined by his introduction of a new, *fourth* set: the "Mysteries of Light" (Mysteria Lucis), covering Christ’s public ministry. To capture this defining *addition* that *re-interprets* and expands the entire prayer, the sculpture must be a set of Rosary beads that explicitly incorporates four, rather than three, distinct sections. A single, stylized string of four large beads (or sets) of luminous material, separated by one cross, concretizes the entire text’s theological innovation into a single, legible, and non-abstract sacred object. Abstract forms like a prism or open hands were rejected because they do not specifically relate to the Rosary prayer itself. A single rosary with *four* distinct large beads and one crucifix is chosen as the clearest expression.
