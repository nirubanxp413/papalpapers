# Render: Sacrae Disciplinae Leges

## Why

I chose a monstrance—the liturgical vessel used to display the consecrated Eucharistic Host—to embody the meaning of *Sacrae Disciplinae Leges*. This encyclical, as a *Apostolic Constitution*, gave juridical force to the 1983 Code of Canon Law, aligning the Church's laws with the theological insights of Vatican II. The monstrance is the quintessential vessel of *showing* and *displaying* the ultimate source of Church life (the Eucharist), mirroring the function of the Code: to clearly structure, protect, and make visible the spiritual reality and mission of the Church. I rejected abstraction because the encyclical is about defining concrete structures; a monstrance is a highly recognizable, singular object that perfectly symbolizes the "disciplined visibility" of sacred mystery.
