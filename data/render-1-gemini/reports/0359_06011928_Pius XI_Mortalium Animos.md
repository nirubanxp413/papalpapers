# Render: Mortalium Animos

## Why

Pius XI’s *Mortalium Animos* forcefully rejects the "pan-Christian" idea that unity is an ideal to be constructed by a federation of equal denominations. Instead, it asserts that the one true Church of Christ already exists as the visible, hierarchical, and indefectible Roman Catholic Church, and that unity means non-Catholics "returning" to its authority. To embody this, a single, dominant, and non-negotiable object is required, rejecting any suggestion of equal parts or abstract synergy. The papal **Crozier** (or pastoral staff) is the perfect concrete symbol, as it is the tool of authority used by a shepherd to rule and govern his single flock. It makes the claim that Christ’s authority resides in a specific, historical office (the Papacy) and that the Church is defined by this visible hierarchy, not a mystical, invisible, or democratic body. A Crozier is a instantly recognizable, unified object, and avoiding abstract geometry ensures the focus remains on the visible institution and its governance, exactly as the encyclical demands.
