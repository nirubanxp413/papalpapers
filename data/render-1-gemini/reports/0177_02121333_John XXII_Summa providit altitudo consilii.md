# Render: Summa providit altitudo consilii

## Why

A ceremonial wax seal matrix is the single recognizable object that best encapsulates *Summa providit altitudo consilii*. The document solves a precise institutional problem: the university could examine candidates, but no specific official held the *power* to officially seal, authorize, and confer the degrees. Pope John XXII formally grants this specific authorization (the *licentia regendi*) to the Scholasticus of Salamanca. The seal matrix is the tool of institutional authority; it is the physical object that validates, confirms, and officially authorizes the degree, translating the abstract legal grant into a concrete, functional administrative item. It was chosen over abstract symbols of education because it focuses specifically on the *conferring power* described in the text, and is a deeply legible object that requires no surrounding context to communicate its purpose as an instrument of official certification. Abstraction would fail to capture the bureaucratic specificity of vesting a single official with the power to stamp legitimacy onto graduates.
