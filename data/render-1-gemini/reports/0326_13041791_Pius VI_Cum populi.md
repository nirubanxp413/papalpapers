# Render: Cum populi

## Why

I chose a shepherd’s crook (crozier) as the single recognizable subject for *Cum populi*. This devotional and archetypal object directly symbolizes the pastoral authority and responsibility of Bishop Giovanni, the document’s primary recipient. The crook embodies the central tension: the risk of the "flock" (the laity) being deceived by the "Civil Constitution," contrasted with the Bishop's duty to protect the ancient Church order, guided by the explicit directives and blessing from the Pope. This concrete object captures the themes of authority, tradition, correction, and care better than an abstract form, as it is a widely understood symbol of a bishop’s role and relationship to the faithful.
