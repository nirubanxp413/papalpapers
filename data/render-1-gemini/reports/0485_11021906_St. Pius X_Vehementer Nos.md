# Render: Vehementer Nos

## Why

I chose a single, massive **chain link** for this sculpture. *Vehementer Nos* is a document of profound condemnation and sorrow over the French State's unilateral shattering of the Concordat, an act Pius X views as a grave injustice and a rupture of the necessary connection between Church and State. A chain link perfectly embodies the broken bond (the ruptured Concordat) and the unjust constraint the new law places upon the Church, while remaining an instantly recognizable, powerful, and single physical object that does not rely on abstract geometry.
