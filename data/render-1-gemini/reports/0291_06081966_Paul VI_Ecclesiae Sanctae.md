# Render: Ecclesiae Sanctae

## Why

A single, grand, organizational Key is the most precise symbol for *Ecclesiae Sanctae*. Paul VI’s document is inherently legislative and structural; it "opens" new goals (the key turning the lock) by providing the practical mechanisms and authority (the norms) necessary to implement the Council's vision. The key rejects abstraction by offering an immediately legible object of transition and utility, capturing the practical "how-to" of Vatican II's canonical and missionary renewal without resorting to vague symbols or complex scenes.
