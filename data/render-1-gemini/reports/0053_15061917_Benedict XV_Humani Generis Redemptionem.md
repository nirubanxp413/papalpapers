# Render: Humani Generis Redemptionem

## Why

To embody Pope Benedict XV's diagnose of a spiritual crisis caused by ineffective, ill-prepared, and vain preachers, I have chosen **a tarnished bronze sounding trumpet** as the singular sculptural subject. This object directly symbolizes the *act* of preaching—the public proclamation of the Word—yet its corroded, cracked, and mute state represents the *failure* of the contemporary messengers to sound a clear, sacred, and efficacious warning. This single, recognizable object powerfully captures the central theme of a vital, God-given instrument rendered useless by human negligence and improper use, far more effectively than an abstract form, complex scene, or figure of St. Paul.

I have selected the tarnished bronze sounding trumpet as the sculptural form to represent *Humani Generis Redemptionem*.

This single, recognizable object embodies the act of preaching (the sounding of the Word) as a specific tool or instrument. By presenting this instrument as cracked, heavily corroded with verdigris, and effectively mute, the sculpture visualizes the encyclical's central argument: that the Word of God remains powerful, but the human messengers (the instruments) are currently inadequate, tarnished by wrong intentions and poor methods, thus failing to sound a clear alarm to society.
