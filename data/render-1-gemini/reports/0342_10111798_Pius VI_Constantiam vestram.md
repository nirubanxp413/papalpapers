# Render: Constantiam vestram

## Why

I chose a gold thurible, used in liturgy to spread incense, to represent Pius VI’s reflection on how persecution can actually purify and glorify the Church. This single, recognizable sacred object perfectly embodies the theological argument: incense represents prayers rising to God and the sanctification of the Church, but it only creates fragrant, ascending smoke after being tested by burning charcoal within. The process symbolizes the Church (the gold vessel) growing in holiness and spiritual beauty specifically through the fiery tribulations and suffering mentioned by the Pope. Choosing an abstract form or a scene of conflict would have obscured the central message of purposeful purification and eventual spiritual glory resulting from persecution, which the functional and symbolic thurible captures directly and elegantly.
