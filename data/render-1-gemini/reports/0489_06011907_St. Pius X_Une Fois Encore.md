# Render: Une Fois Encore

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Une Fois Encore* to embody the Church's resistance to the 1907 separation law as a spiritual defense, not merely a political dispute. It represents the central act of worship—the Eucharist—that the state sought to regulate through 'associations cultuelles.' The monstrance signifies the Church's insistence on maintaining its hierarchical structure and control over the sacred, emphasizing that the conflict was fundamentally 'a war against the supernatural itself.' A monstrance is universally recognized, concrete, and directly related to the 'cult' (worship) in question, fulfilling all criteria for theological fit and immediate legibility over abstract or multi-object concepts.
