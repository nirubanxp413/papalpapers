# Render: Depuis Le Jour

## Why

I chose a bishop's mitre as the sculptural subject to represent *Depuis Le Jour* because it is the quintessential, universally recognized symbol of episcopal authority and leadership within the Catholic hierarchy. The encyclical is addressed specifically to the archbishops and bishops of France, tasking them with overseeing priestly formation and ensuring priestly obedience and sanctity. A beautifully crafted mitre, isolated and elevated, symbolizes the authoritative, discerning presence and guiding responsibility of the bishop in all matters of clerical life, from the seminary to pastoral work, aligning perfectly with the document’s central theme and structure. Abstract forms were rejected because they lack this immediate, undeniable connection to a specific ecclesial office and its associated authority.
