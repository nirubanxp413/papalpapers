# Render: Last Will and Testament of Pope Paul VI

## Why

A single, humble **monk’s sandal** best translates Pope Paul VI's testament, embodying his final themes of personal humility, simple poverty, and faithful pilgrimage without resorting to abstract geometry. The sandal is an instantly recognizable devotional item that signifies the Christian life as a journey toward God and neighbor, mirroring his call to the Church for ecumenism and engagement with the world. By selecting this specific, worn, leather footwear over an abstract concept of 'journey,' the sculpture remains concrete and immediate, emphasizing the tactile, physical reality of the spiritual life he prioritized. This form perfectly captures his paradoxical role: a powerful Pope submitting in profound, simple service.
