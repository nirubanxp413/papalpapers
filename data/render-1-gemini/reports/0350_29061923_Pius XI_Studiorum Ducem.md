# Render: Studiorum Ducem

## Why

The subject chosen is **Thomas Aquinas as a haloed, angelic doctor, holding a radiant, open book and a pen.** This form was selected because it captures the encyclical's central theme: the *inseparable union* of Aquinas's personal sanctity (represented by the angelic form and halo, or *doctor angelicus*) and the authority of his intellectual work (the open book and pen). This concrete figure, immediately recognizable as a sacred authority and scholar, perfectly embodies Pius XI’s argument that holiness disposes the intellect to truth and truth perfectPerfects virtue. An abstract form could not communicate this specific, historical, and lived unity of character and doctrine, while this iconic representation makes the argument instantly legible.
