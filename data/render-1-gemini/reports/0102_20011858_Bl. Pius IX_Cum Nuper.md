# Render: Cum Nuper

## Why

A single, monumental oil lamp is chosen as the sculpture for *Cum Nuper*, symbolizing the Pope's urgent call to clerical vigilance and renewal after the 1857 earthquakes. This everyday object represents the "burning light" of piety, learning, and diligent pastoral care required of the clergy, counteracting the corruption and laxity criticized in the encyclical. This recognizable form avoids abstraction and clearly embodies the spiritual directive to rekindle the fire of faith in the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies through proper pastoral and educational reforms.
