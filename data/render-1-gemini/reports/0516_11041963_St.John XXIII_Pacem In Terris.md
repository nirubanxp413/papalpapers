# Render: Pacem In Terris

## Why

For *Pacem in Terris* (Peace on Earth), I have chosen **a dove holding a small olive branch**, sculpted as a single unified subject. This specific form was selected over abstraction because it is the preeminent and globally recognized Judeo-Christian symbol for peace and divine covenant, perfectly aligning with the encyclical's call for order through natural law. The dove embodies the central argument for universal peace and the specific appeal for disarmament, directly linking the text's theological principles to an intuitive, sacred archetype that is immediately legible as an agent of peace without requiring explanation.
