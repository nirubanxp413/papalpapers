# Render: Pastoralis Officii

## Why

*Pastoralis Officii* addresses dueling as a sinful practice that violates divine and natural law by exposing oneself and others to death without just cause, ultimately condemning private combat and refuting social definitions of honor. We selected a high-ranking military helmet from the late 19th century—specifically a German-style *Pickelhaube*—as the single recognizable subject to represent the social and military structures Leo XIII was challenging. Rather than an abstract form symbolizing conflict, this object concrete embodies the specific historical context, the false sense of 'honor' the Pope refutes, and the military culture that often facilitated dueling, now presented as a sacred artifact subject to divine judgment.
