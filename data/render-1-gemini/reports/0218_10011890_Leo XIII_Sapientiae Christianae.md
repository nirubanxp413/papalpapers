# Render: Sapientiae Christianae

## Why

*Sapientiae Christianae* addresses the essential role of faith and the Church in governing not just private life but the public, social, and political realms, emphasizing the absolute primacy of divine law and ecclesiastical authority over civil law in cases of conflict. A single, stylized, and monumental set of **Keys** best embodies this theme: not just the spiritual authority over the soul (the key of heaven), but the ultimate authority over all temporal domains and institutions. The single object—a set of two intertwined keys (Papal Keys)—clearly represents the binding and loosing authority Leo XIII defines, emphasizing that it is a power, not an abstract principle.
