# Render: Decet Quam Maxime

## Why

I chose a single, open, clerical hand, holding a pile of specific historical coins, made entirely of unyielding, polished dark metal. The hand represents the ecclesiastical officials (bishops down to notaries) whose extraction of fees is the central subject of *Decet Quam Maxime*. The gesture is both a demand for payment and the moment of collection, embodying the central conflict: the inappropriate mixture of spiritual authority with monetary gain (simony). This concrete object immediately communicates the encyclical's administrative and moral core without resorting to abstract geometry; a simple scale might represent general justice, but the hand directly captures the action of avarice and the specific historical context of fee collection prohibited by Clement XIV. A transparent background ensures the sculpture is seen as a standalone object.
