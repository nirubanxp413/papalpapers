# Render: Magni Nobis

## Why

I chose a papal mitre as the single recognizable subject to represent *Magni Nobis* because it concretely embodies the document’s focus on establishing and endorsing a new ecclesiastical institution—The Catholic University of America. The mitre signifies the authority Leo XIII formally grants to the American bishops, specifically the Archbishop of Baltimore as chancellor, over the new center of learning. It represents the official approval and the oversight structure mandated by the text, grounding the complex concepts of papal decrees, university statutes, and academic degrees in one universally legible symbol of the Church's established hierarchy and approval. This choice avoids abstraction by anchoring the text's legalistic authority in a tangible, iconic object of pastoral governance.
