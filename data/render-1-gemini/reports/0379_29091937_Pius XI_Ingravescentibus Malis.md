# Render: Ingravescentibus Malis

## Why

Against the three named political and atheistic evils of the age—Communism, Totalitarianism, and Militant Atheism—Pope Pius XI identifies only one effective remedy and weapon: Marian devotion through the Rosary. A large-scale sculpture of a complete Rosary best embodies this specific and immediate "instrument" of spiritual warfare and renewal. I rejected abstract forms as the Pope demands a concrete return to established devotional practices, and I rejected figures to focus entirely on the sacred object the Pope uniquely commends for its power and accessibility.
