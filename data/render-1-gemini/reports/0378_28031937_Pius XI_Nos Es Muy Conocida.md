# Render: Nos Es Muy Conocida

## Why

To capture Pius XI’s *Nos Es Muy Conocida*, which provides a clear program of action—priestly renewal and Catholic Action—amidst severe persecution in Mexico, the sculpture must balance pastoral supervision with active, structured defense. A lone shepherd or simple cross is too general; the encyclical is about a specific *movement* organized by the hierarchy. The chosen form is a highly stylized, fortified Episcopal Crozier: its recognized shape embodies the Bishop's authority to guide the flock (the two pillars of clergy and lay formation), while its hook is transformed into a segmented, architectural buttress or shield. This single, recognizable object visualizes the encyclical’s demand that the spiritual end always supervises the organized social/political resistance. A single subject is chosen to reject multi-object dioramas, ensuring immediate legibility and focus on a concrete instrument of authority. Abstraction is avoided as it cannot communicate the urgent, practical instruction and ecclesial structure vital to the document's message.
