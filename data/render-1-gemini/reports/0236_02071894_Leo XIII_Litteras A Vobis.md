# Render: Litteras A Vobis

## Why

I chose a single, monolithic mitre and crozier unified into one sculptural form. This subject is an universally recognizable symbol of episcopal authority, specifically representing the core purpose of Leo XIII’s encyclical: to establish a comprehensive pastoral agenda for the newly expanded Brazilian episcopal hierarchy. It directly embodies the roles of the bishop in sanctifying, teaching, and governing, while its singular form expresses the unity and collaboration Leo requested from the Brazilian bishops, thus translating the encyclical’s organizational and spiritual mandate into a single concrete, legible subject that avoids abstract geometry.
