# Render: Gravissimas

## Why

The core conflict in *Gravissimas* is the civil government's attempt to dissolve or strip the fundamental elements of religious congregations, specifically their spiritual vows. Pope Leo XIII insists these spiritual bonds are essential and cannot be abolished by secular law, likening them to a shield that defends the spiritual life and unity of the faithful against attacks. A sculpture of an ornate, resilient religious shield, incorporating the cross and monastic symbols, concretely embodies this idea of spiritual protection and resistance, whereas an abstract form would fail to convey the specific, defensive struggle for the survival of the religious orders against external forces.
