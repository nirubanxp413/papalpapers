# Render: Divino Afflante Spiritu

## Why

*Divino Afflante Spiritu* established modern Catholic biblical scholarship by directing exegetes to use the original Hebrew and Greek texts and to master ancient Near Eastern languages to discern the original, intended meaning. This shift from reliance on centuries-old translations (like the Vulgate) to a direct engagement with original sources is perfectly embodied by a singular, foundational object: a set of ancient scribal tools (reed stylus, inkwell, and stone tablet) rendered in precious, sacred materials. This represents the precise instruments required to recover the sacred authors' "modes of expression," translating a scholarly mandate into a concrete, recognizable archaeological artifact elevated to a devotional standard. The choice of ancient writing implements over abstract forms ensures the subject is immediately legible and emphasizes the concrete, text-based focus of the encyclical, rejecting abstraction to ground the concept in the historical reality of Scripture's creation and interpretation.
