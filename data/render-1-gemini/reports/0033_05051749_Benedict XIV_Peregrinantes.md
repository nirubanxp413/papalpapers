# Render: Peregrinantes

## Why

The chosen subject is a single, heavily fortified liturgical Monstrance, designed with structural elements resembling armor and defensive battlements. This specific subject best translates Benedict XIV’s powerful military metaphor, which frames the Jubilee not just as a pilgrimage, but as "spiritual war" against vice and pride. It captures the tension between the spiritual reality of grace and the martial language used to describe the internal battle required to obtain it, grounding the complex theological and political aspects of the document in a singular, immediately recognizable sacred object without resorting to abstract symbols or scenes. The armor elements are not decorative additions but are integral to the recognizable form of the Monstrance itself.
