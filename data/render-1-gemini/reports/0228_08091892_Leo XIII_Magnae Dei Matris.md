# Render: Magnae Dei Matris

## Why

The most direct and unavoidable translation of *Magnae Dei Matris* is the Rosary itself, the central subject of Pope Leo XIII's encyclical. This concrete, universally recognized devotional object perfectly encapsulates the document’s focus on intense Marian devotion and its purpose: the contemplative repetition of prayers focused on the mysteries of Christ and the life of Mary. Choosing any other subject, especially an abstract form, would fail the requirement for immediate recognizability and neglect the explicit, practical, and tangible method of prayer advocated by the Pope. The sculpture isolates the Rosary as a monumental object of veneration, physically embodying the devotion the encyclical champions.
