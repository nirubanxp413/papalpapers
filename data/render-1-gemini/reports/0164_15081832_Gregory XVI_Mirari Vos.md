# Render: Mirari Vos

## Why

For Gregory XVI's "Mirari Vos," which fiercely denounces a "terrible conspiracy of impious men" and sweeping modern errors to defend the Church's ancient doctrine and authority, I have chosen a *fortified, ancient anchor*. This single, recognizable object embodies the papacy's demand for unwavering stability and traditional orthodoxy against the turbulent waves of religious indifferentism, demands for liberty, and revolutionary change. While a ship (representing the Church) would be abstract or a multi-element scene, the anchor is the single, isolated symbol of that unchanging truth and divine protection (Hebrews 6:19) that holds the Church firm against the storms. The anchor itself is designed with defensive, armor-like protrusions and is chained to the Rock (the Papacy), making it a unified sculptural form that speaks to both spiritual constancy and militant defense, far clearer than abstract geometry or complex allegory.
