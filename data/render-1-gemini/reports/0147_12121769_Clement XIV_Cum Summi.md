# Render: Cum Summi

## Why

I chose a single, traditional episcopal mitre as the sculptural form because *Cum Summi* is a personal and direct appeal to the episcopate regarding their vocational fidelity. The mitre explicitly symbolizes the office of the bishop, directly capturing the target audience and the pontiff's call for their renewed commitment to coordinate vigilance. An abstract form was rejected because it could not communicate the specific Catholic identity and hierarchical responsibility central to the document’s message.
