# Render: A Quo Die

## Why

The choice of a stylized mitre and crozier best encapsulates the pastoral program of *A Quo Die*, which focuses on the shared responsibility between the Pope and bishops. While the encyclical touches on spiritual concepts, using abstract forms would fail to communicate the specific ecclesial authority and leadership duties it outlines. These liturgical objects are instantly recognizable symbols of the episcopate and their duty to "feed the flock of God" with servant leadership, prayer, and defense of orthodoxy, making them far more effective than abstract symbols of unity or authority in representing the document’s focus.
