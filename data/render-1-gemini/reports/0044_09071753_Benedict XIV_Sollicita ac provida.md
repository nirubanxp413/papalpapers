# Render: Sollicita ac provida

## Why

The constitution *Sollicita ac provida* regulates the process of reviewing and correcting books to ensure fairness, charity, and theological precision, distinguishing between correction and condemnation. We selected a highly refined, double-pan balance scale to symbolize this process, as it is the archetypal tool of deliberation, measure, and justice. One pan is designed to hold a book (representing the text under review), while the other, balancing the scales with a set of small, precise weights (representing standard doctrine and prudence), visually translates the acts of weighing opinions and finding the appropriate measure between correction and censure.
