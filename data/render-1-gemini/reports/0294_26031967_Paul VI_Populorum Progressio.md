# Render: Populorum Progressio

## Why

A stalk of ripe wheat, heavy with golden grain and secured on a polished bronze plinth, embodies the core theme of *Populorum Progressio*. It instantly evokes the "development" Pope Paul VI called for—the literal flourishing and nourishment required for human dignity, rising above hunger and misery. While abstract shapes could suggest growth, the specific form of wheat provides a universally recognized, concrete symbol of agriculture, sustenance, and the fruitfulness of collaborative human effort that leads to "complete" development. The ripe stalk captures the document's central assertion that "development is the new name for peace," representing the flourishing life that results from justice and solidarity, without resorting to abstract geometry or complex scenarios.
