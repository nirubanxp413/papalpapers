# Render: Sublimis Deus

## Why

The core argument of *Sublimis Deus* is the definitive assertion that all Indigenous peoples are "truly men" possessing full human dignity and an inherent, inviolable right to liberty and property, flatly rejecting the dehumanizing "dumb brute" narrative used to justify enslavement. A Broken Chain was chosen as the most powerful and instantly recognizable symbol for this decisive breaking of bondage and universal declaration of freedom. I rejected abstraction or subtle religious imagery (like a chalice) because the encyclical deals with the visceral, brutal violation of physical human freedom; a broken shackle, isolated and elevated, provides the starkest and most universally understandable depiction of emancipation as a divinely affirmed right.
