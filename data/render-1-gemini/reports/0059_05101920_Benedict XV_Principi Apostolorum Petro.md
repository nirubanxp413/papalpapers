# Render: Principi Apostolorum Petro

## Why

*Principi Apostolorum Petro* formally elevates Saint Ephrem the Syrian, an Eastern deacon and doctor, as common ground for post-war reconciliation and reunion with the Roman See. I rejected abstract concepts like "reunion" or "theology" and selected the **Cithara of the Holy Spirit** (a specialized lyre and archetypal object often used to depict Ephrem) as the single recognizable subject. This sacred devotional instrument embodies Ephrem’s nickname and his enduring influence through Eastern hymnody while clearly representing a bridge between the theological traditions of East and West, unified under Roman primacy.
