# Render: Officio Sanctissimo

## Why

I chose a single, stout, fortress-like **mitre** as the sculptural form. A bishop's mitre represents the "sacred office" referenced in the title (Officio Sanctissimo) and the direct call to the Bavarian hierarchy. Making it a "fortress-mitre" with masonry textures and embedded defensive architecture (crenellations, buttresses) clearly embodies the encyclical’s demand for vigilance and protection against contemporary threats like secularism and rationalism. This single, recognizable object effectively translates Leo XIII's instructions for the clergy to act as defenders of the faith and its heritage, rejecting abstract concepts for concrete authority.
