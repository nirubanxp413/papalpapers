# Render: Signum Magnum

## Why

*Signum Magnum* emphasizes Mary as the "Mother of the Church" and calls for authentic devotion through the imitation of her virtues, culminating in a renewed consecration to her Immaculate Heart. A sculpture of an **Immaculate Heart of Mary** was chosen because it is the central, recognizable devotional symbol explicitly referenced by Pope Paul VI for the faithful to dedicate themselves to. This form concretely embodies the encyclical's focus on Mary's spiritual motherhood, her cooperating love, and the personal, interior response of the believer, rejecting abstraction for an instantly legible, sacred object.
