# Render: Mens Nostra

## Why

We chose a stylized, open sand timer as the sculptural subject. *Mens Nostra* identifies "thoughtlessness" and absorption in external things as the modern disease and calls for the Spiritual Exercises as a remedy; the hourglass is the essential icon of structured, deliberate time and self-examination. This recognizable object embodies the "recollection" and focus on eternal truths by containing the light and spirit (sand/light) and directing it toward the soul (the lower vessel), rather than an abstract concept of time or thought. An abstract form could only vaguely suggest these concepts, failing to capture the definitive nature and specific practice of retreat.
