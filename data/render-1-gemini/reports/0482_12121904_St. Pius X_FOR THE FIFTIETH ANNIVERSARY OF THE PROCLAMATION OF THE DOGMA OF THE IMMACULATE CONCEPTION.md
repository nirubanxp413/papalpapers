# Render: FOR THE FIFTIETH ANNIVERSARY OF THE PROCLAMATION OF THE DOGMA OF THE IMMACULATE CONCEPTION

## Why

Pius X’s address emphasizes a militant defense of traditional teaching and the protection of the clergy from ideological corruption, calling for vigilance and fortitude against difficulty. A **helmet** is the most potent symbol of this combative, defensive, and fortified posture, representing both the "helmet of salvation" and the readiness to withstand intellectual attack. A military subject was chosen over abstract geometry or passive devotional symbols to capture the urgent, protective, and confrontational nature of the call to arms for the bishops, isolated as a single, resolute object on a transparent background.
