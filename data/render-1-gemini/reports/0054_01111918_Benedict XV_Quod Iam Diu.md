# Render: Quod Iam Diu

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this encyclical because it is the preeminent Catholic object of public, communal, and collective prayer—specifically for adoration, intercession, and divine illumination—which perfectly mirrors Pope Benedict XV's call for all parishes to organize public prayers for the success of the peace congress. The intricate, radiant, starburst form of the monstrance visualizes the desired "divine enlightenment" descending upon the delegates. This choice was selected over abstraction because a non-representational form cannot clearly distinguish intercessory, collective prayer from other theological concepts, whereas a monstrance immediately locates the viewer within the specific context of public intercession and the seeking of divine guidance.
