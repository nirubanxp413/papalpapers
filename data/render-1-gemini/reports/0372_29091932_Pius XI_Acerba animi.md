# Render: Acerba animi

## Why

For *Acerba animi*, which addresses the severe persecution of the Church in Mexico and the necessity of continued ministry under protest, the single recognizable subject is the Monstrance. While the encyclical discusses political conflict and restrictions on priests, the core of the Pope's concern is the preservation of Catholic faith and sacramental life, which the Monstrance directly represents as the vessel for Eucharistic Adoration. The Monstrance embodies the call for unity with the Hierarchy and spiritual resilience, standing as a concrete, devotional symbol of faith persevering amidst hostility, making it a far more resonant and immediate choice than any abstract representation of conflict or resilience. A Monstrance, specifically designed with elements suggesting endurance through trial, effectively captures the tension between suffering and steadfast worship outlined in the text.

This monstrance effectively summarizes the tension in *Acerba animi*. By choosing a liturgical vessel representing the faith the Church sought to preserve, but integrating a crown of thorns into the sunburst, it immediately visualizes the 'bitterness of soul' and persecution of the Mexican faithful. The heavy, aged bronze texture underscores the solemn and enduring nature of this struggle. The dead-center, frontal presentation on a transparent grid successfully isolates the sculpture for inspection as requested.
