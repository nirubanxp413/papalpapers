# Render: In suprema Universalis

## Why

A single, deeply carved wooden bowl containing exactly three identical, stylized bread rolls was chosen as the sculptural form. The bowl represents the human stomach and the specific, regulated single daily meal at the heart of Pope Benedict XIV’s decree. The three rolls, rather than one, clearly signify a very small, restricted portion—the definition of a "frugal meal" or *unicam comestionem*—rather than representing a full, satisfying meal. This choice rejects abstraction by using the most basic, recognizable vessel for food, and rejects a generalized religious figure to focus precisely on the discipline of fasting itself.
