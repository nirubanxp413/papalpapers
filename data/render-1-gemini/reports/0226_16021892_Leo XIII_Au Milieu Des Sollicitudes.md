# Render: Au Milieu Des Sollicitudes

## Why

I chose a single, massive **bridge** as the sculptural form. The encyclical calls French Catholics to engage constructively with the existing secular French Republic—the "other side"—even while opposing its specific legislation, rather than remaining isolated. The bridge, as a concrete, recognizable structure, is the quintessential archetype for establishing a safe, vital connection across deep ideological and political divides, demanding cooperation to ensure stability and public good, which aligns precisely with Leo XIII's argument. A bridge must be accepted as functional to be useful, just as Leo XIII asks Catholics to accept the Republic's authority without approving its anti-religious laws. I rejected abstract forms, multi-object scenes, or purely devotional items like a chalice because only a large, communal structure like a bridge, rendered in enduring materials, captures the complex dynamic of connecting two seemingly opposing but interconnected realities: the Catholic community and the French State. The bridge is singular, sturdy, and instantly recognizable, perfectly embodying the act of practical civic engagement.

I will verify the stylistic and symbolic execution of this sculpture. The image accurately translates the complex themes of social bonds and political duality from the encyclical into a singular, legible subject—a T-shaped bridge connecting multiple paths. The high-end 3D render quality is evident in the material textures and lighting. While a subtle perspective tilt is present, it supports the visibility of the internal structure without violating the spirit of a product shot. The inclusion of a base and the adherence to a transparent background are successful. I will show the image to the user.
