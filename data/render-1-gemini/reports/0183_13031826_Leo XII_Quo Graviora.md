# Render: Quo Graviora

## Why

To represent Leo XII's definitive condemnation and "all-encompassing prohibition" against the Carbonari and all secret societies, a broken chain is the most powerful and recognizable symbol. *Quo Graviora* seeks to expose the "sects" and sever the "oath of secrecy" that binds its members to these "pestiferous" organizations. The sculpture of a thick, heavy chain, violently snapped at its center link, visually embodies the annulment of those forbidden bonds and the liberation of the faithful from their destructive influence, presenting a concrete subject of release and fracture rather than an abstract concept.
