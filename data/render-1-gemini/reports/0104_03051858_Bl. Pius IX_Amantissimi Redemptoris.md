# Render: Amantissimi Redemptoris

## Why

*Amantissimi Redemptoris* asserts the unbloody, perpetual continuation of Christ’s sacrifice in the Mass and the pastor’s binding duty to offer it for the people. I chose a large, ornate **Chalice** to represent the source and continuation of this sacrifice, as the encyclical addresses the misuse of indults that led pastors to neglect this core responsibility. A chalice is an instantly recognizable sacred object, avoiding abstraction while powerfully anchoring the document’s theological and disciplinary argument; a shepherd would be a possible alternative, but the chalice directly signifies the *sacrificial* focus and the specific obligation that Pope Pius IX is reinforcing.
