# Render: Quod Apostolici Muneris

## Why

I chose a shield, specifically one bearing a stylized image of a strong family, because it perfectly visualizes the core argument of *Quod Apostolici Muneris*: the defense of established, God-given social order against existential attack. The shield is instantly recognizable as a protective object, directly translating the encyclical’s urgent call to guard the indissoluble family and private property from socialist and communist movements. A simple shield avoids the ambiguity of abstract forms, while the specific imagery of the 'sanctuary' of the family—the key social unit Leo XIII identifies as under attack—makes the theological fit precise and immediate within a single coherent subject.
