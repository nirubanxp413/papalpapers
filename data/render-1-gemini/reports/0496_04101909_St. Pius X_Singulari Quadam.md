# Render: Singulari Quadam

## Why

I chose a singular, massive key to represent *Singulari Quadam*. The key, specifically styled as the Petrine Key of Catholic iconography, instantly symbolizes authority, access, and the power of binding and loosing, which directly reflects the Pope's intervention and his ultimate judgment on the "Mixed Unions" dispute. The single object embodies Pius X's assertion of ecclesiastical authority over the social and moral questions of labor, unifying the diverse elements of the debate—the mixed and Catholic unions, the German bishops, and the workers—under a single, recognizable symbol of papal discernment and control. Abstraction was rejected because it would lack the immediate theological and historical legibility necessary to convey the specific act of adjudication and the assertion of Church authority inherent in the document.
