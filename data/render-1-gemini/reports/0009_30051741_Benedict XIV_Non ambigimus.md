# Render: Non ambigimus

## Why

A discipline of fasting that is "almost completely eliminated" cannot be visualized by the traditional symbols of a rigorous spiritual life. To capture Benedict XIV's lament that the faithful are abandoning ancient spiritual weapons, the sculpture must depict the very thing being cast aside: an object of extreme spiritual rigor, made physical. I chose a large, heavy bronze discipline (scourge) with nine knots, symbolizing the Lenten period of self-denial and ancient penance. Rather than depicting the *act* of fasting (an abstract process), I selected this solitary devotional object to represent the forgotten intensity of the fast itself; it is rendered sitting idle, abandoned, and slightly weathered, standing alone as a tangible record of a neglected practice that is now only observed in a relaxed form.
