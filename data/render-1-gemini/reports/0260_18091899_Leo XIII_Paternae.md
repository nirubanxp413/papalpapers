# Render: Paternae

## Why

The sculptural subject is a single, large bell. It captures the four central concerns of *Paternae*—seminary separation, a sharp Catholic press, careful public service, and lay charitable giving—by serving as the unifying practical voice and spiritual summons for the Church in Brazil. The bell is an immediately recognizable, everyday sacred object that functions only when all its parts are unified; this avoids abstraction while concretely symbolizing the necessary institutional and practical actions required to preserve and grow the Catholic faith. A bell must be "sound" in its structure and "sharply" cast to ring true, mirroring the encyclical's call for rigor, clarity, and discipline rather than abstract concepts.
