# Render: On Rural Life

## Why

A sheaf of ripe wheat was chosen as the singular recognizable subject because it directly and clearly embodies the core themes of Pope Pius XII's 'On Rural Life' without resorting to abstraction. It symbolizes agricultural labor, the inherent goodness of the land, the stewardship of the farmer, and the cooperative spirit, as many individual stalks are bound together. This organic, concrete subject is immediately identifiable to all, grounding the encyclical's theological concepts in the familiar reality of the natural world. Abstract forms were rejected because they would lack the immediate legibility and direct connection to the agrarian life praised in the document.
