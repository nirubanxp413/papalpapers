# Render: Celebationem magni

## Why

A single, monumental, key was chosen as the most compelling symbol of *Celebrationem magni*. This key directly embodies the core theological concept of the Jubilee extension: the opening of the Church's treasury of merits and the granting of the indulgence (the "power of the keys") to all Catholics worldwide. It is a universally recognizable object of security, access, and spiritual authority, avoiding all abstraction while instantly conveying the theme of universal mercy and the unlocking of spiritual graces through confession and papal decree. It is a single, legible subject rather than a multi-object tableau or symbolic cluster.
