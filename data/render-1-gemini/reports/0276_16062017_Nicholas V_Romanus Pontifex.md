# Render: Romanus Pontifex

## Why

I chose a large, ornate golden key as the single sculptural subject to represent *Romanus Pontifex*. The papal bull is fundamentally about the Pope exercising his perceived "power of the keys" to lock out competing Christian nations and unlock exclusive access, ownership, and authority for Portugal over vast territories, resources, and peoples. This object transforms the theoretical concept of papal sovereignty into a physical, recognizable symbol that unifies the spiritual authority (the key itself, a Petrine symbol) with the concrete, material effects of unlocking an entire hemisphere for exploitation. An abstract form would fail to capture the specific historical and political gravity of this absolute grant of monopoly.
