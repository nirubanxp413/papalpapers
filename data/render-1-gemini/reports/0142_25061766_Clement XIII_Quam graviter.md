# Render: Quam graviter

## Why

A single, massive bishop’s *crozier*—the pastoral staff symbolizing episcopal authority—was selected to represent the divine power and independent jurisdiction of the Church, which Clement XIII argues is fatally undermined by secular decrees. This object embodies the bishops' role as shepherds charged with correcting abuses and defending ecclesiastical governance from royal encroachment. The crozier is a universally recognized symbol of spiritual authority, rejecting abstraction and multi-object complexity to stand as a singular, unified figure of the Church’s resistance to secular re-examination of its divine constitution.
