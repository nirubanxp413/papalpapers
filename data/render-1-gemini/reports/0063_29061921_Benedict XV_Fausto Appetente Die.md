# Render: Fausto Appetente Die

## Why

I chose a monumental Dominican rosary with a robust, almost architectural cross as the single sculptural subject. This object directly and concretely synthesizes the three primary pillars of *Fausto Appetente Die*: St. Dominic’s legendary promotion of the Rosary (Marian piety), the doctrinal depth required to preach it effectively (the cross of learning), and the structural fidelity it represents for the Church (Dominic supporting the Lateran). A rosary is a universal Catholic devotional object, instantly recognizable, fitting the sacred context while remaining practical and legible without abstraction.
