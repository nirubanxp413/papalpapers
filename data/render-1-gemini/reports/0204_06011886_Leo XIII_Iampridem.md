# Render: Iampridem

## Why

*Iampridem* focuses on the disruptive impact of state laws (the Prussian *Kulturkampf*) on the internal governance of the Church, particularly concerning the formation of priests and the authority of bishops. The core argument is the necessity of episcopal freedom to appoint pastors and manage seminaries, which Pope Leo XIII argues is essential for the spiritual health of the people and social order. A single, monumental sculpture of a bishop's pastoral crozier was chosen to embody this theme because it is the preeminent symbol of a bishop’s authority, jurisdiction, and spiritual care for his flock. It concretely represents the very pastoral governance and canonical independence that the Pope insists must be respected and restored, rejecting any abstract representation for this vital, recognizable symbol of ecclesiastical office.
