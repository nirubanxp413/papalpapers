# Render: Magnifica Humanitas

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Magnifica Humanitas* because it is the preeminent Catholic vessel for making the abstract reality of Christ's presence concrete and visible. This captures the encyclical's central argument that technology, represented here by the intricate, geometric "monstrance" (resembling circuits and data structures), must not become a self-serving, dehumanizing "Tower of Babel" but must instead serve as the "Rebuilding of Jerusalem" – a vehicle centered on the real, embodied presence of human dignity and the common good (the Host). A monstrance is immediately recognizable as a sacred object designed to focus attention, rejecting the nebulous complexity of pure technological abstraction by grounding it in a defined, worshipful purpose.
