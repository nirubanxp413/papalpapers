# Render: Salutis Nostrae

## Why

I chose a massive, ornate key as the sculptural subject because *Salutis Nostrae* formally opens a Jubilee, which is inherently linked to the theological "power of the keys" entrusted to Peter and the opening of the "treasury" of merits for the remission of sins. A key is an instantly recognizable symbol of access, authority, and unlocking forgiveness, making it the most direct visual metaphor for the central theme of spiritual reconciliation and the canonical decree of Jubilee indulgence. I rejected abstract forms or a detailed pastoral scene because a single, monumental key provides the necessary symbolic power, historical legibility, and architectural mass required for this premium sculptural render, while avoiding ambiguity.
