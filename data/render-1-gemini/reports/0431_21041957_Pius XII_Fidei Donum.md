# Render: Fidei Donum

## Why

The single recognizable subject chosen is a consecrated ciborium overflowing with countless small, identical hosts, symbolizing the gift of faith which must be selflessly shared and distributed. The ciborium, as a liturgical vessel, directly points to the *Fidei Donum* priests (the 'Gift of Faith' itself) and the Eucharist as the source and summit of missionary propagation and prayer. This form translates the encyclical’s theme of sharing the spiritual 'gift' to regions in need, rejecting abstraction for an immediate, tangible symbol of spiritual sustenance, gratitude expressed through giving, and the active propagation of the faith demanded by Pius XII.
