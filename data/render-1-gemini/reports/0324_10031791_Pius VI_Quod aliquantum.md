# Render: Quod aliquantum

## Why

This sculpture of a broken crosier best symbolizes the core conflict and ultimate condemnation of *Quod aliquantum*. Pope Pius VI condemned the French Civil Constitution of the Clergy for violently shattering established Catholic dogma and ecclesiastical discipline. A crook or crosier is the definitive sacred symbol of pastoral authority, making its clear physical breakage the perfect concrete representation of this rupture, far more immediate and legible than abstract geometric symbols.
