# Render: Apostolica Constitutio

## Why

I chose a single, worn **pilgrim's walking staff** to embody *Apostolica Constitutio*. This specific object immediately identifies the document's context—the Holy Year pilgrimage—while its materials (polished dark wood and patinated bronze) and physical form subtly integrate Benedict XIV's deeper concerns. The staff is not just a tool for travel; it features a carved cross at its head and a built-in oil lamp and small container, representing the light of faith, sacramental confession, and the interior renewal that must accompany the physical journey to ensure it produces genuine spiritual fruit. This choice rejects abstraction by focusing on the most essential and recognizable instrument of the pilgrim, integrating theology directly into its structure.
