# Render: Lamentabili Sane

## Why

I chose a classic, traditional Latin Cross, heavily stylized in a "fortified" manner, as the single recognizable subject. This cross, rooted in ancient ecclesial tradition rather than modernist interpretation, represents the unchanging and absolute truths of Catholic dogma which *Lamentabili Sane* defends against the "condemned and proscribed" modernist claims of evolution and mutation. This subject was chosen over an abstract form (like an evolving sphere or an open book) because abstraction often symbolizes the very ambiguity and human-constructed nature that the document explicitly condemns; a solid, unchanging Cross is immediately recognizable as the unwavering anchor of the faith's historical continuity and divine origin. I rejected abstraction completely because the encyclical seeks clarity and defense of immutable truth, which a recognizable, solid cross perfectly embodies without ambiguity.
