# Render: Ut Unum Sint

## Why

The cross is the universal and essential sign of Christianity, the ultimate point of unity which all Christians, regardless of denomination, recognize. To symbolize the restoration of full visible unity which the encyclical seeks, the subject chosen is a monumental crucifix, yet the corpus (Christ's body) is sculpturally depicted as composed of interlocking, segmented, and joined materials, representing the coming together of the fractured "Sister Churches" and Ecclesial Communities. This singular, universally recognized religious symbol immediately communicates that unity is found only in the Cross of Christ, while the specific, joined materiality captures the essence of the difficult but necessary ecumenical journey toward reunification, far exceeding the expressive power of a merely abstract geometrical form.
