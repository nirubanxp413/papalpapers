# Render: Quae Ad Nos

## Why

I chose a shepherd’s crook and a staff joined as a single subject to represent Leo XIII's call for unity. The crook represents the bishop's authority to protect his flock (the faithful departing from the Church), while the staff symbolizes mutual support and defense. Their physical, intertwined form captures the encyclical's central demand: that the bishops of Bohemia and Moravia must unite their efforts and coordinate a common defense to resist the threat, rather than allow internal clerical factionalism to weaken their response. I rejected abstraction because only a recognizable, functional subject can embody the necessary authority and practical unity the Pope insists upon.
