# Render: Quum Diuturnum

## Why

A compass, the traditional navigation tool, is selected as the optimal sculptural subject. It embodies the consultative assembly of Latin American bishops traveling to Rome by providing direction and safe passage through a vast ocean, symbolizing the unity and discipline Pope Leo XIII desired to preserve across the region. The compass specifically refers to the transatlantic journey of many diverse peoples to the singular center (the Apostolic See, or Rome). This single, recognizable object represents the active navigation of theological, moral, and public flourishing, making abstract geometry or a complex multi-object scene unnecessary and less impactful for this specific encyclical.
