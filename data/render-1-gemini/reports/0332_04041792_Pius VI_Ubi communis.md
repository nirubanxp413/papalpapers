# Render: Ubi communis

## Why

*Ubi communis* issues a final, sharp warning to schismatic clergy: recant immediately or face canonical penalties, effectively demanding they drop their false claim to authority and submit to papal discipline. To represent this confrontation between illicit authority and the threat of severe apostolic correction, I have chosen a *Fasces*, a recognizable Roman symbol of magistracy, authority, and punishment. The sculpture, rendered in oxidized bronze and dark iron, transforms this archetypal object of judgment into a sacred warning; the sharp, prominent axe blade symbolizes the impending, decisive cutting off from the Church, while the tightly bound rods represent the unity and discipline that must be restored, capturing the gravity of the papal brief without resorting to abstract concepts.
