# Render: Humanum Genus

## Why

*Humanum Genus* frames the conflict between the Church and Freemasonry as a fundamental choice between divine authority and absolute human autonomy. The encyclical condemns Freemasonry's naturalism, religious indifference, and promotion of democratic-egalitarian politics over divinely ordained hierarchy, ultimately seeing it as the "kingdom of Satan" opposing the "kingdom of God." To embody this, I have chosen a monumental, defensive key (Clavis Petri), forged in heavy iron and refined gold, which visually translates Leo XIII’s central claim of exclusive, divinely-instituted authority against the secularizing forces he attributes to Masonic philosophy. A key is an instantly recognizable, concrete symbol of exclusive power, authority, and the acts of 'binding and loosing' on earth, which the Pope argues the Church must maintain against internal and external corrosion. The key’s heavy material presence directly rejects abstraction, presenting a singular, protective force that defines the very boundary between sacred order and the destructive forces of the secular world described in the text.
