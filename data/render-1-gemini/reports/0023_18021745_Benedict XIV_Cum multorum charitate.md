# Render: Cum multorum charitate

## Why

*Cum multorum charitate* proclaims a jubilee focused on seeking divine intervention through spiritual sacrifice—specifically prayer and fasting—to combat widespread calamities and coldness of charity. The Lenten Discipline (Prayer and Fasting) is described by Benedict XIV as the "one safe refuge," and a key motivation is the preservation and recovery of King Louis XV. To unite these themes of collective sacrifice, prayer for a ruler, and spiritual deliverance into one recognizable form, I have chosen a liturgical censer. This single, immediately legible sacred vessel embodies the continuous, aromatic offering of a nation's prayer and the sacrificial nature of the requested fasting, presenting it as a single, monumental, refined object rather than an abstract concept of spirit.
