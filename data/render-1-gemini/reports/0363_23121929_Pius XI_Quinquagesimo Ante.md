# Render: Quinquagesimo Ante

## Why

The chosen subject is a grand, papal key with the Lateran Treaty’s coat of arms incorporated into its bow (the handle). This single, highly recognizable religious object perfectly encapsulates *Quinquagesimo Ante*, which celebrates the landmark 1929 Lateran Treaty resolving the "Roman Question" and restoring the Holy See’s temporal sovereignty, symbolized by the Keys of St. Peter. A key naturally represents openings, reconciliation, access, authority, and the unlocking of blessings, fitting the encyclical's tone of gratitude for a jubilee year filled with divine gifts and political resolution. This concrete form is immediately legible and avoided abstraction by anchoring the complex geopolitical and theological themes in a singular, potent Catholic symbol of spiritual and temporal authority.
