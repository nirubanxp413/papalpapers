# Render: Divini Amoris Scientia

## Why

A single, humble **sandaled foot** best translates *Divini Amoris Scientia*. Pope St. John Paul II elevates Thérèse of Lisieux, despite her lack of formal training, as a Doctor of the Church because of her mature "little way" of spiritual childhood and trust. This profound theological truth, accessible to all, requires a humble, grounded, and universal human symbol rather than an abstract form of theological complexity. The simple, well-worn sandal signifies her practical path of humility, service, and spiritual journey, concretely embodying the accessibility of her profound message to the Universal Church.
