# Render: Qui Pluribus

## Why

To translate Pope Pius IX's *Qui Pluribus* into a single recognizable sculptural form, I have chosen a Bishop's Mitre. The encyclical identifies a multifaceted and pervasive assault on the Church from rationalism, indifferentism, secret societies, and communism, all unified in their rejection of divine revelation and ecclesial authority. To counter this, the text establishes the episcopate as the primary defensive bulwark and source of unified action. A Bishop's Mitre serves as the potent, universally recognizable symbol of that teaching authority and spiritual defense, embodying the Church's defined order against external error. An abstract form was rejected because it would fail to immediately convey this specific office and authority, essential to the document’s message.
