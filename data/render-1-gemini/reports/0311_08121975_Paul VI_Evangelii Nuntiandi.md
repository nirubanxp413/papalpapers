# Render: Evangelii Nuntiandi

## Why

We selected a stylized monstrance made of gold, rock crystal, and ivory as the subject because *Evangelii Nuntiandi* centers the entire Church on evangelization, which begins and ends with the explicit, living presence of Christ in the world. The monstrance, a familiar sacred vessel used to present the Eucharist for adoration, perfectly represents this core mission: presenting Christ to the nations with zeal, reverence, and clarity. While abstract forms might fail to capture the evangelizing purpose, and multiple symbols would clutter the message, this single recognizable sacred object embodies the proactive act of witnessing, proclaiming, and forming community around the transcendent presence of Christ.
