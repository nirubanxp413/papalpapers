# Render: Unam Sanctam

## Why

*Unam Sanctam* argues for the absolute necessity of unity under a single, divinely ordained authority: the Roman Pontiff, who holds both spiritual and temporal power. This required a singular subject that embodies hierarchical authority, ultimate judgment, and sacred office. Abstract geometry or symbolic gestures could not capture the concrete dogmatic declaration of papal supremacy; therefore, the Papal Tiara was selected as the essential and recognizable subject. It is an unmistakably sacred artifact that serves as the visual anchor for the Church’s claim to being the "one sheepfold" with "one head."
