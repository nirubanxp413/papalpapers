# Render: Appetente Sacro

## Why

The subject chosen is the Crown of Thorns, a singular and instantly recognizable symbol of Christ’s passion and suffering. This form powerfully captures the central message of *Appetente Sacro*, which demands bodily mortification and penance to satisfy divine justice. It directly embodies the encyclical’s call to enter into Christ's passion through physical fasting, rejecting abstract concepts like 'penance' for the concrete reality of sacrificial suffering. The sharp thorns represent the necessary spiritual and physical rigor required, rather than mere symbolic withdrawal from sin. The circular form suggests the unbroken discipline requested by Pope Clement XIII for the Lenten season.
