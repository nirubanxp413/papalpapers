# Render: Fulgens Radiatur

## Why

I chose a single, formidable stone crozier as the recognizable sculptural subject to unify St. Benedict’s legacy. The crozier represents both his spiritual authority as an abbot leading monks through prayer and labor, and his civilizational authority, using that same structured Rule to stabilize and rebuild post-Roman Europe. The sculpture is rendered in unyielding granite to symbolize Benedict’s solid foundation and "restoration" of order amidst societal "shipwreck," avoiding abstraction and focusing on the concrete tools of monastic stability requested by the encyclical.
