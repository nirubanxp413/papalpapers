# Render: Perpensis circumstantiis

## Why

I chose a solitary figure of a priest, represented through the recognizable form of a man in full priestly vestments (cassock, alb, stole, and chasuble), specifically in the act of raising his right hand in the gesture of absolution, to symbolize *Perpensis circumstantiis*. This document addresses the need for absolution in exceptional circumstances where clergy have fled, and the specific gesture directly visually translates the extended "faculties to absolve" granted by the Pope. The figure stands alone, reflecting both the isolation of faithful priests and the universal nature of the Church's sacramental power, which is no longer confined by the original geographic restrictions; abstraction was rejected because a clear, single figure is the most direct way to represent this specific, human-centered sacramental action that Pius VI is authorizing.
