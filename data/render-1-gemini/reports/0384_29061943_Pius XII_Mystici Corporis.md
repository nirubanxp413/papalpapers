# Render: Mystici Corporis

## Why

A single, monumental *Crozier* (pastoral staff) was chosen to embody *Mystici Corporis*. The encyclical's defining argument is that the Church is the visible, organic Body of Christ, not a purely spiritual concept, and it operates under Christ as its supreme, governing Head. The crozier is the ultimate recognizable symbol of both ecclesial authority and pastoral care, directly referencing the Bishop of Rome’s visible governance while pointing to Christ, the true Good Shepherd and "Head of the Body." This single, concrete object immediately evokes the encyclical’s focus on structured, hierarchical reality over abstract theological geometry or a vague spiritual idea.
