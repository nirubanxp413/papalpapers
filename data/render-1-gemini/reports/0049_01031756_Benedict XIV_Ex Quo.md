# Render: Ex Quo

## Why

I chose a single, highly ornate liturgical book as the sculptural subject. *Ex Quo* is fundamentally about the correction and restoration of the Eastern Euchologion (liturgical book), which the Pope discusses through specific admonitions regarding prayer and ritual. A sculpture of the book itself, elevated to a sacred object, directly embodies the physical fruit of the encyclical's labor while avoiding abstraction, multi-object clusters, or environments. The sculpture emphasizes the preservation of proper, non-schismatic Eastern Christian worship within Catholic unity, as contained and defined within this corrected text.
