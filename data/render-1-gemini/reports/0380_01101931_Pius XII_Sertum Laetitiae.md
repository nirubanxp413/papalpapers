# Render: Sertum Laetitiae

## Why

I chose a single, idealized olive-wood carving of a **thriving, rooted family unit**—a father, mother, and child in an intimate embrace—as the most effective subject for *Sertum Laetitiae*. Pope Pius XII’s document praises American institutional growth but pivots sharply to warn that material progress is shallow without moral health, centering his analysis on the family as the indispensable foundation under attack from divorce and materialism. A simple cross would be too generic, and tools of labor would ignore the moral pivot. This clear, recognizable depiction of a holy and stable family, rendered with a sacred, devotional finish, directly encapsulates the Pope's central argument: true progress and civilization itself depend on defending the sanctity and unity of the family.
