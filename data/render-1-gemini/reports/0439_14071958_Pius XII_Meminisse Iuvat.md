# Render: Meminisse Iuvat

## Why

A single, monumental *rosary* best embodies the core message of *Meminisse Iuvat*. It directly represents the devotional practice Pope Pius XII explicitly calls for: worldwide public prayers to Mary for the persecuted Church. A rosary avoids abstraction and provides a universally recognized, concrete devotional object that visually synthesizes the call to Marian intercession and spiritual renewal through penance.
