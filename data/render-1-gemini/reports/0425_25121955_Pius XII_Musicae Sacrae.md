# Render: Musicae Sacrae

## Why

A single, monumental bell was chosen as the most fitting and recognizable sculptural subject for *Musicae Sacrae*. The bell is the primary liturgical instrument for the assembly, symbolizing the voice of the Church calling the faithful to worship and unifying the community in prayer. Its form inherently references the foundational role of Gregorian chant (often depicted as a single melody) while also accommodating polyphony and other approved sacred music, thus embodying the encyclical's teachings on the theological purpose and primacy of liturgical sound without resorting to an abstract representation of music.
