# Render: The Function Of Art

## Why

Pius XII argues that art’s true purpose is to reflect divine beauty and serve as a “Bible of the people” for spiritual instruction. I chose the single subject of an open, illuminated Bible carved entirely from white statuary marble to embody this concept. An open Bible is immediately recognizable and represents the scripture that great art is meant to communicate, rejecting any obscure abstraction in favor of clear sacred legible form. The open pages, carved with symbolic bas-relief scenes (like a micro-Cathedral door) rather than readable text, represent the visual language of art that makes faith accessible and unites the finite with the infinite.
