# Render: Sempiternus Rex Christus

## Why

For *Sempiternus Rex Christus*, a single, monumental **biretta** was chosen. This recognizable liturgical headwear directly visualizes the definition of Christ as one distinct, coherent subject, refuting any notion of a divided figure or person, a central tenet of the Council of Chalcedon. Abstract geometry or symbolic multi-object scenes were rejected, as they might suggest the confusion of natures or a fracture in identity that Pius XII explicitly condemns. The biretta’s solitary, unitary form, isolated on a transparent background, powerfully reinforces the dogmatic clarity that there is only one "King Christ," distinct and defined, rejecting all theological abstraction or ambiguity.
