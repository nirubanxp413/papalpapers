# Render: Inter Insigniores

## Why

To represent *Inter Insigniores*, which defends the continuous Catholic tradition of ordaining only men based on Christ’s deliberate choice and the priest acting *in persona Christi*, I selected the central icon of the Apostolic succession: the Head of Saint Peter. This specific figure is the definitive archetypal character embodying the male pastoral role established by Christ, ensuring the sculpture is immediately identifiable as a sacred figure rather than an abstract concept. I rejected abstract forms because the document relies on the historical reality of male figures in ministry and the immutability of a concrete sacramental sign; a representation of Peter’s face, sculpted with gravity and authority, directly symbolizes the masculine character of the priestly vocation and the chain of tradition that *Inter Insigniores* seeks to preserve.
