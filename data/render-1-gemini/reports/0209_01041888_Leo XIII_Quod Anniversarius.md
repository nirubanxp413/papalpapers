# Render: Quod Anniversarius

## Why

I chose a single, large Pascal candle set within an ornate, floor-standing candlestick as the sculptural subject. The document, *Quod Anniversarius*, focuses on Pope Leo XIII’s jubilee priesthood anniversary as an occasion to extend spiritual benefits, specifically the grace of a universal Mass for the Dead with accompanying indulgences for the souls in purgatory. The Pascal candle is the quintessential liturgical symbol of the Risen Christ, the source of all grace, and its light is a powerful, concrete representation of the light of glory and relief sought for the suffering souls, moving from the Pope's personal joy (the flame) to the Church's universal charitable act (the entire object). I rejected abstraction because a specific liturgical object is instantly recognizable, grounded in tradition, and directly communicates the concepts of light, offering, and sacred time without needing symbolic decoding.
