# Render: In Ipso

## Why

I chose a highly fortified and stylized **Shield**, incorporating subtle aspects of the Papal Tiara, to visually embody *In Ipso*. This encyclical is essentially a call to defense against a perceived organized assault on Catholicism’s role in society, particularly education and the family. The Shield instantly symbolizes protection, defence, and a steadfast stance, which aligns perfectly with Leo XIII’s desire to fortify the Austrian Church’s institutions. A shield is a singular, recognizable object that conveys strength, resilience, and resistance without reverting to vague abstractions or complex multi-subject tableaux. Abstraction would fail to communicate the defensive, operational urgency and structural fortification that the document demands. The single, potent object of a ceremonial shield with embedded papal motifs perfectly captures the encyclical's spirit of institutional self-defense and mobilization.
