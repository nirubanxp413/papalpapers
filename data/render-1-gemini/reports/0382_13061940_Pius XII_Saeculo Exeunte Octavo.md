# Render: Saeculo Exeunte Octavo

## Why

The cross with its central compass rose is chosen to embody *Saeculo Exeunte Octavo*'s central argument that Portugal's faith was the driving force behind its missionary explorations. This single, immediately recognizable religious symbol fuses Christ's universal message with the nautical tool that charted the paths for Francis Xavier and Vasco da Gama. It concretely visualizes the encyclical's link between historical glory and contemporary missionary renewal without resorting to abstract concepts, environmental tableaus, or confusing metaphors, remaining legible as both a devotional object and a navigational emblem.
