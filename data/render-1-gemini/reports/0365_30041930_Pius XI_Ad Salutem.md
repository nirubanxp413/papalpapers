# Render: Ad Salutem

## Why

I chose a monstrance as the single recognizable subject for *Ad Salutem* because it visually unites Pope Pius XI's primary goals: venerating the saint's theological legacy (the Trinity, grace, and free will, symbolized by the radiant center) while asserting the Church's necessary and definitive authority against contemporary secular errors, just as the monstrance elevates and centers Christ's presence for adoration and instruction. This concrete liturgical object serves as a potent, single-subject symbol of doctrinal truth and pastoral exhortation, avoiding any abstract representation of these complex theological concepts. A stylized figure of Augustine or a collection of books were rejected as too narrative or multi-object, lacking the singular, elevated focus required by the rubric.
