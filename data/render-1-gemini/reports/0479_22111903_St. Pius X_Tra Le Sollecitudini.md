# Render: Tra Le Sollecitudini

## Why

I chose a Gregorian Chant book, open on a sculptural stand, because *Tra Le Sollecitudini* is primarily about the restoration of liturgical music. The document explicitly elevates Gregorian Chant as the supreme model of sacred music, defining it as the proper voice of the Church’s prayer. A recognizable physical book, displaying the unique four-line square notation of chant, directly captures the document’s central binding instruction to return to this "true and proper" music, rather than relying on abstract shapes or symbolic sound which are too vague.
