# Render: Decet Romanum Pontificem

## Why

For *Decet Romanum Pontificem*, which declares the final excommunication of Martin Luther and casts him out of the Church, a monstrance—a liturgical vessel designed to protect and display the Eucharist—is the ideal subject. The sculpture focuses on the dramatic central event of the excommunication: a jagged, definitive split cleaving the monstrance’s circular lunar chamber and rayed halo. This sharp rupture embodies the irrevocable theological division and the spiritual barrier enforced against the heretic, choosing a concrete, damaged sacred object over an abstract representation of separation.
