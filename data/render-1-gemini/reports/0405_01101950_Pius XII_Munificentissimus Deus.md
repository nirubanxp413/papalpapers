# Render: Munificentissimus Deus

## Why

I chose a crowned, ascending figure of the Virgin Mary, depicted not merely as a statue but as an exalted being rising in glory, to directly visualize the Assumption of Mary. This subject was chosen because it is the central mystery defined in *Munificentissimus Deus*: Mary's bodily assumption into heaven. I rejected abstract forms because they would fail to capture the profound theological truth of Mary's bodily preservation and glorification. A literal, physical representation of Mary being taken up into heaven is essential to communicating the dogma’s emphasis on her unique relationship with God and her triumph over death.
