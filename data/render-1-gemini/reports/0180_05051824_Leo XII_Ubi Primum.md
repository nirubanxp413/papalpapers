# Render: Ubi Primum

## Why

I chose a solitary, weathered Shepherd’s staff, or Crozier, for the sculpture. *Ubi Primum* centers on defining the distinct role of the shepherd (the Bishop) in protection, residence, and strict doctrinal authority against modern "indifferentism." The Crozier is the universal, concrete symbol of this pastoral office, its hooked top signifying the duty to reclaim lost souls and assert boundaries. This singular object captures the encyclical's pastoral core far more effectively than an abstract form, which would lack the immediate theological and ecclesiastical recognition required.
