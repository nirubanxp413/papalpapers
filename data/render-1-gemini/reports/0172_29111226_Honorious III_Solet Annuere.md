# Render: Solet Annuere

## Why

The core meaning of *Solet Annuere* is the formal approval of the Franciscan Rule, which is fundamentally defined by "most holy poverty." While the document lists many instructions, the singular physical requirement that separates the Friar Minor from other monastic orders and sums up their specific mode of imitation of Christ is the *habitus*, or religious habit. Therefore, I have chosen a worn, patched burlap habit as the single, recognizable sculptural subject. This garment is the definitive symbol of Franciscan identity and evangelical poverty, making it immediately legible and tangible, perfectly capturing the encyclical's translation of a "form of life" into a visible reality, far better than abstract geometry could.
