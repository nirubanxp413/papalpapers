# Render: Annus Qui Hunc

## Why

I chose a single, magnificent, yet structurally simple bell as the sculptural form because *Annus Qui Hunc* focuses profoundly on the audible sanctity and orderly performance of the divine office and polyphony, particularly in anticipation of visiting pilgrims. The bell is an instantly recognizable, concrete object that serves as the liturgical instrument par excellence for calling the faithful and marking sacred time with grave, resonant dignity. I rejected abstraction because only a recognizable, material object can convey the encyclical’s demand for tangible, audible order, cleanliness, and reverence in the physical practices and instruments of worship.
