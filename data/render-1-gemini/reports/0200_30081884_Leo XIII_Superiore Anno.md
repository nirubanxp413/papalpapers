# Render: Superiore Anno

## Why

*Superiore Anno* demands a concrete devotional item that embodies both the renewed liturgical directive and the pastoral appeal for divine protection. The single object that perfectly merges these elements is the monstrance, specifically configured as a protective vessel enclosing the Blessed Sacrament, which was central to the decreed exposition and processions. Rejecting abstraction, this recognizable liturgical form visualizes the presence of Christ as the Church’s ultimate defense against "Asiatic cholera" and spiritual enemies. The monstrance acts as a sacred shield and sanctuary, focusing the power of the requested Rosary devotion into a singular, tangible sign of hope and protection for the besieged faithful.
