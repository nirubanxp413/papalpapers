# Render: Ad nostras manus

## Why

The core conflict in *Ad nostras manus* is the rejection of a "false bishop" and a call for submission to legitimate authority, centered around canonical discipline. A mitre represents episcopal authority, but one that is broken and cracked visually embodies the Pope's forceful dismissal of the fraudulent, null, and sacrilegious titles assumed by the pseudo-bishop. We choose this concrete, recognizable object, showing its dysfunction through fractured materials, rather than abstractly depicting concepts like "falsehood" or "chaos" using non-representational forms, ensuring immediate legibility as a symbol of broken ecclesiastical order.
