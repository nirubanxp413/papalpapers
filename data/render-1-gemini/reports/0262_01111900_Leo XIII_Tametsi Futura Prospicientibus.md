# Render: Tametsi Futura Prospicientibus

## Why

I chose a monstrance, a clear and single religious object, to encapsulate Pope Leo XIII's *Tametsi Futura Prospicientibus*. The encyclical identifies Christ as the sole remedy for the world's spiritual and social disorders, urging a definitive return to His authority and the Eucharist. This concrete devotional subject rejects the abstract concept of spiritual revival by centering Leo XIII's message in the visible, Eucharistic presence that anchors individual salvation and civilizational renewal.
