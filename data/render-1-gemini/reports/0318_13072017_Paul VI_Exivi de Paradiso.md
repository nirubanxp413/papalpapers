# Render: Exivi de Paradiso

## Why

I chose a pair of Franciscan sandals, elevated as a sacred object and held by an armored fist, to visualize the encyclical "Exivi de Paradiso." This single recognizable object powerfully captures the core theme: the definition and enforcement of the Franciscan Rule of poverty (represented by the simple, worn sandals) by papl authority. The document is juridical, settling doubts and imposing strict obligations, which is symbolized by the sandals being 'raised' or set apart as precepts. An armored fist holding the delicate, humble sandals perfectly encapsulates the tension and the central action: the authoritative papal clarification ("precepts") surrounding the spiritual ideal of poverty ("counsels"). I rejected abstraction because a recognizable everyday devotional item like sandals provides immediate legibility and connects directly to the historical context of the Order of Friars Minor, which a purely abstract form could not do.
