# Render: In Hac Tanta

## Why

I chose a *Crozier*, the pastoral staff of a bishop, to represent the encyclical's focus on St. Boniface and papal obedience. Boniface's mission was grounded in episcopal and papal authority, and the Crozier is the definitive liturgical symbol of that authority and the shepherd's role. It rejects abstraction by presenting a single, recognizable object of governance and communion rather than an amorphous concept of "unity." The Crozier physically embodies the hierarchy and structure that Benedict XV presents as the source of order and true peace for Germany.
