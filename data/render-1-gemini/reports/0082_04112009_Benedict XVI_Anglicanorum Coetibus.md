# Render: Anglicanorum Coetibus

## Why

I chose a **Crozier** for *Anglicanorum Coetibus*. A crozier is instantly recognizable as a liturgical object and a symbol of pastoral authority, making it the perfect choice to represent the establishment of the Personal Ordinariates, a juridical and institutional structure under the Church’s authority. It physically embodies both the spiritual shepherding of the former Anglicans entering Catholic communion and the formal, canonical governance that supports their unique patrimony. I rejected abstract forms because a crozier offers an immediately intelligible visual metaphor for the specific institutional solution of the document.
