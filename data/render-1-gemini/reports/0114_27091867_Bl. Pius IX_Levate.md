# Render: Levate

## Why

A single, monumental *Thurible*—the vessel used for burning incense during solemn liturgies like the public prayer requested by Pius IX—is the most potent and recognizable subject to synthesize the encyclical's disparate crises. The object itself is instantly legible as a primary Catholic sacred vessel, ensuring clarity over abstraction. Its singular form represents the universal prayer of the Church (incense rising) ascending to God *specifically* as the divinely appointed remedy against the converging assaults of political suppression and schismatic interference. The thurible represents the Church's active, unified spiritual response, bridging the gap between the Pope's plea for prayer and the actual, tangible implementation of that plea by the global episcopate.
