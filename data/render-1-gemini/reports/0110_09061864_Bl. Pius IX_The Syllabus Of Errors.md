# Render: The Syllabus Of Errors

## Why

*The Syllabus of Errors* is a firm Papal catalogue condemning ninety distinct propositions of modern thought and political liberalism, designed to defend the traditional authority of the Catholic Church and the Roman Pontiff against external assaults. To embody this defensive yet authoritative stance, I selected a single, formidable sculptural subject: a stylized, monumental shield. The shield functions as an ancient symbol of defense, representing the Church's uncompromising protection of orthodoxy and its divine constitution against rationalism, indifference, and secular ideologies. A non-representational, abstract sculpture was rejected because it would lack the immediate theological and historical legibility necessary to communicate this act of strong defense and authority.
