# Render: Christus Dominus

## Why

A single, monumental, covered ciborium was chosen as the sculptural subject to represent *Christus Dominus*. The ciborium, which specifically holds the consecrated Hosts for distribution during Holy Communion, directly represents the Eucharist itself, the central focus of the document's reform regarding the fast. The dome-like lid and the cross finial clearly identify the sacred vessel, making it instantly recognizable and concrete, avoiding any theological abstraction. The form prioritizes the physical act of receiving Communion, which Pope Pius XII made more accessible to the faithful under modern conditions.
