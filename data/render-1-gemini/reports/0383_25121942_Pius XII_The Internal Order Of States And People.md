# Render: The Internal Order Of States And People

## Why

Pius XII’s *The Internal Order Of States And People* presents tranquil living together in order as impossible without reference to God as the ultimate foundation. To capture this concept of societal order grounded in divine stability, I chose a monumental, architectural keystone. This single recognizable object, which stabilizes and locks an arch, concrete embodies the message’s claim that divine order is the essential element that holds human society together, preventing its collapse. It rejects abstraction by presenting a tangible, ancient structural component, made sacred through material and craftsmanship, rather than a vague geometric symbol.
