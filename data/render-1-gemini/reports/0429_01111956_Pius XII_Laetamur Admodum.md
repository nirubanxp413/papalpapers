# Render: Laetamur Admodum

## Why

For *Laetamur Admodum*, I chose an **unbroken oil lamp** (a traditional ceramic vessel with a burning wick) as the single recognizable subject. This object embodies the core duality of the text: it represents the "new dawn of peace" and the preservation of light in Poland and Hungary after "heavy storms," while its active flame signifies the urgent need for intensified, continuous prayer (the primary pastoral imperative) to avert a "second, still-active crisis" threatening the Near East. An oil lamp is an ancient, recognizable devotional and practical object that symbolizes both watchfulness (the faithful waiting for peace) and divine guidance through reason and justice, perfectly capturing the Pope's appeal without resorting to abstraction or complex narrative scenes.
