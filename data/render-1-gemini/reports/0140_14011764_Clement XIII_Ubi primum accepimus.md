# Render: Ubi primum accepimus

## Why

To translate Pope Clement XIII’s strong endorsement of a specific, preferred candidate (Archduke Joseph of Austria) to become the King of the Romans, I chose a single, recognizable object: an Imperial Crown. This subject avoids abstraction by anchoring the abstract concept of royal virtues and hereditary piety in a concrete, universally understood symbol of sovereignty and leadership. The Crown best embodies the Pope’s joy at the upcoming election, his preference for a specific ruler, and the ultimate purpose of this political act within the context of the Holy Roman Empire, while also signifying the spiritual protection the Pope seeks for the Church's rights.
