# Render: Mater Et Magistra

## Why

I chose a stylized sheaf of wheat as the sculptural subject because *Mater et Magistra* centers on balancing economic progress with social justice, explicitly addressing agricultural decline alongside industrial growth. The wheat sheaf directly embodies nourishment, cultivation, the work of the farmer (agriculture), and communal sustenance, making it a powerful, recognizable symbol of the document’s concern for the common good and the dignity of labor. An abstract form would fail to communicate this specific theological and social grounding in concrete human needs, and avoiding a complex scene (like a worker with tools and fields) maintains the required single-subject discipline while strongly representing the encyclical's core message.
