# Render: Inter omnigenas

## Why

I chose a monstrance as the single, recognizable sculptural subject. This devotional object, designed specifically to display the Eucharist (the body of Christ) for public adoration, symbolizes the essential core of Catholic identity that Pope Benedict XIV is urging the faithful in Serbia to maintain. It directly counters the document's central issue: outward conformity to Islam and irregular sacramental practice. The monstrance represents the visibility of Christ's presence, which must not be hidden or mixed with other faiths; the central circular window, instead of holding the host, is rendered as an impenetrable, empty void, powerfully visualizing the "Withheld Viaticum" (Holy Communion) and the absence of valid, open sacramental life that the Pope deplores. I rejected abstract forms, which would require explanation, and chose this familiar liturgical vessel to instantly communicate the struggle for authentic faith identity on a transparent background.
