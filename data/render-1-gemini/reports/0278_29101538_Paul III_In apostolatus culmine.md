# Render: In apostolatus culmine

## Why

I chose a formal University Seal as the single recognizable subject for *In apostolatus culmine*. This papal bull is a bureaucratic and structural act of *founding*; it does not debate abstract concepts or theology, but legally creates an institution (the University of Santo Domingo), granting it specific powers and parity with established European universities. The bull explicitly authorizes the use of a unique seal to validate the degrees and doctoral authority it confers. An ornate, tangible seal is the most accurate concrete artifact to represent this foundational moment of established, academic authority in the New World, grounding the abstract transfer of power into a physical, legally recognized object. An abstract form or a generic cross would fail to communicate the document's specific, organizational impact.
