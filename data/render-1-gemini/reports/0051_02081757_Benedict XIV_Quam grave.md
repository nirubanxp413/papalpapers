# Render: Quam grave

## Why

The choice of a large, complex, broken iron chain as the sculpture's subject represents the severe, yet merciful, justice delivered in *Quam grave*. The broken chain symbolizes the definitive break from illegal celebration of the Mass by an unordained person (the "chains" of false clerical authority). While Benedict XIV imposes the serious penalty of perpetual galley service (implied by the heavy-duty iron links), he rejects the ultimate constraint of capital punishment for the repentant offenders, thus "breaking" the finality of death. This recognizable object powerfully communicates the interplay between ecclesiastical condemnation, civil penalty, and spiritual mercy without resorting to abstract concepts.
