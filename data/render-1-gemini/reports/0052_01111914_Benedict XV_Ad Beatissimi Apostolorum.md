# Render: Ad Beatissimi Apostolorum

## Why

I chose a single, stout anchor—forged from bronze and reinforced with a heavy chain—to embody *Ad Beatissimi Apostolorum*. Against the fragmentation of society and the violence of the First World War, the encyclical calls for spiritual and ecclesiastical unity, doctrinal orthodoxy, and obedience to the legitimate authority of the Church as the necessary stabilization for human society. A substantial anchor, derived from early Christian symbolism (Hebrews 6:19), is the ultimate concrete representation of this desired stability, holding the human community fast against the destructive currents of war and societal breakdown, rejecting all abstract geometry in favor of this familiar, singular, and potent symbol of hope and steadfast authority.
