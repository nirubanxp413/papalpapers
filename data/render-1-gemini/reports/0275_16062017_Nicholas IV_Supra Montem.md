# Render: Supra Montem

## Why

*Supra Montem* establishes the foundational, administrative rule for the lay Third Order of St. Francis, guiding them on how to live a life of penance and devotion while remaining in the world. To represent this established, structured Way of Life, I chose the Tau cross, which was St. Francis’s signature and the central symbol of the order, symbolizing humility, penance, and commitment. This single, universally recognizable religious symbol powerfully embodies the core spiritual identity and legislative foundation of the Tertiaries without resorting to vague abstraction. The Tau cross, defined by a distinct, rugged, weathered wood texture, immediately communicates the rustic simplicity, poverty, and ancient spiritual heritage of the Franciscan charism *Supra Montem* sought to organize.
