# Render: Redemptoris Missio

## Why

I chose a monstrance to symbolize *Redemptoris Missio*. The encyclical's core argument centers on the unique and universal role of Christ in salvation and calls for a renewed missionary effort. A monstrance is a specific liturgical vessel designed for the public exposition of the Eucharist—Christ's real presence—making it the ideal object to represent "proclamation" and "the *ad gentes* mission" to reveal Christ to all nations. This single, recognizable sacred object immediately communicates the source and summit of the Church's missionary drive without resorting to vague abstraction, environments, or multi-figure tableaux.
