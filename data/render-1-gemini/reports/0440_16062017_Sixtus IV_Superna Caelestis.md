# Render: Superna Caelestis

## Why

I chose a **seraphic mitre** because *Superna Caelestis* is the definitive act of canonizing Saint Bonaventure, known as the "Seraphic Doctor." The mitre represents both his theological authority (as Bishop of Albano and Cardinal) and his entry into the *catalogue of Saints*, directly visualizing the formal act of canonization mentioned in the bull. Choosing a recognizable liturgical headpiece, heavily embellished with seraphic imagery and symbols of heavenly glorification, clearly communicates the elevation of a specific historic figure into the celestial ranks, making the document's complex process and exultant theological claim immediately legible in a single, concrete object. This choice rejects abstraction by focusing on a specific, powerful, and historically accurate symbol of the saint's dual role and ultimate recognition by the Church.
