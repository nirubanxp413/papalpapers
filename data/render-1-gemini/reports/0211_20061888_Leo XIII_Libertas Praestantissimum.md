# Render: Libertas Praestantissimum

## Why

A shepherd with a staff was chosen to represent *Libertas Praestantissimum* because it encapsulates Leo XIII's definition of true liberty: freedom directed by reason and ultimately governed by divine law. The shepherd embodies the legitimate authority derived from God, guiding the flock (humanity) not to restrict their freedom, but to protect it from straying into harmful "license." This single figure is an instantly recognizable sacred archetype of protective guidance, contrasting sharply with political liberalism's view of absolute individual sovereignty, while rejecting abstraction entirely.
