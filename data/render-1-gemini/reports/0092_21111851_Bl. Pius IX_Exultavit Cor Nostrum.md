# Render: Exultavit Cor Nostrum

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a single, massive **anchor** integrated with a stylized, protective **shield**. This combined object perfectly encapsulates *Exultavit Cor Nostrum*, which balances the rejoicing and hope of the Jubilee (represented by the anchor's stabilizing, saving function) with the necessity of collective prayer and resistance against the enumerated "evils" threatening faith and society (represented by the defensive shield). I rejected all abstract concepts and multi-object tableaux in favor of this single, historically potent, and immediately recognizable emblem of hope and defense, rendered in solid, sacred materials to convey the text's gravity.
