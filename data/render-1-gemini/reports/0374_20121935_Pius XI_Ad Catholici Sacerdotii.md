# Render: Ad Catholici Sacerdotii

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Ad Catholici Sacerdotii* because it visually unites the priest's identity and his primary function. The Pope emphasizes the priest as "another Christ" whose defining power is celebrating the Eucharist—the "real Body of Christ." A monstrance is the sacred vessel made specifically to display the consecrated Host; it makes the invisible theological truth concrete and recognizable. I rejected abstract concepts like radiating geometric light because they fail to capture the physical, sacramental, and distinct character of the priesthood that Pius XI meticulously details. The monstrance is instantly readable as a sacred object centered on the Eucharist, embodying the priest’s role as the mediator who brings Christ to the faithful.
