# Render: Sacram Liturgiam

## Why

*Sacram Liturgiam* addresses the urgent process of revising liturgical rites following Vatican II, establishing commissions to oversee new books and provisions while emphasizing hierarchical authority over all changes. The most direct, concrete, and universally recognizable object that encapsulates the entire concept of 'revised liturgy,' 'authorized worship,' and 'official rites' is a ceremonial book itself. This sculpture depicts a massive, authoritative liturgical volume (like a Roman Missal or Lectionary) with a visible cross on the cover, sculpted in materials that suggest permanence, authority, and renewed reverence, such as silver, ivory, and lapis lazuli, physically embodying the core of the Pope’s legislative action without resorting to abstract symbols of authority or change.
