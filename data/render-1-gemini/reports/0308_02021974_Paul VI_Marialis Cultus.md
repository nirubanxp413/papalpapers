# Render: Marialis Cultus

## Why

The choice for *Marialis Cultus* is a specific, unified sculptural form: **A Sacred Monstrance that cradles and holds a Rosary.** This single subject perfectly encapsulates the encyclical's core argument that Marian devotion (the Rosary) is an intrinsic element of Christian worship, but always subordinate to and oriented toward Christ (the Eucharist displayed in the Monstrance). It avoids abstraction by using recognizable devotional and liturgical items, showing Mary's relationship to her Son concretely rather than through symbolic voids or complex theological schemas.
