# Render: Anni Sacri

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Anni Sacri* because Pope Pius XII identifies the root of the world's postwar "formidable conflicts" as "the repudiation of God and contempt for His law." The encyclical does not prescribe social or economic adjustments; instead, its primary solution is a worldwide day of public prayer focused on Passion Sunday, at which the Pope himself will pray. The monstrance is the quintessential Catholic vessel that elevates and displays the Blessed Sacrament for adoration and public prayer, making it the perfect concrete object to embody the Pope’s call for spiritual renewal and divine intervention as the true antidote to atheism, conflict, and social hatred. A recognizable liturgical object clearly communicates this call for public adoration in a way that abstract geometry or a less sacred object could not.
