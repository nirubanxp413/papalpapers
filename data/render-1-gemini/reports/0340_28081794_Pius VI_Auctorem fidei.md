# Render: Auctorem fidei

## Why

I chose a single, large, ceremonial papal tiara as the best recognizable subject because *Auctorem fidei* centers on the Pope (Pius VI) asserting the supreme authority of the Roman Pontiff and the Universal Church against a schismatic diocesan synod and the secularization of ecclesial power. The triple-tiered crown of the tiara concretely embodies the papal office’s teaching, governing, and sanctifying authority that the Pistoia propositions directly attacked, making it an inevitable symbol of Christ’s continued sustentation of the faith through the established hierarchy. An abstract sculpture was rejected because it would fail to capture the specific historical and theological nature of this ecclesiological confrontation; a concrete, immediately legible object of high office is needed to communicate the decisive papal condemnation of the 'rash' and 'schismatic' errors.
