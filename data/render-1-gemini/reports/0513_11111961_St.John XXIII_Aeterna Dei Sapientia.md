# Render: Aeterna Dei Sapientia

## Why

This single-subject sculpture is a **double-arched bridge**, chosen to embody Pope John XXIII’s use of St. Leo’s theological legacy to build a path toward Christian unity. A bridge is an instantly recognizable structure that physically represents connection, overcoming separation, and facilitating passage, directly translating the encyclical’s focus on reconciliation and the Second Vatican Council’s role as a unifying instrument. Instead of abstracting unity into a vague geometric form, this concrete architectural subject clearly captures the *visible union under Peter's successor* called for in the text, illustrating the Pope's pastoral charity and his specific invitation to "separated Christians" to cross over and rejoin the Church.
