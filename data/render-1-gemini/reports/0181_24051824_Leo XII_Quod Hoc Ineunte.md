# Render: Quod Hoc Ineunte

## Why

A single, monumental *Holy Door* (Porta Sancta) is chosen for Pope Leo XII's *Quod Hoc Ineunte*, as it is the paramount symbol of the Jubilee Year and the direct threshold to the plenary indulgences and spiritual renewal central to the document. The text repeatedly emphasizes pilgrimage to Rome and visitation to the patriarchal basilicas—the physical and spiritual act of *passing through* the Holy Door. This specific sacred structure, featuring the distinctive bricked-up *recognitio* panel being unlocked or breached, perfectly embodies the themes of "divinely merciful remedy," "spiritual goods restored," and "liberation," moving beyond the abstract concepts into the definitive ritual action of entering grace and accessing the Apostolic Indulgence.
