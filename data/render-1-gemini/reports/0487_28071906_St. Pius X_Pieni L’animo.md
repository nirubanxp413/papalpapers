# Render: Pieni L’animo

## Why

The chosen sculptural form is an ornate *ferula* (papal staff or pastoral cross), as this object is the premier symbol of papal jurisdiction, universal authority, and the legitimate hierarchy within the Catholic Church. Because *Pieni l’animo* is fundamentally a document reasserting hierarchical order and commanding the strict obedience of priests to their bishops, the ferula encapsulates the source and enforcement of that very authority. It is chosen as a single, recognizable sacred object over abstract forms of order or a figurative statue to emphasize the institution and structure that Pius X demanded be respected, making his instruction visible and tangible.
