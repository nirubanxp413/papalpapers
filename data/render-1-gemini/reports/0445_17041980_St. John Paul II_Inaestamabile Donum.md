# Render: Inaestamabile Donum

## Why

I chose a sculpture of a pelican in her piety to represent *Inaestimabile Donum*. This subject was selected because it is a profound and traditional christological and eucharistic symbol, embodying Christ's sacrificial love for his Church and the faithful. This perfectly translates the encyclical’s central call for a "civilization of love" rooted in self-giving, contrasting with the utilitarianism and spiritual emptiness John Paul II addresses. A single recognizable sacred figure like the pelican clearly captures the text's emphasis on true sacrificial love and spiritual nourishment, whereas abstract forms or a cluster of symbols would have obscured this specific, potent theological message.
