# Render: Ecclesiam Dei

## Why

We chose the single, recognizable subject of St. Josaphat as the perfect embodiment of *Ecclesiam Dei*. The encyclical centers entirely on his martyrdom and its enduring significance as a catalyst for Church unity between East and West. A recognizable sacred figure was chosen over abstract symbols like a split cross or geometric union to ground the theological concepts in the concrete human sacrifice and holiness that Pius XI identifies as the true source of reunion. He is rendered as a Greek Catholic Archbishop in full Eastern vestments, specifically holding a distinctively Eastern-rite chalice—the very vehicle of Communion and the principal means toward reunion urged by the document. This single figure, isolated, speaks of individual witness as the foundation for ecclesial reconciliation, standing clear and legible without abstraction.
