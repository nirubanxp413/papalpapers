# Render: Probe Nostis

## Why

*Probe Nostis* addresses the urgent spiritual battle where the Catholic Church is beleaguered by widespread 'errors' but ultimately victorious through intense, widespread propagation. I chose the single subject of a magnificent, monumental Monstrance because it is the preeminent Catholic symbol for the public 'propagation' and display of the faith itself. It perfectly captures the Pope’s triumphant call: displaying divine truth above all attacks. An abstract form would fail to convey the specifically ecclesial, public, and victorious nature of this mission that is centered on showing Christ to the world, which the recognizable Monstrance embodies naturally and powerfully.
