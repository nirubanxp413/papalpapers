# Render: Communium Rerum

## Why

I chose a medieval shield bearing an embedded crozier and shield boss. A shield, especially in a medieval design referencing St. Anselm's time, perfectly embodies the dual themes of defense and offense against secular encroachment and theological deviation, like Modernism. The integrated crozier specifies that this is not secular combat but the defense of spiritual authority (the *liberty* of the Church) and orthodox doctrine (the truth). This is a single, instantly recognizable object that communicates protection, conflict, and Church identity without needing abstraction or environmental context.
