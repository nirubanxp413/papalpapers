# Render: Officii nostri

## Why

I chose a single, massive, partially cracked and bound ancient iron church key, supported by a heavy chain. This recognizable object embodies *Officii nostri* by visualizing the intersection of sacred authority and procedural limitation. The key is the traditional symbol of the binding and loosing power given to the Church, but the chain and the prominent crack signify the defined, procedural constraints that Benedict XIV placed upon the automatic right of asylum, clarifying when that key must be "locked" against asylum (for premeditated murder) and when it remains "open" (protecting the institution's integrity). The key is the concrete tool of access and refusal, not an abstract representation of law.
