# Render: Divini Redemptoris

## Why

The choice of Saint Joseph translates *Divini Redemptoris* because he is explicitly named as the universal patron of the Church’s fight against Communism and the protector of the true social order it attacks. Joseph represents everything the encyclical defends against materialistic ideology: the sanctity of the natural family, the dignity of honest labor, humble obedience to God’s plan, and the practical care for the Church and the poor. Choosing a figure of patriarchal protection and industrious virtue, rather than an abstract form, provides the most legible, human, and scripturally grounded counter-statement to the impersonal and Godless systems described in the text.
