# Render: Ineffabilis Deus

## Why

The Immaculate Conception is a declaration of Mary's unique and preserved sanctity from the first moment of her existence. The physical subject that most clearly embodies this singular grace, celebrated in the text and instantly legible in Catholic iconography, is the Virgin Mary herself, standing victorious over the symbol of sin. By choosing a specific devotional figure—"Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception"—we honor the single recognizable character required, rejecting abstraction and multi-object complexity in favor of the theological core, isolated in a sacred, premium 3D sculpture suitable for cinematic visualization.
