# Render: Exsurge Domine

## Why

The most recognizable and potent symbol summarizing *Exsurge Domine* is the "Fox in the Vineyard." The document explicitly opens by calling God and the saints to arise against the wild boars and foxes (specifically Luther and his errors) that are "destroying" and "devastating" Christ’s vineyard, a biblical metaphor for the Church and its doctrines. This image captures the perceived nature of the threat (destructive, cunning, invasive) and the urgency of the papal intervention to protect the sacred garden. A sculpted vineyard being ravaged by a fox is far more direct, visceral, and legible than abstract concepts of heresy, condemnation, or authority.
