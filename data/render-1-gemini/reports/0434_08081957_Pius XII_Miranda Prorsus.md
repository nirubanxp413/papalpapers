# Render: Miranda Prorsus

## Why

I chose an idealized, classic mid-century television set as the sculptural form because *Miranda Prorsus* explicitly focuses on the rapid, transformative impact of radio, film, and specifically television on the home and family. The encyclical addresses the dual potential of mass media—as a "gift of God" and a tool for corruption—by setting specific norms for its responsible use and evaluation through Catholic offices. Representing the television itself as a monumental, sacred sculpture directly materializes the central object of the Pope’s concern, emphasizing its inescapable physical presence within domestic life while rejecting abstract concepts in favor of immediate recognition.
