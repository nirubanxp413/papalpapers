# Render: Pascendi Dominici Gregis

## Why

Pascendi Dominici Gregis is a complex text detailing the internal threat of Modernism, but its central action is the definitive labeling and purification of the internal Church body. A simple shepherd, while common, fails to capture the authoritative, theological-philosophical counter-offensive. Instead, the Tiara is chosen: the supreme symbol of papal teaching authority, defining doctrine and cleansing error. This allows for a specific, identifiable object that embodies Pius X's assertion of Petrine authority to dismantle the "synthesis of all heresies" through concrete structure and truth, rejecting abstract geometry in favor of a powerful, recognizable ecclesiastical artifact.
