# Render: Dominus Iesus

## Why

I chose a single, walking **pilgrim's staff** as the sculptural subject. This object instantly encapsulates the encyclical's central metaphor: youth as a *journey* of following Christ. The staff, complete with a distinct gourd for sustenance and an implicit cross, embodies the ideas of vocation, spiritual progress, "growth in wisdom, stature, and grace," and the rejection of a static life, while directly addressing the 'Rich Young Man' narrative that John Paul II employs. An abstract form would fail to capture this clear sense of active discipleship and path-finding which is the core message of the letter.
