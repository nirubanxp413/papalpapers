# Render: Responses to Certain Questions of the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops Concerning Artificial Nutrition and Hydration

## Why

A simple earthenware vessel was chosen as the sculptural form because it perfectly embodies the document's central teaching: that artificial nutrition and hydration (symbolized by water) are ordinary and necessary care, and withdrawing them from a patient, regardless of their condition, violates their inherent human dignity (symbolized by the enduring, whole vessel). It is an archetypal, recognizable object representing sustaining life. An abstract form was rejected because the text addresses concrete, physical needs (nourishment and hydration) that cannot be reduced to vague geometry, requiring a tangible subject of care and provision.
