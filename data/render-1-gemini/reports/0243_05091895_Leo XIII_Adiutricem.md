# Render: Adiutricem

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Adiutricem* because it is the preeminent vessel for "showing forth" Christ, directly reflecting the encylical's central theme of Mary as Mediatrix who reveals and gives Jesus to the world. The monstrance represents the Eucharist as the ultimate source and summit of ecclesial unity, which Leo XIII actively seeks to restore, particularly with the Eastern churches. I rejected abstract forms because only the concrete and recognizable liturgical form of the monstrance could embody the specific theological reality of Christ’s presence and the Church’s visible unity that the document addresses.
