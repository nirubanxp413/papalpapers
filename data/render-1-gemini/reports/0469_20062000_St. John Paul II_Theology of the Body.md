# Render: Theology of the Body

## Why

The Apostolic Letter celebrating the Union of the Romanian Greek-Catholic Church with Rome calls for the renewal of ecclesial unity and the memory of its historical journey. The chosen sculptural subject is a traditional **Eastern-style pectoral cross (Crux Gemmata)**, as worn by a Greek-Catholic bishop, which symbolizes this specific Eastern tradition within the Catholic communion. The form is instantly recognizable and sacred, perfectly embodying the distinct, yet unified, character of the Romanian Greek-Catholic Church and its witness to unity in communion with the Holy See, making abstraction unnecessary and counterproductive.
