# Render: Pacem, Dei Munus Pulcherrimum

## Why

A single, central pillar of rough, hammered iron represents the fragile yet foundational post-WWI reality. Growing from this pillar are intertwined branches and leaves of polished bronze and warm gold, culminating in a single, blooming stylized rose made of deep ruby glass. This combination captures the encyclical’s core message: true peace, represented by the thriving golden rose of Christian charity and reconciliation (as urged by Benedict XV), cannot be built abstractly or through signatures alone, but must flourish organically and enduringly from the concrete, hard realities and scars of conflict, just as charity must overcome the cold iron of division and mutual forgiveness.
