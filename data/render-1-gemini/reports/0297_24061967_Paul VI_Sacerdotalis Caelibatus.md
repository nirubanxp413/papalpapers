# Render: Sacerdotalis Caelibatus

## Why

The choice of a high-quality, singular, consecrated candle (like a Pâchal candle) represents the clerical commitment and sacred identity defined in *Sacerdotalis Caelibatus*. The candle symbolizes a life 'poured out' for God and the Church—a sacrificial devotion that radiates the light of Christ (the flame) through a body (the wax) that is totally and irrevocably dedicated. It avoids abstraction by providing a universally recognizable object of religious devotion that encapsulates the priest's undivided heart and consecrated service without resorting to multi-object compositions or vague geometry.
