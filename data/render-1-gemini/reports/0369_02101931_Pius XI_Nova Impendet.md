# Render: Nova Impendet

## Why

I chose a single, worn **collection box** as the sculptural subject because it perfectly encapsulates the Pope's primary response to the "new scourge" of unemployment. Instead of abstractly representing the economic crisis or the complex idea of "Crusade," I focused on the specific, practical mechanism the document calls for: collecting and distributing alms through the Church to directly aid the poor and children. This object is instantly recognizable as a "devotional item/sacred object" for charity, fitting the selection rubric, and rejects any abstract representation of the broader theological concepts by centering the concrete, physical tool for the "Crusade of piety and of love." A single collection box allows for an isolated, highly detailed render on a transparent background, satisfying the single-subject and isolation tests.

The selection of the collection box provides a strong, concrete representation of the document's call for charitable works, and the text explanation supports this choice well. However, the resulting image has failed to meet the specific technical constraints provided. The prompt explicitly mandated a transparent background, but the generation includes a baked-in checker pattern instead. Furthermore, the instructions strictly prohibited any text or lettering on the sculpture, yet the text 'CRUSADE OF CHARITY' appears prominently on the front. I am regenerated the image to correct these technical errors by enforcing a truly transparent background and ensuring the object is free from any text inscriptions.

To translate this vision into the final sculptural form, I will adjust the model to target a clean, high-end 3D render while meticulously addressing the technical constraints and the stylistic requirements of Nova Impendet. I will begin by eliminating the baked-in checkerboard pattern and replacing it with true transparency, using an alpha channel to ensure a flawless isolated result. Next, I must remove all text, specifically the "CRUSADE OF CHARITY" inscription, and fill that area with smooth, dark wood texture that is perfectly consistent with the surrounding aged grain. To enhance the relief, I will raise and broaden the carved hand symbols to emphasize the theological themes of aid and charity. Finally, I will strictly adhere to the established visual parameters by using a 3/4 high-contrast angle, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, and a polished granite plinth to complete this luxury sacred-object finish.
