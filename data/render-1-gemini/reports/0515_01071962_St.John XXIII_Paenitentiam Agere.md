# Render: Paenitentiam Agere

## Why

I chose a classic monastic bell to represent *Paenitentiam Agere* because it is a single, instantly recognizable object that embodies the encyclical’s core message: a call to prayer, conversion, and penitential preparation. Just as the bell’s clear, resonant ring calls the faithful to prayer, so too did John XXIII’s letter call the Church to spiritual readiness before the Second Vatican Council. A bell is a material tool of spiritual discipline, fitting the Pope's insistence on *external penance* and structured prayer. Choosing this concrete subject avoids the vague geometry of abstraction and provides a clear, symbolic anchor for the text's historical call.
