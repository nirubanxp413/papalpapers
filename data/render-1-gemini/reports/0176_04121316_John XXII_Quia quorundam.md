# Render: Quia quorundam

## Why

A set of keys was chosen as the sculptural subject to embody *Quia quorundam*, which focuses on the Apostolic See's perpetual authority to declare, revise, and adapt rulings regarding religious rules and poverty. The keys directly represent the "keys of the kingdom," a symbol of papal authority and power, which John XXII explicitly defends against critics who sought to limit his power by distinguishing the "key of knowledge" from the "key of power." An abstract sculpture was rejected because it would fail to provide the immediate, historical, and theological recognition inherent in the specific symbol of the keys, which clearly grounds the encyclical's arguments about the nature and scope of papal jurisdictional authority.
