# Render: Graves De Communi Re

## Why

I chose a single, worn **broom** as the sculptural subject. A broom is universally recognized as a tool of humble, practical, daily labor and cleaning, perfectly encapsulating Leo XIII’s definition of Christian Democracy as "beneficent charitable action toward the people" and "organized...charity-driven action on behalf of the poor." It rejects abstraction or complex religious symbols in favor of a concrete object that signifies active, hands-on care and the dignity of labor, rooted in spiritual motivation rather than political ideology or systemic upheaval, distinguishing it sharply from Socialism.
