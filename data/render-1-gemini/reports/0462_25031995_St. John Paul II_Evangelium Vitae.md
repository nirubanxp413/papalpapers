# Render: Evangelium Vitae

## Why

I chose a monstrance to represent *Evangelium Vitae* because it is the preeminent Catholic vessel for displaying and protecting the Eucharist, which is the "source and summit" of the "Gospel of Life." The circular form, often shaped as a sunburst, visually centers the sacred host, the very presence of Christ—who is Life itself—making it immediately legible as a sacred object without relying on abstract concepts. This concrete liturgical item perfectly embodies the encyclical's central defense of human life as a sacred reality meant for adoration, protection, and display, avoiding any vague or non-representational geometry.
