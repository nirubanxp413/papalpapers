# Render: Fin Dal Principio

## Why

I chose a single, traditional mitre to represent *Fin Dal Principio* because it is the preeminent, universally recognized symbol of episcopal authority and identity. The encyclical is a direct appeal to the Italian bishops, asserting the absolute necessity of maintaining a distinct, supernatural priestly character against the encroaching influence of secular modernity. By rendering the mitre not as worn, but as an isolated, monumental, almost archaic artifact, the sculpture visualizes the document's central argument: that the Catholic hierarchy and priesthood are ancient, divinely established, and must remain unyieldingly separate from worldly or political innovation. An abstract form would fail to capture this specific pastoral and authoritative dynamic that Leo XIII so forcefully defends.
