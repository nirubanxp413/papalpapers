# Render: Praedecessores Nostros

## Why

The most direct translation of *Praedecessores Nostros* is an object that embodies the Pope's primary action: the *urgent* collection of alms to relieve acute hunger. A humble, worn alms-collecting vessel—such as a common market basket that has been elevated and consecrated for sacred duty—best captures this tension between scarcity, urgent charity, and papal mandate. This single recognizable subject is chosen over abstraction or complex allegories (like the storm, or purely spiritual indulgence) because it is the concrete means by which the encyclical's primary call to action is realized for the suffering people of Ireland. The basket is immediately legible as a receptacle for tangible relief, avoiding the ambiguity of abstract symbols.
