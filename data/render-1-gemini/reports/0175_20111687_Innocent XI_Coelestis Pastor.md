# Render: Coelestis Pastor

## Why

I chose a single, monolithic iron safe to embody *Coelestis Pastor* because it represents the Church's proactive, defensive role in guarding sound doctrine and spiritual discipline. The safe symbolizes the containment of both the sacred deposit of faith and the dangerous 'poisonous ideas' (represented by the sealed books and condemned propositions) that Innocent XI locks away and condemns to 'perpetual confinement.' Abstract concepts of spiritual danger or containment are too vague; the safe is a universally recognized object of protection, sequestration, and final judgment on hidden threats. The heavy, impenetrable mass of the iron safe directly translates the pope's authoritative and definitive action against Molinos and his dangerous 'prayer of quiet.'
