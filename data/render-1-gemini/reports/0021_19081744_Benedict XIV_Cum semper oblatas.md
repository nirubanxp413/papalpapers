# Render: Cum semper oblatas

## Why

I chose a **liturgical chalice with an elevated host** as the single recognizable subject for *Cum semper oblatas* because it is the quintessential, immediately legible symbol of the Mass, the *pro populo* and conventual obligations that the encyclical addresses. This single subject captures the theological focus of priest, offering, and community without resorting to abstract geometry, complex diorama, or symbolic fragments. A simple cross would be too general; a book would lack the necessary emphasis on specific offering; and an abstract form would fail the core requirement of recognizability, whereas the chalice and host concretely instantiate the precise disciplinary and spiritual duty Pope Benedict XIV enforces.
