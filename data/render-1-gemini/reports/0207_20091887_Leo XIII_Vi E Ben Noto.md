# Render: Vi E Ben Noto

## Why

For *Vi E Ben Noto*, which explicitly calls the faithful to battle a spiritual threat targeting the heart of the Church in Italy using the Rosary as its primary weapon, the subject of a monolithic shield best translates the meaning into a single, concrete form. A shield is instantly recognizable as an object of spiritual defense and protection, which directly embodies Leo XIII's remedy against "the sects." The sculpture integrates the visual structure of the Rosary into the shield itself, avoiding the abstract concept of prayer by materializing it as the physical force required for spiritual combat and the defense of the faith against indifference and attack.
