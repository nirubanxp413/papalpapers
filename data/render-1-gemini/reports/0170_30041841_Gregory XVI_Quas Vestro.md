# Render: Quas Vestro

## Why

I chose a single, worn liturgical aspergillum (holy water sprinkler) as the recognizable subject to embody *Quas Vestro*. The aspergillum represents the "full sacramental rites and blessing" that Pope Gregory XVI is explicitly addressing and forbidding priests from performing in mixed marriages without a dispensation. Instead of an abstract form representing 'conflict' or 'mixed beliefs,' this concrete object directly symbolizes the illicit sacramental action that triggered the papal document. The sculpture's isolated and slightly worn condition visualizes the Pope's reluctant and constrained concession of a "passive presence" while the item itself testifies to the "illicit and destructive" misuse of a sacred blessing. The request explicitly rejects abstraction and requires a single recognizable subject, making a specific liturgical tool perfectly suited to materialize this focused legal and theological correction.
