# Render: Address to Midwives on the Nature of Their Profession

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a pair of cupped human hands protecting a small, emerging flame, which represents the midwife as an "apostle" guarding the sacred threshold of life. This concrete, recognizable form captures the dual themes of protective care and divine law without resorting to abstraction. The hands symbolize the physical and moral responsibility of the midwife to defend God's order, while the flame embodies the spark of life and natural family planning ("agenesic periods"), making the core argument about the inviolability of the unborn immediately legible.
