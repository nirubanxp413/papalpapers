# Render: Spectata Fides

## Why

I chose a single, open book, specifically a Gospel, resting upon a traditional, ornamented desk as the sculptural subject. The encyclical *Spectata Fides* champions the absolute necessity of integrating religious instruction with secular learning in Catholic voluntary schools. The open book symbolizes education, but the Gospel text (suggested by the cross or Christ icon on the visible page) specifically represents the divine revelation and religious formation that Pope Leo XIII insists must be central to all proper schooling. This recognizable object immediately conveys the idea of learning, knowledge, and faith, rejecting abstract concepts for a familiar symbol of study and scripture.
