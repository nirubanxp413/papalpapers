# Render: Christi Matri

## Why

The Rosary is the single most important and recognizable devotional item corresponding to *Christi Matri*, given Paul VI's specific and repeated appeal to "intensify devotion to Mary through the Rosary" as the means to avert a "spreading danger of war." I chose a stylized, monumental Rosary sculpture over an abstract form or a generic figure of Mary because it embodies the concrete spiritual *action* requested by the Pope (prayer) and identifies the specific tool of that prayer. A single figure might fail to clearly convey the theme of active intercession against violence, whereas the Rosary, rendered as a monumental, armor-like object, clearly visualizes the Pope's claim that spiritual weapons are necessary to secure peace.
