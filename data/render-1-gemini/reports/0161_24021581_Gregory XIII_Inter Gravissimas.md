# Render: Inter Gravissimas

## Why

The core purpose of *Inter Gravissimas* is the profound correction of the calendar—taking measurement away from inaccurate, accumulated error and aligning it with the true cosmic reality of the vernal equinox. An abstraction of time would be illegible, but an astrolabe, a specific, recognizable instrument for calculating celestial cycles (including the epacts and the exact moment of the equinox described), perfectly captures this technical correction of the universe's measurement by the Church. It represents the intricate calculations, the necessary precision, and the Authority of Pope Gregory XIII to physically realign human calculation with the heavens, presented as a definitive, unified object.
