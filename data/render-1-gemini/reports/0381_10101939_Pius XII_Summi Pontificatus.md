# Render: Summi Pontificatus

## Why

I chose a single, sovereign crown featuring two unified crowns—one celestial (spiritual) and one terrestrial (temporal)—merged into a single, unified structure. This singular, recognizable object embodies the encyclical's core argument that Christ must be recognized as King over both souls and societies, thus correcting the error of modern secular states claiming absolute, unchecked authority. By presenting this integrated sovereignty as a single, physical artifact rather than an abstract concept or multiple symbols, the sculpture visually resolves the central theological and social conflict *Summi Pontificatus* addresses with immediate clarity. Abstraction was rejected because a crown is instantly recognizable as a symbol of authority, making the claim for divine sovereignty concrete and unambiguous in a way that non-representational forms cannot.
