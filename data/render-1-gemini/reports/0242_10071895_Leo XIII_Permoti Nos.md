# Render: Permoti Nos

## Why

I chose a single, massive **shepherd's crozier** for *Permoti Nos*. This encyclical is Leo XIII's powerful, corrective intervention to resolve discord among Belgian bishops and re-unify the Catholic community under clear, authoritative teaching. The crozier is the primary sacred symbol of pastoral authority, guidance, and episcopal unity, perfectly capturing the Pope's forceful direction to restore concord and lead the flock away from the dangers of independent opinion and Socialism. Abstraction was rejected because a vague geometric symbol cannot communicate the personal, directive, and unifying role of the shepherd leading the flock as immediately and definitively as this recognized liturgical object.
