# Render: Le voci che da tutti

## Why

The most direct and legible translation of *Le voci che da tutti* is St. Joseph himself, who is formally designated as the protector of Vatican II and recognized as the historical patron of the Universal Church. We reject abstraction because only the recognizable figure of Joseph can embody the century of accrued devotion, papal declarations, and personal guardianship that are central to the document. The sculpture visualizes this by presenting Joseph holding a staff—his symbol of guidance and flowering with lilies—which represents his ongoing, active, and sacred protection over the Church and the council.
