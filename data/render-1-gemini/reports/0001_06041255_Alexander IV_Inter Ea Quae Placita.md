# Render: Inter Ea Quae Placita

## Why

I chose a monumental, glowing lantern for this papal bull. The document’s argument centers on "multitudo sapientium" as the health of kingdoms and the king's desire that his realms be illuminated by the "inextinguishable light of wisdom" via the new university. This lantern concretely embodies that specific concept of wisdom as a guiding, persistent illumination for governance, far more recognizable and resonant than any abstract representation of intellect or a collection of books.
