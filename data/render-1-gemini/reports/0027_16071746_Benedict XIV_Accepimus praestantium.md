# Render: Accepimus praestantium

## Why

I chose a large, ornate liturgical altar cross made of gilded silver for the sculptural subject. *Accepimus praestantum* specifically addresses the removal or displacement of the crucifix from altars in favor of other images. The encyclical reaffirms the central and visible requirement of the cross during the Mass, making the object itself the literal and symbolic focal point of the text's argument. An abstract form could not capture the specific liturgical violation the Pope is correcting; only the physical cross, restored to its rightful, prominent place, conveys the encyclical's assertion of theological priority.
