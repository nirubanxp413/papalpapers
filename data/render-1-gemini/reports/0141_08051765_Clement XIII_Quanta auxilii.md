# Render: Quanta auxilii

## Why

I chose a single, reinforced mace with a bishop's mitre at its apex to represent *Quanta auxilii*. This form directly embodies the central tension of the document: the need for gentle, spiritual leadership (the mitre) alongside firm, decisive action (the mace) against those assailing the Church. By unifying these two symbols into one physical object, the sculpture captures Pope Clement XIII's dual instruction to maintain humility while exercising strong episcopal authority to protect the faithful and discipline dissent, rejecting abstract concepts for a powerful, tangible emblem of spiritual defense and authority.
