# Render: On Religious Life

## Why

I chose a single, traditional religious habit as the sculptural subject. It is instantly recognizable as the symbol of consecrated life, which Pope Paul VI defends against being obscured by the emphasis on the lay vocation. The habit explicitly represents the three vows of poverty, chastity, and obedience (materialized in the simple fabric and the cincture with knots), while its unified form embodies the disciplined governance and commitment to the founder’s spirit that the document mandates. Choosing an abstract representation, such as geometric shapes for the vows, would fail the legibility test and lose the concrete connection to the lived reality of the religious institutes that the document addresses.
