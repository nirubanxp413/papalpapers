# Render: Etsi Multa

## Why

The most fitting subject for *Etsi Multa* is a Bishop’s Crozier (pastoral staff) that is being deliberately broken or sheared. The encyclical details the forceful secular takeover of episcopal power, the expulsion of legitimate bishops, and the installation of schismatic pseudo-bishops, actions which *break* and splinter the true authority of the Church. By rendering a physical fracture through the central symbol of pastoral leadership, the sculpture concretely embodies the systematic state-sponsored assault on the Church’s hierarchy described by Pius IX. This form was chosen over abstract geometry because it instantly identifies the specifically clerical and authority-based nature of the attack, grounding the spiritual crisis in a universally recognizable sacred object.
