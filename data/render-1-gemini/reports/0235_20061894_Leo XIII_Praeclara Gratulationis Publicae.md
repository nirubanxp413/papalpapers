# Render: Praeclara Gratulationis Publicae

## Why

This single subject, a traditional shepherd's crook, was chosen because it embodies Pope Leo XIII's central call for unity and pastoral gathering. The crozier is the ultimate symbol of the Church's unifying authority and care for all people, explicitly referencing the concentric circles of appeal in the document: gathering those separated, those wandering, and those in danger. Unlike an abstract representation of "unity" or a complex diorama of various churches, the crozier is a single, instantly recognizable devotional object that carries the theological weight of Christ's prayer for one flock, making it the most legible and concrete symbol for *Praeclara Gratulationis Publicae*.
