# Render: Quas Primas

## Why

To capture the central argument of *Quas Primas*, which establishes the universal and social kingship of Christ over both individuals and civil authorities, I chose a monumental, jewel-encrusted crown. This single, recognizable object directly communicates "kingship" and "reign" in a political and public sense, fitting the encyclical’s focus on Christ’s authority extending beyond spiritual interiority. Abstract forms like geometric shapes were rejected because they cannot convey authority or sovereignty clearly, while complex dioramas were avoided to maintain the visual focus on the solitary, defining symbol of the feast of Christ the King instituted by Pius XI.
