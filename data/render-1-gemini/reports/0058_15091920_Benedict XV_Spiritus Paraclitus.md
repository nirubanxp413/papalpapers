# Render: Spiritus Paraclitus

## Why

I chose a Lion (of Judah) sculpted directly from ancient stone as the single recognizable subject. *Spiritus Paraclitus* is not just about the theological value of Scripture, but its complete historical integrity, inerrancy, and rugged authenticity, defending it against modern critics. A lion, an essential biblical archetype associated with strength, truth, and Christ (the Lion of Judah), embodies the absolute authority and powerful spiritual force that Benedict XV attributes to the entire Bible, as taught by St. Jerome. I rejected abstract forms like light or open books because they lack the necessary monumental density, ancient authority, and immediate physical presence required to symbolize the indestructible and historical nature of the divine text.
