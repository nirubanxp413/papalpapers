# Render: Pastoralis

## Why

To translate the message of *Pastoralis*—which calls Portuguese bishops to unified action, promotes religious societies for overseas missionary work, and champions Catholic doctrine as the bedrock of civic virtue and social order against attack—I have chosen the single recognizable subject of a robust, ornate key. This key embodies Leo XIII’s central argument: that the Church and its doctrines are the "efficacious remedy" that unlock true social stability and guard the "highest principles" from attack. It symbolizes the Church's authority, its role in opening the way for spiritual formation through missionary orders, and its foundational position as the secure mechanism that binds virtue to civil society. A key offers a potent, concrete, and universally legible metaphor for authority and access, avoiding the ambiguity of abstract forms.
