# Render: Inaestamabile Donum

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this sculpture because *Inaestamabile Donum* is primarily a corrective document focusing on reverence toward the Blessed Sacrament and the proper, normed conduct of eucharistic worship. The document explicitly mandates the "correct manner of genuflection" and addresses "irreverence toward the Blessed Sacrament," making the monstrance, which displays the consecrated Host, the ultimate focal point of adoration. I rejected abstraction or multi-object scenes because the encyclical’s core message is about maintaining clarity and distinct roles; a single, highly refined liturgical vessel powerfully symbolizes the required order and sacred focus without ambiguity.
