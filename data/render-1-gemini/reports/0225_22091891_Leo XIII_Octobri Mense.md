# Render: Octobri Mense

## Why

For *Octobri Mense*, which establishes Mary as the necessary intermediary and frames the Rosary as the indispensable spiritual remedy against the afflictions facing the Church, I chose a singular, monolithic Rosary carved from polished black onyx. This form was selected over an abstract representation because the Rosary is the precise, concrete devotional object the encyclical exhorts the faithful to use. A single recognizable object was required; a group of figures or an abstract form representing 'grace' would fail to communicate the specific, practical remedy Leo XIII proposes. The Rosary embodies the concepts of persevering prayer, communal devotion, and the physical link between the believer and Mary, fulfilling the criteria for immediate legibility and theological fit.
