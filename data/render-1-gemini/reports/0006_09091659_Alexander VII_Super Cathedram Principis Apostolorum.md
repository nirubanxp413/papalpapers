# Render: Super Cathedram Principis Apostolorum

## Why

A single, massive stone key was chosen to symbolize the transfer of pastoral authority and jurisdictional 'faculties' from the Chair of Peter (the Petrine Office) to the newly appointed Apostolic Vicar in Tonkin. The key is a universally recognized religious symbol representing both Christ's delegation of authority to Peter and, in this context, the specific delegation to the Vicar to govern the Church in a foreign land. A set of abstract gears or symbols of permission was rejected as it would fail the 'recognizable object' and 'sacred legibility' tests; the key is instantly legible as an icon of authority, stewardship, and responsibility.
