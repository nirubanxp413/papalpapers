# Render: Rite Expiatis

## Why

A single, monumental Tau Cross was chosen as the sculptural subject to embody *Rite Expiatis*. St. Francis of Assisi adopted the Tau Cross as his own signature and seal, symbolizing conversion, the cross of Christ, and absolute poverty; it represents the "Franciscan spirit" the encyclical calls to revive. Rejecting abstract form, this recognizable, concrete subject instantly connects the historical Francis and his foundational charism directly to the Pope’s call for spiritual and social reform in 1926, standing as a stark, sacred antidote to materialism.
