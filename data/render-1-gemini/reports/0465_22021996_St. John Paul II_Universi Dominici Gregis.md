# Render: Universi Dominici Gregis

## Why

I chose a single, monumental papal election key to represent *Universi Dominici Gregis*. The core of the Apostolic Constitution concerns the strict rules and absolute secrecy of the Conclave, the method for locking the cardinals in to elect the new Pope. This physical key, isolated on a plinth, concretely symbolizes the unlocking of the Papacy (represented by the tri-regno) through the spiritual process of the Conclave, which occurs "in freedom" and under the locking action of the rules established by John Paul II. An abstract sculpture was rejected because this sacred mechanism must be universally legible to the viewer.
