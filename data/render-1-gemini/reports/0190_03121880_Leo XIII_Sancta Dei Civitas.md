# Render: Sancta Dei Civitas

## Why

The choice for *Sancta Dei Civitas* is a single, large, ornate **collection box (alms box)**, a recognizable object that directly embodies the Pope's primary call for material almsgiving to support missionary societies. It moves the abstract idea of "support" into a concrete, familiar object of devotional participation, representing the combined generosity requested of the faithful to fund new missions. This specific form was chosen over others like a missionary figure (too narrative/tableau-focused) or abstract light (rejected) because the box is a legible, stand-alone vessel that symbolizes both the practical resource and the spiritual sacrifice of the donor, unified in one object without abstract clutter.
