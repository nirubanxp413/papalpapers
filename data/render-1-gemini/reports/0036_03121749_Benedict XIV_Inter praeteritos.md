# Render: Inter praeteritos

## Why

The Pope systematically addresses the "disputed questions" and ambiguities facing confessors during the Jubilee, using past theological consensus to establish clarity. This act of sorting, clarifying, and decisive categorization is best visualized not through abstraction, but through the recognizable object of a vintage postal sorting unit or "pigeonhole." This structure, with its clear, defined boundaries, translates the abstract theological "resolving" of canonical questions into a tangible, segmented, and organized physical form, emphasizing the Pope's methodical approach to spiritual jurisprudence. The object is instantly identifiable and inherently structured, perfectly embodying the encyclical's drive for order and canonical certainty.
