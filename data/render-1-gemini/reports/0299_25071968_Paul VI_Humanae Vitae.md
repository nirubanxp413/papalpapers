# Render: Humanae Vitae

## Why

*Humanae Vitae* emphasizes the sacred link between conjugal love and the transmission of human life, declaring each marital act must remain open to procreation. A stylized sculpture of a single **nest** containing eggs powerfully captures this themes of natural law, hospitality, new life, and the marital union as a protective vessel for procreation. This subject is universally recognizable, avoiding abstract geometric forms that would lack the encyclical’s pastoral and organic resonance, and is presented as a single, coherent devotional object without forming a narrative diorama.
