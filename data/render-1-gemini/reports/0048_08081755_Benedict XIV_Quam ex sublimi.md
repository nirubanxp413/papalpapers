# Render: Quam ex sublimi

## Why

I chose a solitary, lit, bronze liturgical lantern to embody *Quam ex sublimi* because it powerfully translates the document's instruction for a clear, unbroken line of authority and spiritual governance. The lantern signifies the continuous presence of leadership (the Vicar Apostolic or their designated successor) required to maintain the sacramental life of the faithful in vast and far-flung mission territories, including the East Indies and Mesopotamia. By rejecting abstract concepts of structure in favor of a single, functional, and recognizable sacred object, the sculpture makes the practical yet spiritually vital concept of non-interrupted apostolic governance immediately tangible and relatable to the viewer.
