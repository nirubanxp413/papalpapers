# Render: Paenitemini

## Why

A rough-hewn, oversized, weathered stone basin is chosen as the singular sculptural subject to embody *Paenitemini*. While the document covers broad themes of metanoia, sacrifice, fasting, and social justice, all of these penitential actions find a common locus in the foundational reality of communal and individual cleansing. The basin, especially when rendered in heavily eroded stone and showing clear signs of ancient, repetitive use, becomes a concrete, universally recognizable vessel that immediately visualizes the "total change of heart" and "purification" at the core of true penance. It emphasizes the foundational act of washing, purification, and renewal, capturing the gravity, utility, and enduring nature of the spiritual metanoia the encyclical demands without reverting to abstract geometry or complex theological symbolism. Its material presence as a heavy, simple object of practical spiritual hygiene provides the necessary grounding for the encyclical’s pastoral reorganisation of penitential life, presenting a sturdy, authentic tool for a profound spiritual reality.
