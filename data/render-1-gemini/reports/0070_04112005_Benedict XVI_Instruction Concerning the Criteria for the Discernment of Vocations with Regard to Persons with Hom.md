# Render: Instruction Concerning the Criteria for the Discernment of Vocations with Regard to Persons with Homosexual Tendencies in View of Their Admission to the Seminary and to Holy Orders

## Why

A crozier (pastoral staff) was chosen because it is the preeminent and instantly recognizable symbol of episcopal authority, shepherdly care, and spiritual governance in the Catholic Church, representing the figure of the Bishop himself. This specific Instruction places ultimate responsibility for the "discernment of vocations" squarely on the Bishop, tasked with guarding the integrity of the priesthood and deciding who is suitable for admission to Holy Orders. The crook of the staff, embodying both leadership and the careful protection of the flock, directly translates the Bishop’s duty to ensure candidates truly manifest affective maturity and conformity to Christ, while upholding the Church's binding norms for ordination.
