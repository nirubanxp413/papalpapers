# Render: Quemadmodum preces

## Why

The encyclical, *Quemadmodum preces*, asserts the Church's exclusive authority to compose liturgical prayers, particularly those for secular rulers within the Mass. To materialize this concept as a single recognizable object, I chose a monumental, closed Liturgical Book (representing the established texts of the Mass). Crucially, the book is sealed by a large, ornate Papal Tiara, symbolizing Pope Benedict XIV’s assertion of supreme ecclesiastical jurisdiction over liturgical matters and the rejection of secular interference in prayer formulation.
