# Render: Reflections at Nazareth

## Why

I chose a simple, handcrafted wooden workbench because it immediately represents "work," one of the three core pedagogical lessons Pope Paul VI identifies at Nazareth. The Nazareth address centers on Jesus’ hidden life, transforming mundane human labor into a school of sanctity. This single, recognizable domestic and industrial object captures the synthesis of daily life and the Gospel, rejecting abstraction for the concrete reality of Christ's earthly environment and the universal human experience of work.
