# Render: Lumen Fidei

## Why

I chose a monstrance to embody *Lumen Fidei* because this single sacred object functions as a physical lens of faith, designed specifically to hold and visually amplify the sacramental reality of Christ. While abstract forms could not convey the encyclical’s focus on seeing Christ’s truth, the monstrance provides an instantly recognizable form that captures the encyclical’s definition of faith as both a light that guides history and an encounter with a present, tangible Love. A monstrance is structurally and conceptually centered on this unifying mystery, rejecting the abstraction *Lumen Fidei* critiques while functioning as the Church’s archetypal "icon of faith" that is seen and adored.
