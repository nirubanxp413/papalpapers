# Render: Charitas

## Why

A fractured bishop's mitre was chosen to symbolize the internal division and illegitimacy caused by the schismatic consecrations, rather than opting for a generic symbol of authority or an abstract representation of unity. This singular, broken object captures the specific violation and resulting canonical fissure that Pope Pius VI sought to condemn, visually representing the sacred authority that has been invalidated. Choosing this concrete, recognizable object ensures a direct and unambiguous connection to the encyclical's central theme of fractured ecclesiastical hierarchy and the nullification of the new, illegitimate bishops.
