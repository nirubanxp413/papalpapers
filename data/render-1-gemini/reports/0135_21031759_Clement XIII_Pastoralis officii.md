# Render: Pastoralis officii

## Why

The chosen sculptural subject is a pair of heavy, interlocking bronze keys. These keys represent "ecclesiastical immunity" and the power of sanctuary—the traditional function of the Church as a place of refuge. The keys are a universally recognizable object, immediately legibility to any viewer, avoiding abstraction while powerfully conveying the document's central theme of restricting access to sanctuary for specific offenders. They are a single, unified subject (the pair joined) suitable for the isolation test, and the material allows for a premium, physically-based render.
