# Render: Singulari Nos

## Why

To translate Gregory XVI's condemnation of Lamennais's *Paroles d'un croyant* into a single recognizable sculptural form, I chose a shepherd's crozier (staff) from which a serpent is actively being removed or cast down. This form perfectly embodies the encyclical's central theme of re-establishing obedience and pastoral authority over error and rebellion. The crozier is the primary symbol of the bishop's authority to protect his flock; the serpent represents the "wicked pamphlet" and the spirit of "sedition" and false prophecy that Gregory warns is "dissolv[ing] bonds of obedience." The act of removing the serpent represents the Pope's condemnation and the call for bishops to "suppress the book" and "protect their flocks" from its poisonous influence. This concrete subject was chosen over abstraction because it immediately communicates the conflict and the reassertion of ecclesial order without ambiguity, grounding the theological and social crisis in recognizable Catholic iconography.
