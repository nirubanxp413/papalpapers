# Render: Gravibus Ecclesiae

## Why

For *Gravibus Ecclesiae*, which addresses a global Jubilee in a time of severe moral crisis and limited movement, I selected a highly ornate, ancient-style key as the singular recognizable subject. This object embodies the "power of the keys"—the Church's authority to grant plenary indulgences and loose sins—which is the core mechanism of the Jubilee the Pope is activating worldwide despite his confinement. This specific concrete item is immediately legible as both a literal key and a potent religious symbol of binding and loosing, effectively communicating the encyclical's message of extraordinary spiritual grace and universal access without requiring abstract geometry or environmental context.
