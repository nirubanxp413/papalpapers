# Render: Ubi Primum

## Why

The chosen subject is a single, large, ornate bell, which historically calls religious communities together and regulates monastic life. This concrete object perfectly captures *Ubi Primum’s* purpose: Pope Pius IX is sounding a clear "alarm" to the heads of all religious orders, commanding them to rouse their members to renewal and necessary reform. A bell was selected because it is universally recognized as a tool of collective awakening, discipline, and unity, avoiding the vagueness of abstract geometry while directly embodying the encyclical's call for order, revitalization, and obedience.
