# Render: Exponi nobis nuper

## Why

I chose a highly ornate, antique academic mace as the single recognizable subject. A university mace perfectly embodies the encyclical's theme: the official, papal-authorized establishment of a *studium generale* (university) and the transfer of academic authority. The mace combines the concept of knowledge (represented by the globe or lantern structure) with official authority (granted by the Pope), making it a concrete, historically accurate, and immediately legible object, superior to abstract symbols of "truth" or "instruction." The intricate details allow for a premium, sculptural execution on a transparent background, reflecting the high-end 3D render requirement.
