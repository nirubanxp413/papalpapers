# Render: Laetitiae Sanctae

## Why

I have chosen a sacred heart as the sculptural subject for *Laetitiae Sanctae*. It perfectly synthesizes the encyclical's core argument that the Rosary, by immersing the faithful in the contemplation of Christ's life (His heart), serves as the unique spiritual remedy for modern social evils. The single heart physically represents the union of the Joyful, Sorrowful, and Glorious mysteries that Leo XIII systematically utilizes, and the surrounding thorn cluster embodies both the collective "battalion" of spiritual resistance and the structured mechanism of the Rosary bead chain, translating an abstract theology into an universally recognized devotional form.
