# Render: In eminenti

## Why

For Clement XII’s condemnation of Freemasonry in *In eminenti*, which focuses heavily on the dangers of its secrecy and 'oaths', a heavy, ancient-style key is the most fitting recognizable object. This single form captures the essence of the encyclical's concerns: the *secret keys* of knowledge, ritual, and association that the 'Liberi Muratori' guarded from the Church and civil authorities. A key is a universal, concrete object representing access, security, and (specifically in this context) the guarding of forbidden secrets, far more direct than any abstract representation of 'secrecy' or 'authority' and adhering strictly to the single-subject constraint.
