# Render: Ordinatio Sacerdotalis

## Why

I chose the Twelve Apostles, sculpted in a unified frieze-like block, to embody *Ordinatio Sacerdotalis*. This single recognizable subject directly represents Christ's foundational act of choosing twelve men as the basis for the priesthood, which the document cites as the definitive theological reason. The explicit inclusion of twelve male figures (Peter at the center) makes the meaning instantly legible, rejecting abstraction or indirect symbolism.
