# Render: Dignum Arbitramur et Congruum

## Why

I chose a single, open and elevated key as the sculptural subject. The key is a universally recognized symbol of access, authority, and the unlocking of doors. *Dignum Arbitramur et Congruum* addresses a dynamic of access: the key ensures that the 'door' of the general studium remains open to qualified scholars, preventing further, redundant examinations from acting as new locks or barriers once their competence has been verified. A key represents the legitimate authority granting entry, directly mirroring the Pope's decree validating the Salamanca examination for teaching elsewhere; it was chosen over abstract forms because its concrete function and symbolic power clearly articulate the practical resolution of access and validity that the bull provides.
