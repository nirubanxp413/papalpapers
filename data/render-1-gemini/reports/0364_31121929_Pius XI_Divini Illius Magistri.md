# Render: Divini Illius Magistri

## Why

A sculpture of a large, protective mother bird shielding her chicks with one expansive wing was chosen over abstraction as the single, instantly recognizable form. This figure captures the encyclical’s core metaphor of natural authority: the Church (and secondarily, the parents) as the loving and rightful educator, whose prior, supernatural claim to care for her charges ("the baptized") is absolute and precedes the State. The single maternal figure, rather than an abstract composition or multiple elements, directly embodies the "prior rights" of the family and Church against the potential encroachments and "naturalism" of external forces.
