# Render: Optime Noscitis

## Why

The chosen subject for *Optime Noscitis* is a single, closed book representing Catholic doctrinal knowledge, topped by a papal mitre. The closed book symbolizes the foundational step of establishing the university as a vessel for structured education, a mandate that had long been materials-ready but inactive. The mitre signifies Pius IX’s direct apostolic command to the Irish bishops to convene and execute this founding, rather than allowing further abstract discussion or delay. This concrete pairing of leadership and institution avoids abstract geometry by rooting the meaning in specific, recognizable symbols of authority and established doctrine.
