# Render: Quanto Conficiamur Moerore

## Why

The sculptural subject is a single, large, intricately-worked liturgical censer (or thurible) on a stand. I chose the censer because *Quanto Conficiamur Moerore* ultimately leads to a strong call for prayer, repentance, and divine intervention in the face of intense persecution and doctrinal error. While the document discusses many abstract concepts—indifference, avarice, political oppression—a censer is an immediately recognizable devotional object that physically represents the ascent of prayers and the purifying nature of penance, which are the essential responses Pius IX demands. It was selected over abstract geometry because the censer is a concrete, legible symbol deeply embedded in Catholic life, and I rejected abstraction because the rubric strictly forbids it in favor of a recognizable form.
