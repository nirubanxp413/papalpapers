# Render: Princeps Pastorum

## Why

For *Princeps Pastorum*, which focuses on the development of local clergy and hierarchies in Asia and Africa to establish a truly catholic (universal) Church, I chose **a stylized Mitre (bishop’s headdress) crafted from indigenous, natural materials** rather than abstract geometry. The Mitre is the definitive symbol of clerical authority and leadership, perfectly capturing the core theme of established native hierarchies. To express the encyclical’s specific emphasis on "inculturation"—integrating the Gospel with local traditions and materials—I rejected the traditional gold and precious gems of a European Mitre in favor of a sculpture that integrates polished native woods, woven organic fibers, and semi-precious stones typical of African and Asian craft. This choice makes the specific theological argument immediately tangible and recognizable without needing abstraction or explanation.
