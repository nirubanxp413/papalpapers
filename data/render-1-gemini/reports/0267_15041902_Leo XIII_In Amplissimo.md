# Render: In Amplissimo

## Why

*In Amplissimo* is an encyclical of praise and joy for the flourishing, organized, and faithful Catholic Church in the United States, particularly its robust institutions, Catholic education system, and unity with Rome. To capture this sense of abundant life, successful growth, and institutional strength, the single recognizable subject is a thriving, mature American White Oak tree (Quercus alba), sculpted with an intricate root system visible through the openwork base. A tree is immediately recognizable and avoids abstract symbolism; it represents vitality, longevity, a deep connection to its environment, and the fruitful growth Leo XIII celebrates in the American Church. This specific tree, native to North America, embodies the localized yet firmly rooted strength of the Church in the USA, supported by a healthy system of organized faith (its roots) that connects it directly to the life-giving soil of Catholic tradition.
