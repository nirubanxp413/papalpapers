# Render: Quanta Cura

## Why

*Quanta Cura* primarily condemns the rise of naturalism and the separation of the State from the authority of the Church, particularly concerning education and family life. A Papal Tiara, representing the supreme authority of the Bishop of Rome over both temporal and spiritual realms, functions as a direct symbol of the Church's asserted sovereignty against these secularist philosophies. This singular, universally recognized religious item immediately communicates the Pope's claim to divine and universal jurisdiction, which he seeks to protect against the errors reprobated in the encyclical. A Papal Tiara is chosen over abstract geometry because its specific form instantly evokes the theological and political context of the Church-State dynamic central to the document.
