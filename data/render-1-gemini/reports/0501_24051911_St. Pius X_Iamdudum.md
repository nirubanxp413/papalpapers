# Render: Iamdudum

## Why

I chosen the *Mitre* as the single recognizable subject for *Iamdudum*. This liturgical headdress symbolizes the spiritual authority, office, and independence of the Pope and the episcopal college, making it the ideal representation of the authority Pius X asserts against the Portuguese Republic. Since the encyclical denounces the state's severe restrictions on episcopal communications, governance, and seminary formation, and its goal to "sever the Portuguese Church from Rome," the Mitre captures the *ecclesiastical autonomy* that is being attacked and defended. We rejected abstract forms (which would fail in legibility) and complex scenes (which violate the single-subject rule) in favor of this potent and immediately identifiable symbol of papal and episcopal unity.
