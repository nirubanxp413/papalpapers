# Render: Renewal Of The Religious Life

## Why

I chose a monstrance for this sculpture because *Evangelica Testificatio* emphasizes that the renewal of religious life must be rooted in an encounter with Christ, not merely external adaptations. The monstrance is an instantly recognizable sacred object that displays the Eucharist—the "living center of consecration" and source of communion that Paul VI describes. A highly finished, traditional monstrance communicates that renewal revitalizes the essential spiritual core rather than dismantling the external forms, rejecting abstract concepts for a definitive, traditional subject.
