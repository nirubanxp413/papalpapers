# Render: Ecclesiae Fastos

## Why

I chose a single, elaborate shepherd's crook as the recognizable subject. The encyclical *Ecclesiae Fastos*, written for the 1200th anniversary of St. Boniface, uses the historical example of the missionary saint to emphasize the necessity of union with the Apostolic See (the Papacy) and the primacy of Rome. This culminates in Pope Pius XII’s direct appeal for all Christians to return to "the unity of the fold, to be fed by a single Shepherd," a clear and powerful metaphor for papal authority derived from Christ as the Good Shepherd. I rejected abstraction because a shepherd's crook is an instantly identifiable symbol of pastoral leadership and unified guidance within a specific structure, directly capturing the encyclical’s core message of centralized, Roman unity.
