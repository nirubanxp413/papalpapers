# Render: Pastoral Norms For General Sacramental Absolution

## Why

The document reaffirms individual, auricular confession as the sole ordinary path for forgiveness, while carefully defining strict exceptions for collective absolution that do not erase the future need for individual confession. A large, ornate single Key, representing the power of the keys to bind and loose (the sacrament of Reconciliation), best symbolizes this authority and its primary personal application. A key is an ancient, universally recognizable everyday object and sacred symbol of authority; an abstract form would fail to communicate this specific doctrinal mechanism, authority, and concrete priestly function of "unlocking" forgiveness.
