# Render: Religiosorum institutio

## Why

*Religiosorum institutio* centers on the careful screening and disciplined formation of candidates for religious life to ensure genuine vocations and prevent future defections. This requires testing suitability, cultivating obedience and self-sacrifice, and demanding total commitment before ordination. We selected the **thaler or assay cup** as the ideal concrete subject. A single, refined liturgical metal chalice placed within an elevated, skeletal framework—representing a crucible or forge—vividly symbolizes this process of refinement, purity testing, and the forging of a resilient character through a transformative formation. We rejected abstraction as it cannot capture the intentional human discipline and material refinement inherent in the priestly formation process.
