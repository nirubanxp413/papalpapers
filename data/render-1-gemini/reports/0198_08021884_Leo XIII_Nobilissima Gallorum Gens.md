# Render: Nobilissima Gallorum Gens

## Why

To visualize *Nobilissima Gallorum Gens*, I chose a single, monumental fleur-de-lis, the definitive historical symbol of France (the "eldest daughter"). The entire letter hinges on France’s specific identity and the threat to its foundation, making this recognizable emblem essential. Abstraction was rejected because it cannot communicate the unique national-spiritual connection Leo XIII addresses; the form must be the concrete symbol of France itself, now presented as a sacred artifact constructed from materials (dark iron, gold, and stone) that embody the historical friction between earthly authority and Divine Law.
