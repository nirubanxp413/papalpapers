# Render: Meeting with the Representatives of Science

## Why

I chose a classical Greek Doric column capital supporting a substantial, aged tome to represent Benedict XVI's argument in *Meeting with the Representatives of Science*. The fluted column capital explicitly visualizes Greek reason (Logos) as the foundational support and structure for Biblical faith and theology, embodied by the substantial book resting upon it. This unified object clearly avoids abstraction by utilizing two historically recognizable and concrete forms—the *arche* of Greek architecture and the primary symbol of sacred text and university learning. It immediately legibly translates the concept that the historical synthesis of Biblical faith and philosophical inquiry (the column supporting the book) is historically decisive and must remain indissoluble, rejecting 'dehellenization' as a weakening of this necessary intellectual structure.
