# Render: Il Fermo Proposito

## Why

I chose a single, recognizable subject—a processional cross with a strong, integrated base—to embody the meaning of *Il Fermo Proposito*. The document defines and defends "Catholic Action" as the organized, unified engagement of the laity in restoring Christ to society. However, Pius X strongly emphasizes that this activity must remain strictly subordinate to the authority of the Church hierarchy (episcopal and papal). The processional cross is the perfect symbol for this: it is a cross "in action," meant to be carried and raised above the people in movement, symbolizing Catholic engagement in the world. Crucially, the processional cross is only effective and legitimate when unified by the central shaft (symbolizing the necessary organizational structure and Church authority) and anchored by its base (submission to the hierarchy). The choice of a concrete religious object ensures immediate recognition and avoids abstract geometry, while rejecting a multi-object scene keeps the focus entirely on this core principle of unified, authorized action.
