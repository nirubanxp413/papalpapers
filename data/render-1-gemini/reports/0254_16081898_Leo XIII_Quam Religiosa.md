# Render: Quam Religiosa

## Why

The subject chosen is an ornate, jeweled marriage crown, referencing the crowns used in many Catholic wedding liturgies, particularly in Eastern Rites but suitable here to symbolize the sanctity and sacramental nature of marriage. This concrete object embodies the union that Pope Leo XIII is defending as a strictly religious and sacramental event, which the Church alone has authority to regulate. It represents the binding spiritual bond, which the encroaching civil laws and state ceremonies attempt to usurp or redefine, making a recognizable liturgical item the perfect vessel for this theological argument. An abstract form would fail to capture the specific defense of the *sacrament* of marriage and the authority the Pope claims over it.
