# Render: Paterno Iam Diu

## Why

I chose a solitary *loaf of bread* as the sculptural form. While *Paterno Iam Diu* references the Divine Infant and almsgiving, its immediate context is the devastating famine and extreme deprivation in Central Europe, specifically the lack of food for children. A loaf of bread is the universal, non-abstract symbol of fundamental nourishment and relief, directly addressing the core *social* emergency the Pope seeks to alleviate. Abstraction was rejected because it cannot communicate the urgency of hunger; only the recognizable object that *satisfies* hunger possesses the necessary legibility and moral weight to represent the encyclical's central, desperate plea for material aid.
