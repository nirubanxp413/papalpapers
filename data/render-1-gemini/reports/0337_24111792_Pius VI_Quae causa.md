# Render: Quae causa

## Why

A single, monumental Jubilee lantern was chosen to symbolize *Quae causa*, as it concretely represents the intensification of prayer, fasting, and almsgiving the Pope orders to combat crisis. The lantern, made of oxidized bronze and defensive iron straps, embodies the intersection of spiritual and temporal defense while housing the Jubilee indulgence itself—represented by the radiant flame within. This single, recognizable object avoids abstraction by offering a tangible, devotional subject that clearly communicates the Jubilee’s call to vigilance, sacrifice, and the persistent light of faith during dark times.
