# Render: Haurietis Aquas

## Why

To embody Pope Pius XII's *Haurietis Aquas*, which provides a comprehensive theological defense of devotion to the Sacred Heart, I chose the Sacred Heart of Jesus itself as the primary, recognizable symbol. The encyclical defines the Heart as the supreme symbol of Christ's threefold love: divine, spiritual human, and sensible human. A detailed, premium sculpture of the physical heart, complete with the Cross and the crown of thorns—the 'legitimate object of latria'—avoids all abstraction and makes the central argument instantly legible. The visceral, yet elevated form captures the profound intersection of the human and divine described in the document.
