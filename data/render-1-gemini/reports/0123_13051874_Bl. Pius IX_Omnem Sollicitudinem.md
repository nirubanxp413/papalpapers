# Render: Omnem Sollicitudinem

## Why

The core conflict of *Omnem Sollicitudinem* concerns the preservation of legitimate Eastern liturgical traditions against unauthorized innovation and usurpation of authority. The liturgical fan (rhipidion), a primary symbol of Eastern rite worship and episcopal authority used specifically during the Hierarchical Divine Liturgy, perfectly captures the focus on defending proper liturgical practice. A traditional Greek-style fan is a concrete, identifiable object with a clear symbolic purpose, ensuring immediate recognizability and rejecting abstract representation for direct liturgical expression.
