# Render: Grande Munus

## Why

I chose a **liturgical gospel book** as the single recognizable subject. *Grande Munus* focuses on Saints Cyril and Methodius bringing the faith to the Slavs, specifically by inventing an alphabet and translating scripture for vernacular worship. The liturgical gospel book, particularly one with a Byzantine cross (referencing their context), concretely embodies this intersection of divine revelation, the written word, liturgical approval, and the civilization they brought, which the encyclical emphasizes. This specific, functional object is a common sight in religious practice and is immediately identifiable, unlike an abstract representation of "unity" or "translation," fulfilling the requirement for a legible subject.
