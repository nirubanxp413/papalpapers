# Render: Quo fluctu

## Why

I chose a simple, unadorned **Oil Stock** (a small, portable container for holy oil) as the central sculpture. This single object perfectly encapsulates the encyclical’s core message: the provisional delegation of sacramental authority in an emergency. The oil stock represents the very substance needed to celebrate the restricted "mysteries and ceremonies"—specifically the permission for bishops to consecrate holy oil outside Holy Thursday and to delegate simple priests to bless and reconsecrate using it. By stripping away complex figures or symbols of institutional power, this utilitarian yet sacred object focuses purely on the essential, decentralized application of grace during the persecution, fulfilling the need for a recognizable, concrete, and single subject rather than abstract geometry.
