# Render: Dall’alto Dell’apostolico Seggio

## Why

A Rosary was chosen as the sculptural subject to represent *Dall’alto Dell’apostolico Seggio* because Pope Leo XIII concludes this analysis of the systematic anti-Catholic campaign by urgently calling the faithful to pray, specifically naming the Rosary under the intercession of Mary. This devotional item is instantly recognizable and concrete, serving as a material anchor for the spiritual response required by the Church. Unlike an abstract representation of ideological conflict, the Rosary symbolizes both the specific Marian devotion the encyclical promotes and the unified, disciplined prayer the Pope sees as the essential weapon for the restoration of religious and social order.
