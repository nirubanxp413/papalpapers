# Render: In gravissimis

## Why

*In gravissimis* addresses the urgent request from bishops operating in extraordinary and dangerous circumstances (post-revolutionary France) for exceptional, temporary powers. Pope Pius VI grants a detailed indult, delegating Apostolic authority for specific faculties normally reserved for the Pope or higher ordinaries—such as absolution, dispensation, and control over religious orders—all limited by time and geography. The chosen subject is a single, large **skeleton key**, forged from aged brass and dark iron. This object, instantly recognizable and concrete rather than abstract, perfectly encapsulates the concept of "delegated faculties" and "expanded powers." The key is the universal symbol of authority and access, specifically referencing the "power of the keys" (binding and loosing) being temporarily shared or extended to the bishops for the salvation of souls in a crisis.
