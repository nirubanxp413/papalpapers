# Render: Nimiam Licentiam

## Why

I chose a *Crozier*—the liturgical staff of a bishop—because *Nimiam Licentiam* is a direct papal intervention enforcing strict ecclesiastical discipline and regulatory authority in the face of procedural abuse. The crozier is the definitive symbol of the bishop's governing office and his duty to shepherd the flock, correct errors, and uphold canon law. It was chosen over abstract forms because its recognizable silhouette instantly communicates authority, governance, and spiritual order, rejecting abstraction as vague and incapable of capturing the concrete legal correctness Pope Benedict XIV is establishing.
