# Render: Licet Multa

## Why

*Licet Multa* addresses internal Catholic conflict, particularly intellectual divisiveness and public controversy. To represent the document’s unifying corrective, I have chosen a specific sacred character: Saint Thomas Aquinas. Pope Leo XIII directly spotlights Aquinas in the encyclical as the "guide and support" for reconciling intellect and faith. I chose this clear sacred figure over abstraction because an abstract form cannot represent the concrete person named by Leo XIII as the solution to Belgium’s intellectual fragmentation. Aquinas is the "one single thing" that best carries the message of unified doctrine.
