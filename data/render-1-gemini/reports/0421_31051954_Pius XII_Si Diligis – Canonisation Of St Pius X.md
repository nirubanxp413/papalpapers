# Render: Si Diligis – Canonisation Of St Pius X

## Why

I chose a shepherd’s crook or crozier as the single recognizable subject for *Si Diligis*. This object perfectly encapsulates the encyclical’s core message regarding pastoral authority, vigilance, and the hierarchy of the Magisterium. As the standard attribute of a bishop, the crozier symbolizes the divinely-instituted authority and the duty to guard the flock against "spiritual contagion," reinforcing that teaching must conform to episcopal oversight. It is immediately recognizable and inherently symbolic, avoiding abstraction while powerfully conveying the document’s focus on the shepherd’s duty of governance and teaching.
