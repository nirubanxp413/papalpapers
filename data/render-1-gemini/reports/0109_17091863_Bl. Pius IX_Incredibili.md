# Render: Incredibili

## Why

The core conflict in *Incredibili* is the New Granada government's systematic dismantling of the Church's earthly structure, forcing the clergy into exile and confiscating its property, while Pope Pius IX praises the faithful resilience of those who endure. The most potent single symbol capturing both this suffering and this enduring fidelity is the Crown of Thorns, representing the Church suffering in imitation of Christ. Choosing a human figure, like an exiled bishop, risks becoming too narrative or diorama-like; selecting a secular symbol of resistance fails to capture the essential religious nature of the conflict. The Crown of Thorns is an instantly recognizable sacred object that translates the encyclical’s "incredible grief" and Christ-like persecution into a single, concrete, and deeply resonant sculptural form.
