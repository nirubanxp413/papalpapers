# Render: Humani Generis

## Why

A shepherd's staff and mitre are chosen as the unified subject, as they directly symbolize the hierarchical authority of the Magisterium (mitre) and its protective, guiding function over the flock (croszier) which *Humani Generis* seeks to defend. The entire sculpture is constructed from a solid piece of refined gold and dark, petrified wood, representing the unchanging truth of Thomistic philosophy. This single, recognizable subject was chosen over abstract forms because it immediately communicates the central theme of spiritual authority and the protection of correct doctrine against philosophical errors, avoiding the vagueness of abstraction.
