# Render: Ad Petri Cathedram

## Why

A lighthouse is the ideal singular subject because it perfectly embodies the encyclical's core concepts of illuminating truth, guiding toward unity, and offering peaceful harbor. *Ad Petri Cathedram* diagnoses the rejection of truth as the source of social and ecclesial discord, framing the Church (and the upcoming Council) as the definitive beacon. A lighthouse rejects all abstraction by being an instantly recognizable structure of guidance, navigation, and hope, standing alone to fulfill its essential purpose.
