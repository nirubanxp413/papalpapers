# Render: Dilexit Nos

## Why

I chose a single, anatomically grounded heart, elevated with sacred materials and a crown of thorns, to directly represent the subject of *Dilexit Nos*: the human and divine heart of Jesus Christ as the integrating center of love. This specific form, a recognizable heart *rather than* an abstract geometry, anchors the encyclical's central argument that the "heart"—not a metaphorical or liquid concept, but a concrete source of personal unity and affection—is the essential response to a fragmented world. I rejected any abstract representation of "fluidity" or "integration" in favor of this singular, culturally legible, and devotionally precise figure, which functions as both the biological seat of life and the divine furnace of charity.
