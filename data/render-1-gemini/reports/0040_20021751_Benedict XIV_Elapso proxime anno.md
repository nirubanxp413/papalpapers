# Render: Elapso proxime anno

## Why

The most fitting concrete subject to capture the meaning of *Elapso proxime anno* is a single large key, specifically detailed with a dual-pronged bit, symbolizing the precise delineation of ecclesiastical jurisdiction and civil law. Benedict XIV's document functions as a legal mechanism, defining who is protected by religious asylum (granted refuge by the authority of the Church) and who is extracted (released by the keys of spiritual and temporal authority working in tandem). This key, rendered as a sacred, monumental sculpture, physically embodies the Pope's role as the arbiter of sacred law and the resolver of jurisdictional conflict, moving far beyond abstract symbolism into a recognizable tool of authority.
