# Render: Centesimus Annus

## Why

I chose a pair of interconnected scales of justice—one side resting on a solid foundation and the other tilted, suggesting a dynamic struggle—to symbolize *Centesimus Annus*'s evaluation of past (Rerum Novarum) and present (the collapse of Communism and rise of capitalism) societal models. The balance metaphorically represents the text’s central concern: weighing the demands of social justice, private property, the role of the state, and market dynamics against the baseline of human dignity and divine order. This single, universally recognizable object effectively concretizes the encyclical's multifaceted assessment of economic systems and moral responsibilities without relying on abstract or overly complex imagery.
