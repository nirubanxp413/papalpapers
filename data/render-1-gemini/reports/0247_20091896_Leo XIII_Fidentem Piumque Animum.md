# Render: Fidentem Piumque Animum

## Why

The encyclical *Fidentem Piumque Animum* champions the Rosary as the essential devotional tool for the faithful, emphasizing its power for persistent, communal prayer and its role in nurturing faith and seeking Christian unity. To capture this central meaning while maintaining perfect legibility and avoiding all abstraction, I have chosen to sculpt the primary object of the encyclical itself: a single, complete **Rosary**. This familiar devotional item embodies the entire message of persevering prayer and the meditation on Christ's life that Pope Leo XIII describes. A single, clearly defined Rosary is universally recognized, respects the "single-subject discipline," and avoids the "abstract, symbolic only through vague geometry, or dependent on explanation" forms forbidden in the prompt. I have specifically configured the Rosary with the Crucifix at the top, emphasizing the spiritual focus and providing a distinct sculptural apex, while its circular, looped form is rendered as a monumental, cohesive object for optimal legibility.
