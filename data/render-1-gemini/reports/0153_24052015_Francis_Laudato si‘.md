# Render: Laudato si‘

## Why

I chose a single, vibrant green sapling growing within a rough-hewn, cracked stone basin because it perfectly encapsulates the core argument of *Laudato si’*—"integral ecology." The sapling represents the "cry of the earth" (creation's vulnerability and the Gospel of Creation) and the urgent need for ecological conversion and biodiversity protection. The stone basin symbolizes the "cry of the poor," illustrating the inseparable link between social injustice and environmental degradation, as the earth is not a limitless resource to dominate but a fragile gift to be received and nurtured. This concrete, recognizable subject is instantly legible, avoiding abstract geometry while directly embodying the encyclical's central message.
