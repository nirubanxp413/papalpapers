# Render: Papal Directives For The Woman Of Today

## Why

I chose a single, monumental votive lantern, made of aged bronze and radiant glass, to materialize Pius XII’s directive for Catholic women to courageously enter public life (voting, politics) without sacrificing their spiritual center at home. The lantern, a universally recognizable object of guidance, captures the "supernatural faith" that must illuminate their unexpected civic role. It rejected abstraction because it clearly symbolizes "faith in action" and "spiritual illumination" while remaining a concrete, graspable object of duty and vigilance (Matthew 25).
