# Render: Menti Nostrae

## Why

I chose a monstrance for *Menti Nostrae* because the encyclical defines the sanctity of the priest as entirely dependent on his identity as an "alter Christus" (another Christ), which is supremely realized in the celebration of the Eucharist. The monstrance is the sacred vessel designed to hold and display the Blessed Sacrament, the source and summit of this sacrificial life and ministry. It perfectly symbolizes the priest’s duty to contain and present Christ to the world, making it the one single object that most clearly embodies the core spiritual and theological meaning of the entire document.
